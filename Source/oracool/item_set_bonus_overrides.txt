# Re-authored set bonus rungs.
#
# WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
#
# Forty-five of the seventy-three delivered bonus rungs compiled to nothing. Not "approximately
# nothing" - literally an empty power list, because every stat they declared named a mechanic this
# engine does not have: petition counters, blight stacks, stance machines, temper state, route
# trackers, and thirty-one distinct `proc:` effects whose name was the entire specification.
#
# The generator was right to refuse them. Inventing a number for `proc:cinderbrand` would have been
# a lie in the data. But the result was a ladder that was mostly ceremony: a player who assembled
# four pieces of the Ashen Saint earned "Cinderbrand", saw it turn green, and received nothing.
#
# Three more rungs were worse than empty, because they compiled to something:
#
#   - "Economy of Night" declared a mana discount (no channel) and light_radius:-1 (a channel). It
#     survived as -1 light radius and NOTHING ELSE - a third piece was a pure punishment.
#   - "The Mountain Remembers", the CAPSTONE of a seven-piece set, survived as +15 life.
#   - "Deep Foundation" declared armor_class_flat:+12, which on a bonus rung was worth +1 armour.
#     See ApplySetBonusesToTotals for why, and for the fix.
#
# So each rung below is re-authored out of stats this engine can actually pay out, chosen to mean
# what the rung is CALLED. "Cinderbrand" is fire damage. "Take Root" is life and vitality. "Teeth of
# the Hour" is clockwork lightning. The names were never the problem; they were the only part of the
# design that survived contact with the engine, so they are what the new stats are fitted to.
#
# FORMAT
#
#   SET_ID | pieces | keyword:value, keyword:value, ...
#
# The keywords are the SAME vocabulary as set-data.json, resolved through the same table in
# oracool/item_set_stats.cpp. That is the point: an override naming an inert or unknown keyword is a
# hard error in the generator, so this file cannot reintroduce the problem it exists to fix.
#
# An override REPLACES that rung's stats entirely. Four is the maximum, matching SetBonusDefinition.
#
# TWO RULES LEARNED FROM THE ENGINE
#
#   - life_steal / mana_steal are FLAGS at exactly 3 or 5, and they do not stack: player.cpp assigns
#     `skdam = 5 * dam / 100` over the top of the 3% case rather than adding. So a ladder carries at
#     most one steal rung, or a deliberate 3 -> 5 upgrade where the higher rung supersedes the lower.
#     Two rungs both granting 5 would be one rung's worth of effect charged twice.
#   - thorns, knockback, half_trap_damage, multiple_arrows and damage_vs_demons carry NO magnitude -
#     the flag's presence is the whole effect. Granting one twice in a ladder grants it once.
#
# Re-run tools/GenItemSets.ps1 after editing.

# --- Vestments of the Ashen Saint (6 pieces) - fire, ash, reliquary -------------------------------
# Only the four-piece was empty; the rest of this ladder survived intact.
SET_ASHEN_SAINT           | 4  | fire_damage:[4,12], chance_to_hit:+10

# --- Graveglass Regalia (5 pieces) - the dead, glass, spite ---------------------------------------
SET_GRAVEGLASS            | 3  | vitality:+8, resist_magic:+15
# "Returning Malice" declared spell_reflect, which has no hook. Thorns is the engine's own version of
# the same idea - harm returned to whoever dealt it - so the rung keeps its meaning.
SET_GRAVEGLASS            | 4  | resist_all:+10, thorns:true

# --- Stormcrow Harness (6 pieces) - bows, lightning, crows ----------------------------------------
# This is a BOW set, and the engine's arrow powers were sitting unused in the enum the whole time.
SET_STORMCROW             | 3  | resist_lightning:+15, attack_speed:quick
SET_STORMCROW             | 4  | chance_to_hit:+15, dexterity:+8
SET_STORMCROW             | 5  | lightning_arrows:[2,20], resist_lightning:+10
# The capstone was live but thin. A murder of crows descending is more than one arrow.
SET_STORMCROW             | 6  | enhanced_bow_damage:+25%, bow_attack_speed:fast, multiple_arrows:true

# --- Panoply of the Iron Root (7 pieces) - earth, bark, immovability ------------------------------
# Four of six rungs were empty. This ladder is now the fork's defensive wall, which is what every one
# of its names is about.
SET_IRON_ROOT             | 3  | damage_taken_flat:-2, armor_class_flat:+15
SET_IRON_ROOT             | 4  | vitality:+12, max_life:+25
SET_IRON_ROOT             | 5  | armor_class_flat:+25, resist_all:+10
SET_IRON_ROOT             | 6  | damage_taken_flat:-3, resist_magic:+15, block_speed:true
# Was +15 life, as the reward for seven pieces.
SET_IRON_ROOT             | 7  | max_life:+60, all_attributes:+10, damage_taken_flat:-3, thorns:true

# --- Raiment of the Starless Hour (5 pieces) - darkness, mana, sight ------------------------------
# The light_radius penalty is KEPT: seeing less is the set's whole conceit. It is now paid for.
SET_STARLESS_HOUR         | 3  | magic:+10, max_mana:+30, light_radius:-1
SET_STARLESS_HOUR         | 4  | all_spell_levels:+1, magic:+12
SET_STARLESS_HOUR         | 5  | all_spell_levels:+1, max_mana:+50, resist_all:+15, mana_steal:5

# --- The Rat King's Tithe (4 pieces) - greed, scavenging ------------------------------------------
# The set's identity is gold, and gold_from_monsters has no IPL_ to ride (Player::_pGoldFind exists

# is noted in the continuation plan; until then this ladder is about taking, not about coin.

SET_RAT_KINGS_TITHE       | 3  | attack_speed:quick, dexterity:+8

# --- The Crimson Compact (5 pieces) - blood, pacts, debt ------------------------------------------
