# 1.8.25 - Two greys and a book that moved the wrong way

Both of 1.8.24's fixes were wrong in the game, in ways the build could not see. Plus one open
question on a user-reported bug I have not solved.

## 1. The panel came out white

`PAL16_GRAY + 12` for the panel and `+ 15` for the cells rendered as light grey and near-white. The
reasoning behind those numbers was that a higher offset into a 16-shade ramp is a darker shade -
which is what the palette header's own example says (*"(dark blue): PAL16_BLUE+14"*), and which is
true for every ramp except the one I picked. The top of the palette is the UI's white end.

The fix is to stop doing arithmetic on a ramp whose direction I am inferring, and to name indices
that are already proven in this exact context. `DrawOrnateBorder` samples four, with measured RGB in
its comments:

| Index | RGB | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| 204 | (57, 49, 29) | the panel ground - dark stone |
| 223 | (15, 5, 0) | the grid cells - near black |

Both are in the shared upper half (128-255), identical across town and all four tilesets, so the
fill still cannot recolour itself by level.

**The lesson is narrower than "check your colours":** a named constant plus arithmetic looks like
derivation but is really a guess when the ramp's direction is assumed rather than read. Prefer an
index someone has already looked at on screen.

## 2. The book's fallback was worse than the bug

1.8.24 made the recipe book flip to the **right** of the window when it did not fit on the left.
The right is where the inventory and the mini-map live, so the book landed on top of the panel the
player had open - a bigger problem than the clipping it replaced.

It now places left and **clamps at x = 0**. Sliding left until it touches the screen edge costs at
most a few pixels of overlap with Levski's own window, and the book draws after it, so the book
stays readable. A fallback that moves a window somewhere occupied is not a fallback.

## 3. Open: Ctrl+Click gold to stash

> "i could not CTRL+Click these 5 gold stacks in my inv grid into my stash."

**Not fixed, and I will not guess at it.** Reading the path end to end - `CheckInvItem`'s
`IsStashOpen && isCtrlHeld` branch, `TransferItemToStash`, `AutoPlaceItemInStash`'s gold branch,
`CheckInvHLight`'s tab-0 `pcursinvitem` encoding - every step looks correct for gold in tab 1.

A reproduction test is parked in `test/inv_test.cpp` as
`DISABLED_CtrlClickTransfersGoldStackFromBackpackToStash`. It faults with an access violation
**inside `AutoPlaceItemInStash` before the first assertion**. That is either the reported bug or
missing test-environment init, and I have not separated the two, so it is disabled rather than
reported as a finding.

**The one datapoint that splits it:** does Ctrl+Click move a *non-gold* item from the backpack to
the stash? If it also fails, the fault is in click routing and gold is a red herring. If only gold
fails, it is in the transfer, and the disabled test is pointing at the real thing.

## Also found while reading, not fixed

`AddGoldToInventory` (`Source/inv.cpp`) still walks a **10x4** grid - `for (int i = 39; i >= 30; ...)`
and `for (int y = 2; y >= 0; ...)`. The backpack is 10x7. Gold can therefore never be auto-placed
below the fourth row. This is the same shape of bug as the stash's "last 6 rows" one fixed on
2026-08-16: a literal that did not grow when its grid did. Parked for the Pipeline rather than
folded into a screenshot-response build.
