# 1.8.31 - A title that measures itself

> "Reduce the font of the title to fit the name."

Levski's Roar rendered as **"LEVSKI'S"**. At `FontSize24` the name is wider than a window sized to
three 28px cells plus padding, and `DrawString` clipped it without complaint.

The title now picks the largest size the whole name fits in - `FontSize24` when
`GetLineWidth(title, GameFont24) <= ContentWidth`, `FontSize12` otherwise - and is drawn against
`ContentWidth` rather than `GridWidth`, which is what it was measured into before (the grid is
narrower than the window's buttons since the 3x4 rebuild, so the title had even less room than it
looked).

**Measured, not chosen.** Hardcoding `FontSize12` would have fixed today's string and broken silently
on the next one. This way a longer name drops a size on its own and a wider window lets the name grow
back - the same reason the recipe book now takes the width it can have rather than assuming 420.

Silent clipping is the theme worth noting: three defects in this window (the book off the left edge,
the book over the inventory, this title) were all "text drawn somewhere it does not fit", and none of
them raised anything. Only a screenshot ever showed them.
