# 1.8.33 - Torment stops being Hell with bigger numbers

Third of the Small batch, and the Pipeline entry was out of date before I started it.

> "Per-difficulty immunities - the resistance data exists; make it difficulty-aware so Hell and
> Torment demand real resistance gear."

**Difficulty-awareness already shipped.** `MonsterResistancesFor` has handled Normal, Nightmare and
Hell for some time: Normal uses `resistance`, Nightmare unions it with Hell's set demoted to plain
resistances, Hell uses `resistanceHell` outright.

The actual gap was the second half of the sentence: `DIFF_TORMENT` fell through to the same `return
data.resistanceHell` as `DIFF_HELL`. **Byte-identical.** The fourth difficulty asked nothing the
third had not already asked, so there was no reason to build a second resistance set for it.

## What Torment does now

Hell's resistances harden into immunities - the same step Nightmare-to-Hell takes, taken once more.
`PromoteResistancesToImmunities` is the mirror of the existing `DemoteImmunitiesToResistances`, and
the ladder is now symmetric: demote for Nightmare, use as authored for Hell, promote for Torment.

**With one rule it cannot do without: no monster may come out immune to all three schools.** That is
not a harder fight, it is an impossible one for the caster classes - and the physical classes would
never notice the difficulty existed, which is the worse half. So promotion runs in school order and
stops before the last one standing: whatever the monster was weakest to on Hell stays merely
resisted on Torment.

That constraint turned out to be the more interesting design, not a safety valve. Every monster on
Torment keeps exactly one answer, and finding it is the game.

## Tests

- `TormentHardensHellResistancesIntoImmunities` - the concrete case, and that Hell itself did not move.
- `TormentNeverLeavesAMonsterImmuneToEverything` - **all 64** authorable combinations of the six
  school bits, not a sample. Asserts no combination promotes into total immunity unless it was
  authored that way, and that Torment is never softer than Hell.

One existing assertion had to change: `NightmareDemotesHellImmunitiesToResistances` asserted
`Torment == resistanceHell`. It now asserts the opposite. That is the change being made, not a
regression - and it is exactly the kind of edit worth naming out loud, because silently rewriting an
assertion is how a real regression gets waved through.
