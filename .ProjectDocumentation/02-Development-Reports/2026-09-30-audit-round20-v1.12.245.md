# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 20 (v1.12.245)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 20 ran these read-only tracks:
- a regression review of v1.12.244;
- hero movement and landing;
- skill hover text against what each skill does, for the Sorcerer, Rogue, Monk and Necromancer (one track per class);
- bounds and index safety;
- level state across the stairs;
- keyboard actions while a window is open.

Every finding was verified against the code first.

## Fixed: regressions of round 19

- **Smite always connects now in play.** v1.12.244 asked which animation the swing wears, which is false wherever the
  shield sheet is loaded, so the rule never fired outside the tests. It asks the Smite latch now.
- **The left well** shows a Paladin melee skill red under a bow, as the right well and the picker do.
- **A held Weapon Throw** says its refusal once.
- **The class line** on an item card sits with the requirements.
- **The stun flag** is cleared per level and per monster slot.

## Fixed: movement

- **P1: a chill made a fast running hero faster.** The slowed run added four frames to the slowed walk with no ceiling.
  From about +60% speed a chilled hero outran an unchilled one; from about +90% he crossed a path in a single tick.
  The slowed run is now never faster than the same hero's unslowed run, and the sheet reads it the same way.
- **A Leap taken under a held walk button** no longer snaps back to the take-off point. The hero stops walking before
  the landing, and a mid-step leap takes its light with it.
- **Leaps and other teleport moves** swap with the hero's own companion or minion on the landing tile, as a walk does.
- **A Charge at a monster the hero cannot reach** ends its dash. The dash had stayed armed for 2 seconds, so the next
  melee click sprinted and landed the arriving blow without paying.
- **Turning Pike** will not land beside a barrel or another object.
- **The run button's hover** reads the step as the feet take it.

## Fixed: skills that did not do what they said

- **Multiple Shot, Guided Arrow and Strafe** added the damage bonuses twice. They now fly with the bare dice, and the
  hit adds the bonuses once, as for a plain arrow.
- **The eight RfA-12 bow skills count as arrows** for Night Stalker, Thrill of the Hunt, Archery, Leech and the marks.
- **Fend, Whirlwind, Wheel of Heaven and Sweeping Reed** strike with a full blow (+% damage, flat damage, Strength).
  They had rolled the bare weapon dice.
- **Temple Bell** strikes undead as holy light, through magic immunity. Most undead are immune to magic, so its damage
  had reached almost none of the monsters it names.
- **Glass Cannon's +15%** reaches spells and skills. It had been weapon +% damage only, so a Sorceress paid the armour
  for nothing.
- **Chilling Armor** chills a ranged attacker for its own duration, as its facts say.
- **Life Tap** heals from the Necromancer's own skills (Teeth, Bone Spear and every other tree skill).
- **Dim Vision** loses a monster already in the fight. It had only stopped a monster from waking. Uniques, Diablo
  and the Dread bosses are exempt, as from Terror and Confuse: a blinded boss would never fight back, and a blinded
  Diablo's death skipped its sweep of the level.
- **Weaken and Decrepify** blunt a cursed monster's blows on the army too.
- **Freezing Arrow** spares the Rogue's Valkyrie, Decoy and converted allies.
- **Hover text corrected:**
  - Fire and Lightning Mastery (weapon blows, not spells);
  - Blaze (a wall of fire rolling forward);
  - Firestorm (aimed fireballs that burst on the first thing in their path);
  - Lightning Strike (chain lightning from the hero);
  - Seven Reeds and Hundred Fists (only the extra blows take the share; no "up to seven");
  - Flowing Step;
  - Inner Sight and Spirit Ward (raised by books);
  - Tranquility (2 tiles);
  - Teeth (one more tooth a rank, up to six);
  - Iron Maiden (melee blows);
  - Dark Reaping (weapon blows and arrows).

## Fixed: keyboard and windows

- **The Chat Log key** obeys the window rules: not while dead, paused or in a store; it closes every window, the
  fork's included; and the log and the help text are modal, so a click closes them and reaches nothing behind.
- **The runeword book closes** when the inventory, the sheet, the quest log, the spell book or the Abilities window
  opens. Each had opened hidden under it.
- **Escape on the gold-split prompt** closes the prompt alone, not the stash and the backpack with it.
- **Typing on the chat line** no longer readies skills with the F-keys or fires other actions.
- **Space on the death menu** no longer dismisses it.
- **A and S** do not open the skill lists over a store dialog.

## Fixed: level state and saves

- **The rift guardian leaves his own body.** He inherited the slot's last unique's corpse entry, which could turn a
  dead champion's body into his, or leave him none.
- **Floor items** loaded from a level no longer keep a stale "just landed" mark.
- **Damaged save records:**
  - a book of a spell past the spell-level table is dropped, and the spell-level message is bounded;
  - an item cursor id past the sprite tables is dropped;
  - the inventory appends never write past the list.

## Not changed (open)

- **Movement speed calibration:** the sheet's percentages assume a 10-tick walk, and the engine's chained strides are
  2 ticks shorter. +100% really moves at about 267%. Retuning changes the feel - the user's call.
- **Leap Attack and Vaulting Strike** charge the full cost for a leap that strikes nothing; the blow needs a second
  click. This is how the signed-off Barbarian leap works - the user's call.
- **Bone Spirit** takes no Marrow, Serration or Rigor Mortis; **Bone Prison's** hold spares uniques; **Iron Golem and
  Aberrant Animator** answer melee blows only; **Virulence** skips Bane. Each is a text or a code choice.
- **Mark bursts on any death, and Conversion kills' XP** carry on from round 19.
- **Escape over the skill picker, the HUD menu or the Monument menu** also closes the side panels behind them.
- **The game-speed keys** still act while typing on the chat line.
- **Another melee skill clicked mid-Charge** plays Charge's burst and cue (not its damage). Older than this round.
- **Smaller text slips** from the four class reports are recorded in the reports, not changed here. These include:
  - Power Strike's range;
  - Immolation Arrow's "wall";
  - the Inner Sight and Slow Missiles durations;
  - the Critical Strike line;
  - Clay Golem's "slow";
  - Bone Splinters' "spread";
  - the curse "paid in Essence" lines;
  - the Monk's unstated unique exemptions.
