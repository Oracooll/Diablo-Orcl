# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 73 (v1.12.321)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit 89da292a.

Three read-only auditors ran:
- a regression review of v1.12.320;
- quests, level generation, town, waypoints and portals (7th pass);
- the Necromancer (13th).

## Necromancer
- **Gather the Dead and the companions:** they are never set down on the far side of a wall. `PlaceCompanionNear` searched out to radius 4 with no sight check, so in a corridor beside a room, skeletons landed in the room. This is the gap v1.12.318 closed in `SpawnBody`.
- **A golem recast:** it now refuses, before the old golem is dismissed, when the new one would have nowhere to stand. The dying golem keeps its square until its last frame, so with the room around full, the old golem went and nothing replaced it.
  - New helpers: `MinionTileNear` (SpawnBody's search) and `MinionBodyFitsAt`.
- **Formation rings:** they renumber after a level change. A body that died on the stairs left a gap in the order, and two minions shared one home tile.

## Regression review of v1.12.320
- **`Lay` re-aims after the new curse stands.** Before, the re-aim ran between the release and the new curse:
  - a Confuse laid again aimed the monster at the hero;
  - an Attract laid again sent its neighbours off.
  
  A fresh Confuse or Attract now re-aims too.
- **A dropped item put back in the pack** recalculates the hero, since charms count from the pack.
- **A `CMD_PUTITEM` handled off-level** drops its queued copy.

## Quests, level generation, town, waypoints and portals
Nothing new. The quest gates in rifts, the waypoint gates, the town object positions, the per-tick town logic and the rift roster stability were all traced and hold.

## Left open
- **Item drop with nowhere to go:** a dropped item can still be lost. That needs a full pack, a stash that refuses it, a hand refilled within the same tick, and no free tile.
- **Extra blows:** the RfA-12 and class-skill extra blows each count as a swing for Cadence, Counterstroke and the combo counters.
- **Guardian Rift keystone:** whether it is refunded on exiting an unfinished rift is the user's call.
