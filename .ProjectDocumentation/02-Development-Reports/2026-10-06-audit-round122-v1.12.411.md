## The user's rulings (2026-10-06)
- **Might and Berserk losing their bonus on monsters with overflow damage:** "FIX!"
  - Might: `PackAdjustedDamage` now returns an int, unclamped. Its callers already work in int and lay the overflow percent on after.
  - Berserk: it scales the monster's real damage, overflow included, through `SetMonsterDamage`. Same draws, same order.
- **The Paladin's weapon casts** (Blessed Hammer and the rest) take no item on-hit powers: "they don't need to - they are always-hitting spells." Closed.

## Fixed: regressions in v1.12.410
1. **A revived corpse came back too weak (P2).** v1.12.410 set a minion body's overflow to 100, but a corpse recorded only its damage bytes, so a revived Torment or overflowed monster hit at 1/overflow. Now:
   - the corpse records its real damage (`MonsterEffectiveDamage`);
   - the body is built from it with `SetMonsterDamage`, its special keeping its type's real values;
   - the forced 100 in `AddMinionBody` is removed.
2. **Aegis Slam with a Knockback item:** the plain hit pushed the target off the tile the slam then looked on, so it escaped the stun. `PlrHitMonst` takes no item knockback while the armed swing moves its own target (`Rfa12ArmedSwingMovesItsTarget`).
3. **Diablo's Apocalypse** carries his damage percent: the rift faces' 150% and Torment's overflow.

## Fixed: draw and UI crash safety
4. **A stale `oracool.mpq` (P2):** the item icon sheet was never matched against the icon table. A build with new icons and no repack read past the sheet at start-up. A mismatch now stops with a clear message: "oracool.mpq is out of date ... Repack it with tools\build_oracool_mpq.cmd". The test suite loads the current archive's icons, so the shipped archive matches.
5. **`DrawObject`,** the one draw path with no sprite guard, now returns with no sheet or past its frames.
6. **An object whose art was not found** clears its sheet, so a reused slot no longer keeps the last level's freed one.
