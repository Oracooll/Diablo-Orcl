# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 112 (v1.12.398)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 950 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks:**
- a regression review of v1.12.397;
- the Paladin's and the Barbarian's skill effects against their text.

## Fixed: regressions in v1.12.397
1. **"Fastest wins" moved into the frame skipper.** Clearing Quick and Fast from the flags (rounds 110-111) had to skip bows, whose flags add arrow speed. A javelin throw is a staff-look swing that works with a bow in hand, so it still got the slower Fastest+Fast count. The flags are no longer touched. `StartAttack` and the character sheet both read Fastest first, with Quick or Fast too, so the bow keeps its arrow speed.
2. **A rebuilt cleared rift floor** could lay the reward again while it sat in a Cube or workshop window that refused to close (pack and stash full). The re-lay now searches both by seed.
3. **The refusal message** now reads "Put the keystone your last rift left you in your pack first." The old one said "pick up", which was wrong for the Cube and the bench.
4. **Dead Ground and Deadeye skip bursts.** `Rfa12DamageDealtPercent` takes a burst flag. The javelin pulses, Shock Arrow's arc and side blows no longer roll Deadeye or take and spend Dead Ground. Round 111 claimed this but did not do it.
5. **Poison Javelin strikes the first enemy on the line, as its text says.** It is refused unpaid when that enemy is acid-immune. Round 111 passed over it, which read as piercing.
6. **Dev report corrections.** Rounds 110 (cold) and 111 (curses) had lesser-unique "fixes" that were defensive only: every lesser unique already counts as unique. Both reports now say so.

## Fixed: Paladin and Barbarian
7. **Seismic Slam and Earthquake (P1):** the Barbarian's two cast weapon blows were missed by rounds 110-111.
   - **With a bow:** they cast and rolled the bow's damage. Both are now refused.
   - **Counted as spells:** they skipped Bloodlust, Deep Wounds, Lasting Wounds and Retaliation. They now strike as melee blows: Seismic Slam counts once a cast, and each Earthquake pulse is a burst.
   - **Throwing Mastery** no longer boosts them, since they are not hurled.
8. **Conversion** turned the first eligible monster in scan order (top-left), not the one clicked. It now takes the eligible one nearest the cursor.

## Reported, not coded
- **Hammer of Faith's splash** is a share of the front blow's final damage, applied bare. Bane of Evil and the class modifiers are judged on the front target, and the splashed monsters get no on-hit effects. A fix means striking each neighbour as its own side blow, with `SideBlow` exported.
- **Blessed Hammer's spiral** hits through walls and doors. Diablo II's does too: a design call.
- **War Cry's damage** takes none of the damage passives that Earthshaker Cry takes.
- **War Cry and Earthshaker Cry** are paid when the only monsters in earshot shrug them off.
- **Iron Will, Bloodcall and Rallying Cry** are paid again on a recast at full duration.
- **Text mismatches:**
   - Towering Shield's "25% harder" is points for Smite but a multiplier for Blessed Shield.
   - Wrath of the Heavens' row says "over 3 seconds" where the hover says 6.
   - Leap Attack's row says "onto a distant enemy", but it leaps onto empty ground.
   - Earthquake is paid in an empty room.
