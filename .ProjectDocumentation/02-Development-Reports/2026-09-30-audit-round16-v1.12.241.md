# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 16 (v1.12.241)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 16 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.240;
2. the Paladin and the Barbarian after the round 13-15 changes to `Strike`;
3. item tooltips against effects (third pass);
4. crafting (third pass);
5. the vanilla spells (third pass);
6. the town and vendor dialogs as a state machine.

Every finding was verified against the code first.

## Fixed: regressions of round 15

- **Final Service in town.** Round 15 offered the death saves before `SyncPlrKill`, including in town, where no one can
  die. Final Service then dismissed the whole army there, and the once-a-minute saves spent their cooldown for
  nothing. Town is skipped now.
- **Dead Ground paid on every skill hit.** Round 15 added its bonus to `Strike` but never started its cooldown. The
  bonus now starts the six-second, per-enemy cooldown, as its text says.
- **Sale proceeds still went into an unreadable stash.** Round 15 locked that stash but missed the sale path, so the
  gold was lost at exit. Sales now pay into the backpack when the stash file could not be read.
- **Making room for a guaranteed drop** now takes the cheapest ordinary item. It never takes gold or a quest item: the
  Anvil, the Magic Rock and the Blood Stones had passed its old test.

## Fixed: the Paladin and the Barbarian

- **Endurance and Battle Orders were a free heal loop.** Turning a life bonus off kept the hero's current life, but
  turning it back on added the bonus again. It is symmetric now: the bonus comes off with its life, and the hero is
  floored at 1 life, as round 7 wanted.
- **A hit during Charge's dash no longer strips the charge.** The blow was paid for, but after a stagger it landed as a
  plain swing, with the mana spent and the cooldown running.
- **Holy Lance** strikes each monster once, not the front target again. A walker stood on two lance tiles and took
  two blows.
- **Aegis Slam** shoves, then stuns. The shove's knockback had cancelled the stun.
- **Converted monsters** are no longer struck by the Paladin's own area skills, and Sanctuary no longer sends them
  running.
- **The holy auras** take the RfA-12 damage-dealt half, so a Judgment mark reaches them.

The track found no damage counted twice after the `Strike` changes.

## Fixed: tooltips

- **Attack speed on a bow** now reads "quick/fast/faster/fastest arrows". The speed flags quicken the arrow, not the
  draw, so the old "faster attack" line promised frames the bow never skips.
- **Thunderclap** says what it does in single player: a 3-6 fire hit, not charged bolts.
- **The Charm of Luck** uses the affix's own words: "chance a plain weapon or armor found is Rare".

## Fixed: crafting and spells

- **Ennoble and Reroll Uniques** no longer spend the crafted unique's one drop. Every press had struck one more
  unique from the drop pool for the rest of the game.
- **A cast that does nothing is not paid for.** This covers Phasing with no landing, Stone Curse on a monster already
  stone, and Town Portal with no free tile.
- **Stone Curse** no longer picks a monster that is already stone.
- **Chain Lightning** no longer aims bolts at the hero's own army.
- **Charged Bolt's quoted maximum** matches its roll.

## Fixed: town and vendors

- **A towner no longer opens a counter under a bench or the Cube that refused to close.** The hidden shop had taken
  the clicks meant for the bench. The Crafting book and the runeword book back out the same way.
- **The character sheet and the quest log** close the workshop and the Cube, instead of opening invisible under them.

## Not changed (open)

- **Zod then Free the Sockets is a free full repair**, ethereal items included. The proper fix stores the pre-Zod
  durability on the item, which needs an item format bump. **The user's call.**
- **Salvage materials can be minted** by looping Break Down, Refine, Enrich, Ennoble and salvage. A balance call.
- **The Cube's auto-pick** runs Reforge on a unique when one reagent stack satisfies several recipes.
- **Barbarian book spells** check and spend a mana pool he cannot see. **The user's call:** refuse books for Rage
  users, price them in Rage, or show the mana cost.
- **Gamepad in a grid shop:** A buys the selected shelf item wherever the pointer is. Gamepad questions are parked.
- **Blitzen and Flambeau** pick their missile from the summed lightning minimum, so any other lightning source changes
  what they fire.
- **Retaliation's stacks** are read by side blows without being used up.
- **Several damage quotes** differ from what lands: Fireball's double hit, Flame Wave, Rune of Light, the two walls,
  and the level-0 staff quote. Deciding per hit or per cast is a design call.
- **Minor:**

  - Unidentified items hide their sockets.
  - Light radius is shown in two units.
  - The Consecrate slot check.
  - Ctrl-click into a hidden grid.
  - Towner text rows act on the press.
  - The Rift Monument menu on a gamepad.
