# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 83 (v1.12.331)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit 2bda5725.

Three read-only auditors ran:
- a regression review of v1.12.330;
- the Barbarian's warcries, Rage, Leap and Whirlwind (10th pass);
- monster population, scaling, XP and loot numbers (8th pass).

## Barbarian
- **Leap landing:** a Leap or Leap Attack whose landing is taken by a monster stepping onto it no longer lands past a wall.
  - `ProcessTeleport`'s fallback search now accepts only tiles in sight of the tile aimed at.
  - Before, in a one-wide corridor it went round into the next room, up to 5 tiles off target and past the leap's reach.
  - Teleport's own aim may still be past a wall. Its fallback stays on that side.
- **Warcry tooltips:** Shout, Battle Orders and Battle Command now show Inspiring Presence's doubled time. "40 s" was showing for 80 s.
- **Doc comment:** `rage.h` now says Rage carries across levels, which it has done since 2026-09-16.
- **Checked and clean:** recast refreshes, buff expiry, the cries' sight gates, the Whirlwind economy, Rage gain and decay, Leap Attack's single payment, and the Ancients.

## Companions
These follow up the v1.12.330 regression review. Its own changes checked clean.
- **Summoning at the cursor:** a companion summoned at a cursor that is solid or out of the hero's sight now stands beside him.

## Numbers
- **Town-given quest uniques on Normal:** these now carry their quest floor's item level (Griswold's Edge, the Harlequin Crest and the rest). They had been stamped with town's level 1.
- **Unique-monster lookup:** the unique row is indexed by `size_t` (it was `int8`). This is latent: it would go negative past 127 rows.
- **Checked and clean:**
  - the area ladder (Nest is floor-8 and Crypt is floor-8);
  - the uint8 damage fields at every difficulty;
  - HP at Torment;
  - pack placement headroom;
  - variants never stacking on lesser uniques;
  - XP bounds;
  - drop item levels;
  - gold.

## For the user
- **Chest and barrel loot depth:** chests, barrels, racks and theme rooms still draw base items from twice the floor's level. That doubling is vanilla's. Monsters on the same floor draw from the floor's level plus 0-3, via `ItemLevelOfMonster`.
  - Example: a Nightmare floor-8 chest can roll any base in the game, stamped item level 24.
  - Affixes and uniques are still capped by the item level. Only the base pool and the magic chance are inflated.
  - Changing it is a loot-balance call, so it is left as it is.
