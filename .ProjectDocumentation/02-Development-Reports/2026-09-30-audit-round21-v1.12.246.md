# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 21 (v1.12.246)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 21 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.245;
2. the durability lifecycle and broken items;
3. the hero's resources: mana, Rage, Essence, life and potions;
4. compliance with the user's standing design rules;
5. unique and set item powers against their tooltips;
6. the scripted boss fights against the fork's systems.

Every finding was verified against the code first.

## Fixed: regressions of round 20

- **The damaged-book guard** checks the spell id's range before reading the spell table. v1.12.245 read the table first,
  so a far-out id read past it inside the guard meant to stop that.
- **Game speed (F9, F10)** does not change while typing on the chat line.
- **Opening the chat log** keeps the automap and the event log as they were.

## Fixed

- **Charge no longer charges again for every click during its dash.** Each click had paid 10 mana and restarted the
  dash, because the cooldown starts only at the arriving swing.
- **The Barbarian gains no Rage while dead.** Unforgiving's calm clock ran on a corpse, and the Rage carried through the
  respawn.
- **A potion's remaining healing** does not follow the hero into town after a respawn.
- **A rift Na-Krul's speech** stops when he falls.
- **A rift Leoric's raised skeletons** fight at the rift's scale.
- **Flambeau and Blitzen** choose their spectral missile from the bow's own values. Any other lightning source had turned
  Flambeau's fireballs into bolts or plain arrows. Two arrow flags from two sources (a Stormcrow rung and a fire bow) no
  longer make spectral arrows.
- **Rerolling away "of the ages"** leaves the item whole but wearing again, not indestructible for no reason. Zod's stamp
  and an Oil of Permanence still survive a rebuild.
- **Oil of Permanence** is refused on an ethereal item, as the other durability oils are. It had repaired a worn-down
  ethereal to whole and indestructible.
- **The Cornerstone of the World** no longer brings back an item stored in `diablo.ini`. Its save had lost its only
  caller, so a placed item vanished at the game's end while an old build's stored item came back in every new game.

## Not changed (open)

- **Zod + Free the Sockets repairs any item for free**, ethereals included, and repeatably. This is already on the open
  list, because the pre-Zod wear needs a new stored field (an item format bump).
- **Buttons that act on the press, not the release**, are the parked press/release pass: the Abilities window, the
  inventory Sort, the skill wells, the HUD menu, the red X buttons, the skill picker and the crafting host buttons.
- **The HUD's durability warning** covers 4 of the 10 slots that wear; the other six have no icon art.
- **The Barbarian's hidden mana pool:** +Magic and +Mana gear give him mana the HUD never shows. Mana potions drink into
  it, and a book spell can be cast from it.
- **Leap Attack and Vaulting Strike** charge the full price for a leap that strikes nothing, as in round 20.
- **Whirlwind's per-second drain** goes around the spend hooks (Bloodthirst sees only its start cost).
- **Sacrifice's life price** is paid as damage, so Spirit Ward and damage reduction shrink it.
- **A crafted power merged into a Rare's same-kind affix** can reach about twice the item-level ceiling in one line. It
  depends on how the user reads the ceiling rule.
- **Set and unique text:** attack-speed lines promise frames a bow never gets in Hellfire mode. Tiers that do not stack
  are printed as if they do, and a Fastest weapon with a "quick attack" set line gets slower. "One handed sword" appears
  on three one-handed set weapons. Stormcrow's lightning arrows also strike in melee.
- **Broken weapons** still drive the class cleave, Decay and Heavenly Strength's penalty, and a broken bow still reads
  as a bow on the sheet.
- **Shrine and oil repairs** leave the broken flag set on backpack items until the next recalculation.
