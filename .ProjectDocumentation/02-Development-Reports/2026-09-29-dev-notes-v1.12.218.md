## 1. The Advanced Stats window's X

Note: "put the common X close button on its designated spot on the advanced stats window".

**Cause:** `scrollrt.cpp` drew the Advanced Stats X only in an `else if` after the inventory's and the Abilities window's. Since 2026-09-27 those windows share the screen with it (opening it no longer closes them), so beside either one it had no X.

**Fix:**
- **Draw:** now an independent `if`. It uses the common `DrawWindowCloseButton` at `GetWindowCloseButtonRect`, the same corner as every other window.
- **Click:** in `diablo.cpp` it is tested ahead of the inventory's and the Abilities window's, since it is drawn over them where they overlap.
- **Preview:** `OracoolPreview.DISABLED_HeroSheet` now draws both windows' X.

## 2. The holy auras' calibration

Note: rank 1 of an upper-tier aura should deal what the lower-tier aura deals at the rank it would have had, with a point every level until the upper one unlocks.

Holy Fire, Holy Freeze and Holy Shock unlock at levels 6, 18 and 24, at one point per level (`SkillPointsPerLevel`). `HolyAuraCarriedRanks(lower, upper)` counts the unlock levels inclusive, times the points per level, capped at the lower aura's rank cap:
- Holy Fire to Holy Freeze: 13.
- Holy Freeze to Holy Shock: 7.

| Aura | Was, rank 1 / rank 30 | Now, rank 1 / rank 30 |
|---|---|---|
| Holy Fire | 4-8 / 91-182 | unchanged |
| Holy Freeze | 3-6 / 61-151 | 40-80 (= Holy Fire rank 13) / 127-254 |
| Holy Shock | 1-14 / 30-246 | 1-173 (the average of Holy Freeze rank 7, 58-116) / 30-405 |

- **Growth:** each grows at Holy Fire's rate (+3-6 a rank).
- **Holy Shock:** keeps lightning's wide spread, 1 at the bottom and +1 a rank, with the top set so the average matches.
- **Holy Freeze:** loses its small discount for the chill; the note asks for as much damage.

**Test:** the holy aura test now pins:
- the carried ranks, 13 and 7;
- Holy Freeze rank 1 = Holy Fire rank 13;
- Holy Shock rank 1's average = Holy Freeze rank 7's;
- each tier's average at least the one below at every rank from 1 to 30.
