# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 107 (v1.12.391)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 949 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks (two):**
- a regression review of v1.12.390 (the reward keystone debt, the cost fixes);
- the vendors and the artisans (gold, stock, services, the workshop, Ogden's boards, the Cube).

## Fixed: the reward keystone
1. **The town settle was dead code.** It sat after `ProcessRift`'s "no rift open" return, and a cleared rift walked out of is ended by the town's rebuild. A reward carried out on the cursor and put away in town stayed owed until the exit save. It now runs first, only while a reward is owed, once a second.
2. **The periodic autosave did not settle.** It does now, before `SaveHeroAndStash`. A settle also schedules a save of its own, so the cleared debt reaches the disk soon.
3. **A reward held on the cursor rode into the next rift.** Its debt carried into the new rift and, put away later, wiped the debt of the keystone that rift spent. Opening a Guardian Rift now refuses with "Put away the keystone you hold first."

## Fixed: cost
4. **The guardian spot's path budget counted cheap sight failures.** A tile out of sight spent one of the 32 path tries without tracing a path. Sight is now checked before the try is counted.

## Fixed: vendors and artisans (both P3)
5. **Wirt's gamble kept an old price when a slot restocked.** The roll is made at the hero's current level, but the restocked slot kept the price it was first stocked at. Levels gained inside one restock band rolled higher bases for a lower price. The restock now prices at the current level, as the stock does.
6. **A Cube craft that failed its rebuild was a free re-roll.** Recipes 22-25 re-tier their target before the rebuild. A rebuild failure returned empty, with the target already re-rolled and nothing consumed. A transmute that makes nothing now restores the grid. This could not happen today (the rebuild fails only on an empty item); it is closed before the next recipe opens it. No recipe takes gold, so the rollback cannot refund anything.

## Checked and fine (vendors and artisans)
- **Charging:** carried gold, piles and stash gold are taken in order, with 64-bit sums and no double spend.
- **Every purchase path:** the index, then the gold, then the room, before anything is taken.
- **Selling, Sell All:** proceeds fit exactly; tabs 2-10, socketed, quest items and charms are skipped.
- **Repair, Recharge, Identify:** prices are capped, the work happens before the fee, and a stale cursor is disarmed.
- **Gillian's workshop, Ogden's boards:** the room is checked before anything is taken; the bench falls back to the stash.
- **The transmutes, salvage, Refresh Until:** all bounded.
- **Statics:** new-game resets cover all of it.
