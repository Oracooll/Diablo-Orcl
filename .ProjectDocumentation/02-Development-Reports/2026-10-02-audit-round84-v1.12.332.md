# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 84 (v1.12.332)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit 8a514709.

Three read-only auditors ran:
- a regression review of v1.12.331, which found nothing;
- the outcomes of the item-transforming recipes (2nd pass);
- the Sorcerer's vanilla-derived spells (11th pass).

## Sorcerer
- **Town Portal in town:** it can no longer be cast there. `CheckSpell` now refuses it in town from spell, scroll or staff alike.
  - Before, the spell is free in single-player and always in the book. Cast in town, it placed a portal that led to the last portal's level and spot. For a new hero, that was another seed's floor, possibly inside a wall.
- **The Golem:** the hero's own spells now spare it. The slot-0 Golem is neither a companion nor a minion, so Fireball, Nova, Inferno, Lightning and Flame Wave struck it. Apocalypse, Chain Lightning and Guardian already spared it.
- **Recasting Golem with no free tile:** the old Golem now stays and the cast is unpaid. Before, the old one died first, the new one had nowhere to stand, and the cast was paid.
- **Town Portal in the dungeon with no valid tile:** it is now unpaid and leaves the standing portal alone. Before, it spent the scroll and wiped the visible portal.
- **Converted allies:** these targeting paths now skip them:
  - Stone Curse;
  - the Guardian's target pick;
  - Chain Lightning's forks;
  - Apocalypse's booms.
