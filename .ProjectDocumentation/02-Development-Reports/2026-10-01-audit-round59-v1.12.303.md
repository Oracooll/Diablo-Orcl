# 2026-10-01 - Audit round 59 (v1.12.303)

**Date:** 2026-10-01. Debug only. 902 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest.

## Regression review of v1.12.297-302
- **The vortex stood four tiles right of the Sorcerer.** `MissileFileData::animWidth2` was an `int8_t`, so Absolute Zero's 224 (and v1.12.298's 480) wrapped to -32. It is `int16_t` now, like `Missile::_miAnimWidth2`. Test: `OracoolCooldowns.AbsoluteZeroSheetIsCentredOnHim`.
- **Town:** every spell casts there, but the field ends in town, so a town cast spent mana and started 30 s of cooldown for nothing. In town it now shows the vortex only: no field, no cooldown.
- **A vortex re-shown down the stairs** lasts only its field's time left. A sheet plays at least once whole, so it had outlived the field by up to 2 s.
- **Cooldowns survive a death** (`ClearRfa12PlayerBuffs` keeps them); a new game clears them.
- **Only the local hero's cooldown starts.** The clocks tick for him alone, so another player's would have refused his casts on this client for ever.
- **The melee blow is clamped again after the share.** Double Swing's share passes 100% from rank 6.

## HUD, 5th pass
- **Under the game menu only the Menu slot answers.** The XP bar and both points frames sat below the menu's line and opened the hero sheet or the Abilities window behind the pause.
- **A held button does not re-ask a skill that is cooling.** Every Absolute Zero had ended in a red line and "I can't do that". The hold ends; a fresh press is told.
- **The XP counter takes the bar's gates** (option, chat, null hero): with the bar off, its strip ate clicks over the world.
- **Escape skips a hidden event log** (behind the full automap), so the game menu opens.
- **No HUD hover sounds** under the game menu or the spell list.
- **A field's countdown row** reads its length as cast (`Field::ticksTotal`), not clock + ticks left; some clocks start seeded.
- **Open:**
  - At 960x720 the two points frames may overlap the bottom corners of open side windows. The geometry was worked out by hand and needs a screenshot.
  - With HUD Plate Art off, the row floats 44 px up. The option is rarely off; left as is.

## Rogue / Monk / Sorcerer passives, 4th pass
- **Companion arrows are not the hero's shots.** The Valkyrie's volleys had:
  - fed Night Stalker, Archery, Leech, Hot Pursuit and Thrill of the Hunt;
  - pierced with the hero's Pierce;
  - spent Dead Ground and the Sharpshooter count.

  Their damage still takes the passives.
- **Sharpshooter** no longer rolls on a weapon's fire or lightning bursts.
- **A staff's side blow** is a burst of the swing: no second Counterstroke charge, no extra Sharpshooter roll.
- **Shiver Armor's retort** takes the damage passives (Glass Cannon, Cold Blooded, Elemental Exposure) and the element marks.
- **Row texts:**
  - Sharpshooter says "+100% damage" (in the pool it is no longer a true double on weapon blows).
  - Swiftness says "to 34% at level 30".
- **For the user:** The Guardian's Path's dual-wield half can never trigger, because only the Bard may hold two weapons.
- **Left as is:** Grenadier counts only plain bow attacks, not the cast bow skills.
