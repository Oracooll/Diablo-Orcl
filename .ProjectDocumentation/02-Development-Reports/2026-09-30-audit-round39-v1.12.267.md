# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 39 (v1.12.267)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 39 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.266;
2. the artisans' workshop;
3. rifts (sixth pass);
4. the Paladin and the Barbarian (bugs only, second pass);
5. the test suite itself;
6. the town.

## Fixed: regressions of round 38

- **The Zod refusal is a refusal.** The Cube and the workshops played the success chime over it, and auto-pick kept
  choosing Free the Sockets for an ethereal Zod host.
- **The weapon's fire and lightning** burst after the swing's skills, where the struck monster stands then (a Bash's
  knockback moves it). There is no burst on an empty tile any more: under "spells never miss" it lay in wait there for
  the next monster to step in.
- **A rift click** interrupted by a window or a held item is forgotten, not kept: it fired minutes later.
- **The hero-select preview** clears the last game's warcry buffs without a recalculation, which could load sprites in
  the menu.
- An indentation made a gated line read as a no-op.

## Fixed: the workshop

- **P2: Gillian's reroll keeps the oils.** Every oil wrote the base fields that the rebuild puts back, so a reroll wiped
  Sharpness, Accuracy, Fortitude and the rest. The rebuild now measures the oil work as the item less the same item
  rebuilt with its own affixes, and adds it back.
- **Cleanse** doubles the removal price, as Remove does.
- **An oil, identify or repair cursor** is no longer lost by clicking the bench or the craft grid. An oil was spent for
  nothing.

## Fixed: the Paladin and the Barbarian (bugs only)

- **A warcry buff recast at another rank** reaches the sheet at once. Battle Orders under Battle Command kept the old life,
  then healed the difference later.
- **The cursor skills need the hero's sight:**
  - Conversion.
  - Grim Ward, Find Potion and Find Item, which took their corpse through a wall.
  - Redemption's corpses.
  - Fist of the Heavens, which is refused before the mana is spent.
- **Chilling Armor** does not answer a ranged hit from a dead Sorceress.

## Fixed: town

- **Glacial Shatter, Ice Arrow and Freezing Arrow** no longer read a townsperson's id as a monster slot. They froze the
  stale monster in that slot.
- **An item no longer lands on or beside an object that takes its clicks:** the waypoint sigil, the Cube, the Rift
  Monument. The cursor found the object first, so such an item could only be picked up by its label.

## Fixed: the test suite

- **Every test starts from the process's own globals:** gbIsHellfire, multiplayer, the screen size, the level, the store
  screen, the cursor, the mouse, MyPlayer and InspectPlayer. The shuffle lane had run tests after others that left these
  changed, so it tested different things from the per-case lane.
- **The shuffle seed of every repeat is printed.** `--gtest_brief` hid gtest's own seed line, so a shuffled failure could
  not be reproduced.
- **The shuffle lane's timeout is 1200 s.** The audit binary's three repeats reached 600 s on a loaded machine.
