# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 115 (v1.12.401)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 951 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks:**
- a regression review of v1.12.400;
- input handling in the Orcl windows and panels (keyboard, mouse, gamepad).

## Fixed: regressions in v1.12.400
1. **Blood Craft turned a 5% "of blood" into the craft's 3% (P2).** Round 114 dropped every kept life-steal roll. Now only a steal no better than 3% is dropped. A 5% is kept first, with one roll more of room, and the craft adds no steal.
2. **A rift guardian could keep a dead monster's variant palette on a revisit.** Round 114 kept him out of `RestoreVariantTint`, so his slot's palette was never reset. He is let through again; `VariantOf` answers None for him, so he is simply reset, and his glaze is drawn at render time.
3. **Sovereign Measure** no longer counts Earthquake pulses or a skill's side blows: `Strike` passes the burst flag to `OnRfa12Hit`.

## Fixed: input
4. **Ctrl+click beside Levski's Cube dropped the item on the floor (P2).** A worn piece, a belt potion or gold that the grid refused fell through to vanilla's Ctrl-drop. Ctrl beside the Cube now does nothing more, as beside the workshop and the stores.
5. **The gamepad's Cancel dropped the held item on the floor (P2),** where a rift's or an arena's floor loses it. `BlurInventory` now puts it away first, as `CloseStash` does: the belt for a potion, then the pack, then the stash. It drops only when there is no room anywhere.
6. **The Rift Monument menu** now closes the other shop surfaces when it opens, as every other opener does; a store or bench could open under it.
7. **The waypoint list** opens with no row chosen. Enter, meant for chat, travelled to row 0.
8. **The crafting book's host buttons** check that the book is still open on release.

## For the user (design calls)
- **The pad's Cancel does not close the Orcl windows** (Cube, workshop, Rift Monument menu, waypoints, the books, the skill picker, Advanced Stats). It closes only the vanilla panels. A fix is for Cancel to act as Esc.
- **Escape on the Mystic's reroll offer** closes the whole bench, not just the offer. Nothing is lost, but it breaks the one-window rule. The same goes for the Salvage confirmation.
- **The companion and minion panel headers** change stance on the press, not the release.
- **A Telekinesis cursor clicked on a window** is spent on nothing.
- **Mouse wheel:** with a store open, it moves the store selection wherever the cursor is.
- **No keyboard focus** on the Cube, workshop, runeword book, crafting book or stash ("keyboard-reachable" rule).

## Checked and fine
- **Modal prompts** own every input.
- **The Escape order** matches the draw order.
- **The shop surfaces** exclude each other, and no item is duplicated between them.
- **Click-through** is absorbed everywhere.
- **Press/release** elsewhere is correct.
- **Hotkeys** are gated.
- **The wheel** scrolls only the window under the cursor.
