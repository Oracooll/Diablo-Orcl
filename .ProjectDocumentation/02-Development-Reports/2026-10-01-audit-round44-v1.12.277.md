# 2026-10-01 - Audit round 44 (v1.12.277)

**Date:** 2026-10-01. Debug only.

**Tracks:**
- regression review of v1.12.276;
- attributes and derived stats at large totals;
- skill levels at large values;
- monster combat against the hero (5th).

The attribute and skill tracks follow the user's questions about 255 base points and stacking affixes.

## Fixed

### Regressions of round 43
- **The rift's kill bar** counted the skeletons now pre-spawned in sarcophagi and barrels. They are active but off the map, and can't be killed until their object opens. The bar now sums only monsters standing on the floor.
- **Ennoble** restores the ethereal keepsake before it re-applies the oils. After, the oils' work took the ethereal +35% and halving.
- **Oils on non-uniques with no affix record:** the measurement runs on any non-unique, so a white item's oils survive Reforge.
  - Uniques are still not measured: the probe doesn't replay unique powers, so they would read as oil work.
- **Spending a stash oil** schedules the stash autosave, as every stash change does.
- **Fit to Screen** is read straight from the ini when the resolution loads, because its own entry loads after.

### Skill levels at large values
- **`ScaleSpellEffect`'s ceiling is now INT_MAX/512:** at INT_MAX/8, `MonsterMHit`'s `<<6` wrapped Flash from about spell level 91 and Fireball from about 107. The stale "MaxSpellLevel 98" comment is corrected.
- **Golem damage:** the vanilla Golem's damage is held at the byte the monster keeps it in (it wrapped past level 119), and the Necromancer golems' spec is clamped too, so their tooltips and the Fire Golem's burn read what they strike for.
- **Flame Wave** asks for the map's bounds before reading `dPiece`.

### Attributes
- **`maxstats` (debug):** raises each base to 255, not the class row's vanilla maximum, which lowered a Paladin's 200 Magic to 50.
- **Found clean:** nothing overflows at totals up to 2000 at today's 255 base cap.

### Monster combat
- **Dread boss kill:** the recalculation runs only when the milestone was claimed and the hero is not dying. Over a dying hero it gave the corpse life.
- **Vampiric champions and Devouring bosses** drain what the hero's life actually lost, melee and missile alike. Before, the blow counted before resistance, the passives and Mana Shield.

## For the user (decisions)
- **Lifting the 255 base cap:** a cap-lift checklist is ready.
  - **Storage:** widen `PlayerPack` `pBaseStr/Mag/Dex/Vit` and `pStatPts`.
  - **One constant:** a single `MaxBaseAttribute` replaces about 15 literal 255s:
    - `ValidatePlayer` and `CheckStats`;
    - `ModifyPlr*` and `CalcStatDiff`;
    - `StatPointsToSpend` and the + buttons;
    - the sheet colour;
    - the elixir sale cap;
    - msg.cpp's `<= 256` add guard.
  - **The amulet and ring conversions:** widen `_iPLHP` / `_iPLMana` (int16_t in 64ths; they wrap past about 1023 base mana / 1279 base life).
  - **Two places that still use the class rows:** the touch + button and `UnPackNetPlayer`.
  - **Optional:** wider item requirements.
  - **The user's open questions:** the ceiling, and Strength as a D2 percentage.
- **Past spell level 30:** "+spell levels" pushes vanilla spells' exponential and level-scaled curves very far:
  - Etherealize: 4.5 minutes at 30, about 15 minutes at 40;
  - Charged Bolt: 34 bolts at 60;
  - Fire Wall and Lightning Wall: minutes long;
  - Fireball: about 145k at 60.
  - Capping the level these formulas read is the user's call.
- **Scrolls and staff charges** take the hero's book level and "+spell levels". A scroll of an unlearned spell fires at the bare bonus, or at 0.
- **Passives and auras** take no "+skills" (D2's do).
- **Melee elements:** the elemental part of a monster's melee blow can go below the 25% floor with stacked element passives and Battle Hardened. The math needs restructuring; not changed.
