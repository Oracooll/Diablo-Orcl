# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 102 (v1.12.386)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 947 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks (three):** a regression review of v1.12.385, rifts end to end with the roster, and the UI added since v1.12.347.

## Fixed: rifts
1. **About 8 roster bosses built an empty rift (P1).** The boss, his escort, his summons and a skeleton type were charged to the floor's 4000 image budget before the roster drew. A Balrog Ascendant, Vortex Sovereign, Vargoth, Sareth or Vael left no room for any ordinary type, and the Thunder Throne, Bone Colossus, Magmaheart and Grimwing left almost none.
   - **What it did:** nothing could be placed, so the bar never filled and the boss never rose. In a Guardian Rift the keystone was spent and, once the clock ran out, lost.
   - **The fix:** the boss and his own are no longer charged to that budget, and a rift with no roster type still gets the lightest one it accepts.
2. **A roster boss's tier scaling was lost on Hell/Torment (P3).** `ScaleRiftMonster` clamped his damage bytes at 255 before `OnRiftBossRisen` reckoned the overflow. A non-original row now takes the tier percent inside its own sums, for life, damage and armour (`RiftMonsterScalePercent`). The four faces of old are scaled as before.
3. **Blinks in narrow passages did nothing (P2, a round-101 regression).** The sighted blink tried one random direction, which in a corridor rarely had a tile the hero sees. It now tries all eight, then beside the hero.

## Fixed: UI
4. **The rift HUD line ran over the elixir and spell timer column (P2).** A long roster name pushed it there. It now shows "the boss" when the name would not fit the mini-map's width; the health bar carries the name.
5. **The last hovered monster's health bar hung over the open game menu (P3).** The hovered monster and item are now cleared under the menu too, as the hover text was in v1.12.376.
6. **The health bar dropped part of a long name (P3).** A long boss name plus a lent aura " (Defiance)" was clipped. The aura is now shown only where it fits.
7. **"Restores all mana." on a Hellfire Elixir of Magic (P3).** The line no longer shows for a Barbarian.
