# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 31 (v1.12.256)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 31 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.255;
2. state that outlives a game (third pass);
3. bounds and index safety (second pass);
4. companions (third pass);
5. using items, every IMISC path (second pass);
6. the spell casting core (second pass).

It also carries the user's two answers from round 30.

## The user's calls

- **Escape closes the top window only** (user: "Top window only"). Each press closes one window, drawn-topmost first:
  1. the prompts, the chat input and the full-screen texts;
  2. the skill picker, the HUD menu, the Rift Monument menu and Advanced Stats;
  3. the benches, the Cube and the runeword book;
  4. a store, one level at a time;
  5. the left panel, then the Abilities window, then the backpack;
  6. the event log last.

  The game menu and death still clear the whole screen; they press Escape until nothing is left, with a bound for a window
  that refuses to close.
- **Quest items cannot be dropped in a rift or a Sealed Map arena** (user: "refuse drops"). Neither floor is ever reloaded,
  so a quest item left there was gone for the game; the Staff of Lazarus that way sealed level 16 until the next game.
  The drop is refused with a line in the log, by mouse and by gamepad.

## Fixed: regressions of round 30

- **P1: respawn keeps the lit aura's bonuses.** v1.12.255 set the 1 life only after the totals, but the aura check leaves
  the aura out for a hero at 0 life whatever his mode - the round 12 bug again, and the next recalculation then healed the
  aura's life for free. The life is set before the totals, as round 12 had it, and again after them.
- **P2: the Chamber of Bone's first Guardian rank** is always given. The book rule asks level 18 for Guardian, the Chamber
  is off level 6, and a refused read spent the quest's only reward. The rule still caps the ranks after the first.
- **The rift's way home** never lands on the corpse tile: a neighbour off the pile first, then any valid one.
- **The stash walk-away** leaves the stash open while an item is in hand, rather than putting the item on the floor.

## Fixed: death and respawn

- **The RfA-12 buffs end on respawn** (Bone Armor's shell, the Mantras, Iron Will, Saga, Rally and the rest), as the
  warcries' do since round 28.
- **Army of the Dead** stops pulsing when the Necromancer dies.

## Fixed: items

- **P2: the belt refills from any potion that stacks with the one drunk.** It matched the base item and the identified flag,
  and potions have two base items each (vendor and drop) and a flag that differs by source: the slot stayed empty beside
  backpack potions of the same kind. The test that pinned the old match now checks the new one (dated note).
- **Using up a backpack slot** recalculates the hero when the list moved: the charm cap counts in list order, and a charm
  that jumped forward was active on its tooltip but not in the totals.
- **The keystone's tooltip** says it is spent at the first step through the portal, as the code does.
- **A signet used from the stash** plays its own sound, as from the backpack.

## Fixed: casting

- **P2: holding the gamepad's cast button** no longer repeats an old spell. The press overwrote the hold's action with a
  cast after the skill had set a swing (or nothing, on a refusal), so a held X with Cleave readied cast the last Fireball.
- **Quick-cast** restores the held spell as well as the held action.
- **A scroll used up or a staff run dry** says "I can't do that" and stops the hold, instead of doing nothing silently.

## Fixed: companions

- **A companion past the leash that stops getting closer** is set down beside the hero after a dozen tries, as a regroup:
  a wall between them left it pushing into the wall.
- **Talic regroups while spinning** when the hero is past the regroup distance; hemmed in, he stayed behind.
- **A volley or double throw** is spent with a shot that did not fly, not carried to the next ordinary shot.
- **A fallen companion leaves no Golem rubble.**
- **The companion and army panels take every click on them**; a click on a row walked or cast at the tile beneath.

## Bounds

No P1 or P2: spell ids against the 290-wide tables, class-tree slots, item, unique, set, cursor and affix indexes, grid cells,
monster and missile slots, every inventory grid, visited levels, quests, lights and the hero file's chunk decoding are all
guarded at the source. Three P3s are carried to round 32: the sprite-mix disk cache is not checked for structure (a
corrupted cache file can read past its buffer while drawing), `UnPackPlayer` clamps the level one past the visited array
(multiplayer-only), and the dead game-save path writes hotkeys as one signed byte.

## Not changed (open)

- **Companions survive the hero's death** (the army is dismissed, D2-style). This was put to the user and is still open.
- **Companion missiles** trigger the hero's on-hit passives (Leech, Momentum, marks) while their melee blows do not.
  Whether both should, or neither, is a design call.
- **Casting:**
  - Tree skills cast at spell level 0 (a -2 item under one invested point), while book spells refuse it.
  - An F-key bound over the picker's staff or scroll cell takes the learned spell's type.
  - The staff bit can say "charged" for an empty staff after some recalculations.
  - A scroll or staff moved away mid-cast (a town ctrl-click into the stash) casts for free.
- **Oils** are spent where they change nothing (an indestructible item, a capped stat). A Barbarian can drink mana potions
  for nothing. An elixir at the 255 cap is spent (vanilla).
- **Gamepad use on pages 2-10** skips the scroll checks. **Gillian's Imbue** finds shards on page 1 only. **A scroll used
  from pages 2-10** can take a page-1 stack of the same spell.
- **The wheel over the bottom HUD strip** no longer zooms (it counts as interface).
- **Telemetry** keeps one session per process, so two heroes in one run share it.
