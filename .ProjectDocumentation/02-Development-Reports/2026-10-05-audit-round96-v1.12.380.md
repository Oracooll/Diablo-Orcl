# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 96 (v1.12.380)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 946 tests pass, one new (`OracoolRogueArrows.AKeysBowShotIsOneShotAndTheHeldSkillReturns`). The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks (three):** a regression review of v1.12.379, the Sorcerer's lightning family, and a hunt for empty item pools (any loot path that, like the armour stand, can draw from an empty pool).

## Fixed: regressions in round 95's own fixes
1. **A stagger made a key's one-shot bow skill permanent (P2).** Stashed for the held button, the latch lost its one-shot flag, so every held shot cast and paid for the key's skill again. A key's one-shot latch is no longer stashed (`IsBowLatchOneShot`).
2. **The held button's own bow skill was lost after a key's shot (P3).** A held Multishot became plain arrows. `MarkBowLatchOneShot` now keeps what the held button had armed, and `DropOneShotBowLatch` puts it back. Test added.
3. **Rift boss missiles were weaker than before the round-95 fix (P2).** `LiveMonsterDamageRange` recomputes arrows, elemental arrows, Fireball, Lightning Control and Inferno from the bytes at hit time, so the overflow percent was thrown away. That chokepoint now applies it, and so does `MonsterAttackMonster` (a boss against the army).
4. **A missed first Jab gave two free thrusts (P3).** A chain's first swing now pays whether its thrust lands or not, because it arms the chain either way.

## Fixed: loot pools
5. **A base type with no base at all (P3, latent).** `RndTypeItemsUpTo` now returns `IDI_NONE` instead of the stale pool slot, and `CreateTypeItem` drops nothing. Reachable only in a build without those bases, such as the shareware one.
- **Nothing else live:** the hunt found no other combination of floor, difficulty, class, rift or Torment that empties a pool.
- **Noted, not coded:**
  - The chokepoint itself still reads `ril[0]` when its pool is empty. Its 11 callers would each need an `IDI_NONE` guard; three already check the type.
  - CreateMagicItem still uses the old twice-the-floor pool.
  - Wirt's gamble leaves a heavy armour, ring or amulet slot blank below character levels 17, 4 and 7.

## Fixed: lightning
6. **Ball Lightning rolled through closed doors and struck the room behind (P2).** It now stops at a closed door (`SightLineClear`), and so does the Lightning Clone's run.
7. **Random lightning strikes picked lightning-immune monsters and dealt nothing (P2).** This affected Ball Lightning, Lightning Rod, Faraday Ring and Lightning Clone. They now pick only from those it can hurt (`LightningStrikableWithin`).
8. **Ball Lightning with no ball was still charged (P3).** When no ball spawns, the cast fizzles, unpaid, and its field is cleared.
9. **Static Charge played its cue on an attacker it cannot touch (P3).** An immune attacker, or one already dead to the reflect, gets no strike and no cue.
10. **Faraday Ring and Lightning Rod let trap missiles through (P3).** They now catch every enemy missile, traps included, as the text says.

## Reported, not coded
- **Lightning Rod and Storm Crucible share a sheet:** ending one can end the other's art on the same tile. Rare, cosmetic.
- **The armour-stand fallback:** on the affected floors it always drops the type's lowest base.
