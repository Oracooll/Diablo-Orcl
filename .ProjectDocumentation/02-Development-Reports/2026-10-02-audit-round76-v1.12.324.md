# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 76 (v1.12.324)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit 71204400.

Three read-only auditors ran:
- a regression review of v1.12.323;
- the Rogue and Monk actives (11th pass, with the extra-blow counters);
- the missile and lighting infrastructure (1st pass).

## Rogue and Monk
- **Skill side blows** now strike as bursts, as the weapon cleave's have since v1.12.318: the per-blow passives pay, and the swing counters wait for the front blow.
  - This covers two groups. The RfA-12 swing extras (`SideBlow`: Sweep, Cleave, Crusade, Aegis Slam, Backhand, Holy Lance, Reaping Point). And the class spins and sweep (Wheel of Heaven, Sweeping Reed, Fend).
  - Before, a Wheel of Heaven into five monsters advanced Mythic Rhythm five times, so it never lapsed. It cycled Cadence almost twice, so side blows landed its +50% beat every swing.
- **Momentum's +25%** goes only to a blow that spends one of its charges. Bursts had taken it for free, so "your next 3 blows" became three swings of up to nine empowered blows.

## Input and the Cube
- **Held buttons** spring back without acting on two more occasions:
  - the focus pause (the window losing focus);
  - the start of a load. A button held into a load had still acted on a release after it.
- **Free the Sockets on a Zod-only ethereal host:** when the player picks the recipe, the Cube now gives the reason ("Zod is bound for good in an ethereal item"). v1.12.323's fallback could not reach that message.

## Missiles and lights
- **A lit missile that leaves the map** frees its light. Before, it was deleted before its own process could free it and held one of the 64 slots until the next level. A charge's light is the exception, as it belongs to the monster.
- **Census-effect end and stonegate portals:** they clear `_mlid` after freeing it.
- **Missile drawing** skips a frame its sheet does not have, instead of indexing it unchecked.

## Left open
- **Tint table:** `TintedTable` rebuilds its 256 entries per tinted missile per frame (about 1-2 ms at a few hundred missiles). A cache keyed by base and colour would remove it.
- **Saved missiles:** a saved missile's animation length and width are not restored on load. This does not matter in V1, which always starts a New Game.
- **Press-time controls:** they wait for the user's press/release pass.
- **Workshop wells:** an empty transmute at a well plays the success sound.
- **Guardian Rift keystone:** whether to refund it is the user's call.
- **Earlier rounds:** the items listed in the round 75 report.
