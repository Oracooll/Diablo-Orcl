# 2026-10-01 - Audit round 43 (v1.12.276), and the 750 attribute check

**Date:** 2026-10-01. Debug only.

**Tracks:**
- regression review of v1.12.275;
- Levski's Cube recipes and the workshops (6th pass);
- quests and level generation (3rd);
- the save pipeline and autosave (3rd);
- windows, input and Esc (3rd).

**Also in this build, at the user's word:** "Remove the per tick 750 check in single player".

## Fixed

### Regressions of round 42
- **Belt and stash oils (P1):**
  - An oil used from the belt or the stash was still spent at once, then a second one was taken from the pack when it landed. With none in the pack, it was refused and lost.
  - Now no use path spends an oil when it is picked. `HasOilToSpend` / `SpendOneOil` cover the pack, its tabs, the belt and the stash.
  - The oil test covers the belt.
- **Blight's pool:** it is the last open tile of the straight line to the clamped aim. `LineEnd`'s 8-way walk strayed off the line, and an aim on a wall tile put the pool inside it.
- **Saved resolutions:** a size off the curated list is kept only with Fit to Screen on.

### Cube and workshops
- **Oils on rebuilt items:** they survive Reforge, Retier and Ennoble. The measurement Gillian's reroll used is now shared: `MeasureOracoolOilWork` / `ReapplyOracoolOilWork`, Permanence's 255 included.
- **Cleanse Shards:** Gillian's Transmute never picks it. A Transmute had stripped shards for free, and could win the grid-alone pass over an Enrich the player meant.
- **Recast depth gate:** Recast offers only pieces the item's depth could drop, as Consecrate does. The two Recast test fixtures built set pieces at item level 0; they now carry a found level, as a drop does.
- **The Cube grid:**
  - a grid item no longer jumps onto an oil, Identify or Teleport cursor;
  - Ctrl+click places only into a grid that is on screen (not the Recipes tab or Griswold's Salvage page);
  - gold never goes into the grid, the craft well or the bench.

### Windows and input
- **Shop under the picker:** a right-click on a skill-picker cell no longer buys or sells the shop item under it.
- **Chat line:** it takes every key but F12 and PrintScreen, so Tab, F11 and the keypad no longer act while typing.
- **Mouse wheel:** it scrolls the quest log only under the cursor.
- **HUD menu:** its Spellbook entry is refused over a store, as B and D are.
- **Skill picker:** it ignores clicks during the death fall.

### Saves
- **Lost level save:** a level marked visited whose save cannot be found is built fresh (`LevelSaveExists`, logged), instead of a fatal error.
- **Gold held at death:** it goes to the stash first, as at exit.
- **Autosave:** it also requires an empty hand.

### Level generation
- **Rift sarcophagi and barrels:** they raise their skeletons. They were placed before the floor had monster types, so `FinishRiftLevel` now pre-spawns them and scales them with the theme packs.

### The user's questions
- **The 750 attribute check:** `CheckCheatStats` clamped Strength, Magic, Dexterity and Vitality to 750 every tick, after life, mana and damage had been worked out from the full totals. Past 750 the sheet, to-hit and the requirements read 750 while the rest used the real value.
  - The clamp now runs in multiplayer only (`ClampAttributesForMultiplayer`).
  - The life and mana ceiling still runs everywhere.
- **The +spell-level total:** it is held at the int8 field's range. Past +127 it wrapped to -128, which would have dropped every active skill to level 0.

## Open, or the user's call
- **255 base attributes:** the user asked whether the cap can go, D2-style. A redesign was proposed in chat:
  - one `MaxBaseAttribute`;
  - the hero file's base attributes and unspent points widened to 16 bits;
  - the + buttons and sheet boxes checked at four digits;
  - optionally, Strength as a D2 percentage.
  - It awaits the user's answer.
- **Keystone tiers:** loot from any difficulty against the current difficulty's monsters at +3% a tier.
- **Hive and Crypt:** "Unlock All Town Entrances" leaves the Hive and Crypt quests' texts stale.
- **Barbarian mana potions:** the decision is still open.
- **Murphy's Shrine:** still uses carried gold only.
- **Others:**
  - D2 crafts can merge past the affix ceiling;
  - Ogden's ladder can lose its inputs when every container is full;
  - Imbue reads only the main pack page;
  - Recast and Consecrate don't carry oils;
  - a workshop reroll offer dismissed with Esc keeps its gold;
  - three buttons act on press (the parked press/release pass);
  - the vanilla Fire Wall and Lightning Wall lost their countdown rows.
