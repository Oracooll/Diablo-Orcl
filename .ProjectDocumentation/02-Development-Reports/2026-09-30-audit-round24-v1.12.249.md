# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 24 (v1.12.249)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 24 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.248;
2. what changed for the Paladin and the Barbarian since the user signed their skills off (v1.12.225);
3. monster AI against the new stun and Dim Vision rules;
4. level transitions and portals (second pass);
5. sockets, runewords, charms and shards (second pass);
6. tests that pass for the wrong reason.

Every finding was verified against the code first.

## Fixed: regressions of round 23

- **A refused stash** (newer version or item format) says it is left untouched, not "saved correctly from now on".
- **Making room for a reward** again treats an empty-socket white item as junk; only filled sockets protect an item.
- **Closing up an extra backpack page** at load no longer re-checks the readied scroll before the later pages load.

## For the user: Paladin and Barbarian changes since the sign-off

These are intended fixes, but they change how skills the user signed off play. They need a play check:

- **Stuns now last through blows.** From v1.12.244, a skill stun (War Cry, Smite, Stun, Paralysis) runs its full
  length. Before, the first hard hit replaced it with a flinch, so a War Cry followed by Whirlwind let each monster fight
  back after one spin blow. From this version a knockback no longer ends a stun either.
- **Fist of the Heavens strikes its target once**, with the 150% blast its text names. Before v1.12.247 all 36 ring
  bolts also hit the centre, about 15 times the stated damage, and the ring flew across the room.
- **Charge ends its dash** (10 mana, no refund) when no path to the target exists for a tick, for example a target
  boxed in by its pack. Before, the dash stayed armed for 2 seconds, and the next melee click got the arriving blow
  free.
- **Leap and Leap Attack** lost Teleport's take-off sound (two sounds instead of three), as the other teleport skills.
- **Melee skills refuse a bow**, Smite always hits, a respawn empties Rage, and a held Weapon Throw keeps throwing.

## Fixed: monster AI

- **A knockback no longer ends a skill stun.** Bash, a knockback weapon, Bone Wall's shove and Unstable Anomaly moved
  the monster through the flinch, which reset the stun.
- **A Counselor blinded by Dim Vision** no longer stays invisible and unhittable. Once it faded out to retreat, its AI
  returned before the fade-in for as long as the curse lasted.
- **A stun clears a pending special move.** A perched or healing gargoyle kept it, and the next swing lifted it into
  its special attack mid-stun.
- **Dim Vision reaches zombies and Sneaks.** Their AIs never read the "noticed" clock, so a blinded one hunted as ever.
- **A Zeal burst ends** when another skill takes the latch. The rest of the burst swung free, carrying the new skill.

## Fixed: transitions

- **The Portal button and T work under -spell-level gear** (Crackrust, Bovine Plate). The built-in Portal had
  reached level 0 and did nothing, silently.
- **A respawn ends a cold armour.** Its tint and freeze-on-hit had followed the hero into town.
- **A pending "step into the rift" click** ends at a level change. It had outlived a Sealed Map trip and pulled the
  hero in, spending the keystone, the next time he walked near the portal.

## Fixed: sockets

- **P2: salvage never destroys stones.** Only White items with sockets were protected. A punched Rare full of runes was
  salvaged with every stone in it, and a salvage charm could do it on pickup.
- **P2: Sell All skips socketed items with stones in them.** The price is the base's alone, and an Enigma sold for a
  white body's quarter, runes and all. A single sale from the list still works.

## Tests

- **Two tests had pinned the champion-as-unique bug** fixed in round 23. Their champions had no unique type, which the
  game never makes. They are fixed in v1.12.248.
- **Two Paladin tests** hold usable shields, as the game's rule has required since v1.12.243.

## Not changed (open)

- **Right after a stun ends,** a skeleton, snake or counselor attacks without its usual roll. Vanilla's delay behaves
  this way.
- **Charge's dash lost to a momentary no-path:** a refund or a longer grace is the user's call (above).
- **Imbuing a runeword:** dragging allows it, the Mystic refuses it.
- **Make Ethereal then Cleanse** removes more durability than Tempering gave.
- **Charms:** the active ones follow list order, not grid order, and the tooltip never says whether this charm is
  active. Gillian's charm loan can pick an ineligible charm first.
- **A negative gold find** does nothing unless another source makes the total positive.
- **The legacy skill-point functions** are tested but no longer called. The tooltip sweep never asserts that each
  recipe ran.
- **Timed buffs other than cold armour** (Infravision, Etherealize) still ride a respawn.
