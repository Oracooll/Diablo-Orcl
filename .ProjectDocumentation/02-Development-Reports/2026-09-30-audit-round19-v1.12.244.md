# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 19 (v1.12.244)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 19 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.243;
2. status effects, across every system (third pass);
3. rules computed in more than one place;
4. every timer: game ticks against the wall clock;
5. the hero's basic attack (third pass);
6. the monster death pipeline: XP and loot (second pass).

Every finding was verified against the code first.

## Fixed: regressions of round 18

- **P1: refunding stats could still kill.** The life floor read "was alive" after the stat change had already
  recalculated the gear, so it never fired in the cases it was written for. It is read first now.
- **Sealed waypoint rows** lose the "(sealed)" suffix. The longer names wrapped onto a clipped second line; the dormant
  pad and the gold name carry the state.
- **A refusal under a held button** is said once, not at tick rate.
- **Heavenly Grip's shield** stays in hand, with a message, when no room can be made anywhere on a full floor.
- **The Rift Monument's refusals** show on screen and in the hero's voice, as the waypoints' do.

## Fixed: the hero's attack

- **P1: two skill latches rode one swing.** A Paladin tree skill and an RfA-12 swing (Judgment, Crusade, Aegis Slam and
  the rest) could both be armed at once. Both skills then fired and both were paid: Zeal's speed, to-hit and chain on a
  Judgment, or Judgment's bonus and price on every Zeal blow. Arming one now clears the others.
- **Melee skills refuse a bow**, as in D2, with a red plate. The hero had drawn the bow and struck in melee with the
  skill's bonus. A bow skill's latch also rode a held melee button and fired paid volleys. Plain Leap is still allowed.
- **Smite always connects**, as its text says.
- **Monsters turned by Conversion** are allies: the swing, the cleave, Zeal's chain, Hammer of Faith's splash, the
  arrows, Radiance and the cursor all spare them.
- **A held Weapon Throw** throws every time. It threw once, then swung at the air in place.
- **Long Reach** carries the weapon's fire and lightning to the enemy it reaches.
- **The RfA-12 swings** strike their sides and rear even when the front tile is empty.
- **The multiple-arrows flag's second frame** no longer wears the bow or counts a Grenadier shot when a bow skill has
  already loosed its volley.
- **Bash with a Knockback item** pushes one tile, not two.
- **Jester's damage** is computed in 64 bits.

## Fixed: status effects

- **P2: Paralysis's stun was wiped by the hit that caused it.** A hard hit flinched the monster out of any stun. A
  stunned monster no longer flinches out of it, and the longer of two stuns wins.
- **Relentless Assault** counts real stuns, not the pause every AI takes between decisions.
- **Diablo and the Dread bosses stand as uniques.** Frailty executed them, Terror and Confuse turned them, and freezes
  held Diablo solid. Static Field takes half from them, as from uniques.
- **A flinch or knockback mid-step** brings the monster's light along.
- **Decrepify under Eternal Torment** no longer wraps its slow to twenty minutes.

## Fixed: timers

- **P2: the holy auras and Static Field pulsed on every re-light.** Swapping the aura off and on with two F-keys gave
  about twelve times the damage. Their clocks keep running while the aura is out.
- **Mercy's cooldown and Soft Tread's quiet** survive a level change. A new game still clears them.
- **Readied-skill and aura swaps** save on the spaced trigger kills use, not on the next loop after every swap. Points
  spent and passives slotted still save at once.
- **A new Fire Golem** no longer burns early on a reused record.
- Two comments that still called Charge's clock wall-clock time are corrected.

## Fixed: rules computed twice

- **Tree skills the hero cannot pay for** (mana, Rage, Essence) go red on both wells, as the click refuses them.
- **Zeal's tooltip** says 1 mana a strike, what it charges.
- **Weapon Throw** goes red without a sword or axe to throw.
- **Adria no longer buys a Guardian Keystone** for 1 gold. Griswold already refused it.
- **An item the hero's class cannot use** (a head in a Paladin's hands) says so in red on its tooltip.

## Fixed: death, XP and loot

- **Necromancer bases** (wands, scythes, heads) get the drop tail: sockets, ethereal, Magic Find, the noteworthy log.
- **Gharbad, the Defiler, the Hork Demon and Na-Krul** reach the fork's drop families: sets, gems, necro bases, signets,
  Sealed Maps.
- **No later drop lands on or beside a rift's way home**, so a pickup walk cannot end on it.

## Not changed (open)

- **Mark bursts on death** (Ashen Brand, Exploding Palm, Bloodcall, Tithe of Ash) fire only when the hero or his army
  lands the kill, not on a trap's or a turned monster's. "Erupts when it falls" may mean any death - the user's call.
- **Kills by Conversion, Confuse or Berserk** pay no XP. D2 credits Conversion kills - the user's call.
- **Lachdanan's scripted death** logs a kill row in the telemetry and can roll kill passives. Harmless.
- **The Rift Monument and the Cube's town tick hooks** never run in town. Harmless today: the Cube is one frame.
- **Wall-clock cosmetics** (spins, aura ground cycle, missile tints) move during Pause. Acceptable.
- **A frozen monster's death animation** keeps the ice tint.
- **Heavenly Strength's penalty** stops with a shield under its requirements, a side effect of round 18's shield rule.
  It reads as the grip doing its job.
- The open list carries on from round 18: staff and scroll cells collapsing into the learned spell, the gamepad's rift
  entry, the rift portal's lower tiles, the low-life aura swap, the gamble prices.
