# 2026-10-01 - Audit round 53 (v1.12.289)

**Date:** 2026-10-01. Debug only. 899 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest.

**Tracks:**
- regression review of v1.12.287-288 (nothing serious found);
- the Necromancer (9th pass), with corpses now restored on a revisit.

## Fixed
- **Bone Storm renews instead of stacking.** Three casts used to make three storms with three times the damage, while the buff row showed one. A recast now renews the running storm's time, rank and art.
- **A body's record goes with the body.** `ForgetCorpseAt` runs in all of these:
  - `AddCorpse` (a death that records the body re-adds it right after);
  - the scavenger's meal;
  - the Barbarian corpse cries;
  - Epitaph.

  Before this, an eaten body's record could revive the eaten monster out of the next sprite laid on that tile (a Stone Curse shatter, the Golem's rubble). Since v1.12.288 such a stale record was also saved and restored on a revisit.
- **Dark Mending** heals in 64-bit math. A Revived minion at 4x a high Torment corpse's life could overflow it.
- **Storm Crucible** strikes and draws the first conductor's tile too.
- **Comment:** `MonstersOnLine` has its comment back.

## Open, for the user

- **Monsters killed by Stone Curse** leave no usable corpse (the shatter's rubble is not recorded).
