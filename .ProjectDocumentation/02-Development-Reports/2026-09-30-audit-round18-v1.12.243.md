# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 18 (v1.12.243)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 18 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.242;
2. waypoints and the Rift Monument (second pass);
3. class-tree investment, respec and the point stores (second pass);
4. readied skills, the skill picker and hotkeys (second pass);
5. the quality-of-life overlays: auto-pickup, labels, health bars, the XP bar (second pass);
6. class-specific items (Necromancer wands and heads, Paladin shields, bows) and the equip rules.

Every finding was verified against the code first.

## Fixed: regressions of round 17

- **The keystone drops before the way home is chosen.** Laid after it, the keystone could land two tiles out, right
  beside a way home at three - the pickup-walk trap round 17 set out to close.
- **The way home is a short walk from the corpse.** A tile three out across a wall had beaten a corridor tile five out,
  and a cleared Guardian Rift ends only through that tile.
- **The goat shrine and the skeleton room** keep their packs off a rift's arrival too. Round 17's check covered only
  the ordinary theme packs.
- **A piece bought from the Set shelf** takes its own value back. The 2,700 salvage floor is the shelf price only; the
  bought piece had sold for 675 and repaired at about 60 times a found copy's.

## Fixed: waypoints and the Rift Monument

- **Sealed waypoint rows look sealed.** A floor reached in an earlier game whose quest gate is still shut this game
  (level 16 before Lazarus falls, the Hive and the Crypt before their entrances open) draws with a dormant pad, a gold
  name and "(sealed)". Clicking one says why on screen and in the hero's voice; its one line had gone to the event log,
  closed by default.
- **Telekinesis from two tiles** no longer lights a sigil and opens a list that closes itself the next frame.
- **Releasing the mouse during Pause** no longer travels or opens a rift. Travel is also refused mid level change.
- **The waypoint list closes on every level load**, as the Monument menu does.
- **The Monument's Guardian row** finds a keystone on any backpack page. A keystone auto-placed on page 2 had left the
  row red.
- **A refused rift** says why once. A vaguer second line ("does not answer", "would not turn here") followed the
  specific one.

## Fixed: readied skills and hotkeys

- **Equipping or recharging a charged staff under a lit aura** no longer readies the staff's spell beside the aura. The
  aura is the right button; the hero had both, and the hover and the well disagreed.
- **A left F-key bound to a scroll** survives a reload.
- **The eight RfA-12 bow skills** (Barbed Shaft to Phantom Volley) are refused at the click without a bow. They played
  the whole cast animation first, every cycle of a held button. Their plates, and the Rogue's arrow skills', go red
  without a bow, as shield skills do without a shield.
- **The built-in Town Portal** is no longer listed among the picker's spells. It is the Portal button, hidden wherever a
  real spell shows; Town Portal scrolls stay under SCROLLS.
- **The left picker shows the F-key** of a right-button-only skill bound from it. There was no badge, and a second
  press silently unbound it.

## Fixed: class items

- **Heavenly Grip's released shield** goes to the stash when the backpack is full, and the floor makes room first. On a
  full floor the drop did nothing and the shield was cleared anyway.
- **A shield whose requirements lapsed** no longer powers Smite and Blessed Shield.
- **Worn gear the class may not use** loses its stats, as gear under its requirements does.

## Fixed: quality of life and respec

- **Monster kill counts** reset with a new game. They had carried from the previous character.
- **The monster bar's XP line** reads 0 for a hero at the level cap.
- **Auto-pickup of gold into the stash** stops while the stash file is refused.
- **Refunding stat points** never kills: a living hero keeps at least 1 life.

## Not changed (open)

- **A staff or scroll cell and the learned spell collapse** into the learned spell for F-keys, for the readied pair on
  reload, and in the Abilities window. A proper fix carries the binding's type through the hover and the save.
- **A gamepad cannot step into a rift.** A pad reaches it only by pointing the right stick and pressing R3. The gamepad
  pass is the user's call.
- **The rift portal's lower tiles** open the Monument menu instead of entering: only about three of its seven boundary
  tiles enter.
- **Melee skills swung with a bow** walk up and swing it. D2 refuses this; it is the user's design call.
- The open list carries on from round 17: the low-life aura swap, the Thunderclap line, the material ladder, Gillian's
  jewellery reroll, the Talic regroup, the charger light lag, the gamble prices.
