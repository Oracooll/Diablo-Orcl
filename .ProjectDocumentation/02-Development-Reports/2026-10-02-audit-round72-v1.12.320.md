# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 72 (v1.12.320)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit 23916480.

Three read-only auditors ran:
- a regression review of v1.12.319;
- the Sorcerer, Rogue and Monk actives (10th pass, weighted to the lightning and fire families);
- inventory, stash and belt (7th pass).

## Inventory
- **Two quick drops:** two drops inside one game tick placed a copy of the second item twice, and the first item was lost.
  - Cause: a drop parked the whole item in one shared `ItemLimbo` until its `CMD_PUTITEM` was handled on the next tick, and the next send overwrote it.
  - Easiest to hit: Ctrl+clicking junk out of the pack during an autosave hitch.
  - Fix: `ItemLimbo` is now a queue of the hero's own drops, matched by seed, index and create info. Only `CMD_PUTITEM` uses it.
- **A drop with no tile left by the time it is handled:** it goes back to the pack, the stash or the hand. Before, it was thrown away, with the hand already empty.

## Sorcerer
- **Lightning Clone:** it bursts where it stands when its next step is a wall. Before, after a Teleport or a corner it cut straight through walls for up to 8 s and struck packs in rooms the hero never saw.
- **A dead hero's Lightning Clone and Funeral Spiral:** they end at once. The field loop never runs for a dead hero, so they stood frozen beside the corpse until their missiles ran out.
- **Storm Crucible:** it refuses, unpaid, a second conductor with a wall between it and the first. Before, the arc was drawn through the wall but struck only up to it.

## Regression review of v1.12.319
- **A charge's blow on the army:** its unmissable 500 stays unmissable, as on the hero. The new minion odds had clamped it to 95.
- **Re-aiming after a curse:**
  - A curse laid over Confuse or Attract re-aims too, as does a live release.
  - `ReaimMonsterAfterSideChange` no longer clears `MFLAG_TARGETS_MONSTER` by hand. With no enemy found, the monster id it kept was read as a player index.
