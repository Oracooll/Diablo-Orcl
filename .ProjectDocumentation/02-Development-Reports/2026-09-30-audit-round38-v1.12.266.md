# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 38 (v1.12.266)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word ("resume non-stop audits").

Round 38 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.265;
2. travel between levels;
3. hero stat formulas;
4. sockets, gems, runes and runewords;
5. the hero's death and respawn;
6. the stash and the backpack's extra pages.

## Fixed: regressions of round 37

- **P1: the weapon's fire and lightning** (Enchant, Vengeance, the Masteries, fire and lightning weapons) now burst only
  where a blow lands, and then always land, as in D2. Round 37 had them roll the spell to-hit, which a melee hero misses
  almost always past Normal. Before that, they were spawned before the swing's own roll and burned on a miss.
- **P1: fresh Town Portal scrolls** no longer come back on a reload. The hero's items rebuild from their seeds, so round
  37's index swap returned as a scroll. A fresh useful drop now moves its seed on until it rolls something other than a
  scroll, so the seed it keeps rebuilds into what dropped. Stored seeds replay unchanged, so the pack goldens hold.
- **Corpse Explosion and Poison Explosion** take a corpse the hero can see from where he stands, not any corpse near a
  cursor he can see.
- **The held-button guard** keeps the modal Help and Chat Log screens.

## Fixed: stats and looks

- **P2: a stat change in play reloads the hero's look** when a weapon turns red or back: taking a charm to the stash,
  slotting a passive, refunding a tree point, a milestone. The sprites and attack frames stayed on the old weapon until
  the next level.

## Fixed: sockets

- **P2: Zod stays in an ethereal item.** Freed, it returned the host whole at its maximum: a free repair of what no smith
  repairs, and the same Zod did it again for the next item.
- **Zod in jewellery** no longer counts as indestructible. It did nothing there, and the ring said it did.

## Fixed: death

- **The respawn ends Etherealize, Infravision and Search.** They rode it into town, where Etherealize kept the hero immune
  to melee.
- **A dead hero deals no retaliation** (Retribution, Static Charge, Thorns, the cold armours, Feedback). A rift guardian
  could die to the corpse, and the rift cleared.
- **Gold on the cursor at death or exit counts again.** At exit it goes to the stash first. The shops under-counted it.
- **The HUD menu** does not open windows during the fall.

## Fixed: stash and travel

- **A stash page flip** forgets the hovered item: a Ctrl+click in the same moment moved the last page's item.
- **Stash items that grew** go back to the stash first, not into whichever hero's backpack loaded next.
- **SORT's sound** plays only when it sorted.
- **No autosave** between a level change's request and its load.
- **The rift entry** waits for an empty cursor and no open window. A keystone lifted on the way was "no longer with you".
- **A waypoint journey** starts from a stand, still beside the sigil.

## Not changed (open)

- **Press/release:** SORT still acts on the press. The press/release pass is parked for the user.
- **Gamepad:** it cannot move items between the stash and pages 2-10. The gamepad pass is parked for the user.
- **Stats:**
  - The vanilla Rage cooldown never re-checks requirements (debug-only reach).
  - The attack-frames rows differ from StartAttack's skip.
  - The Barbarian's shield counts at full for +% armour.
  - The sheet's draw clamps `_pStatPts`.
- **Sockets:**
  - Four Hel runes show -20% each against a 60% cap.
  - Freeing a runeword blanks the stored name.
  - A non-ethereal item freed of Zod is still repaired to full (the wear before Zod is not kept).
- **Death:**
  - A curse's tag makes any later death of that monster the hero's kill.
  - Telemetry logs a set level's id as the floor.
- **Stash and pack:**
  - Page numbers of 100 and up are accepted on load.
  - Locked pages still feed the belt refill.
  - A cancelled two-handed shift-equip leaves stale stats (rare).
- **Travel:** an open Cube that cannot close rides the stairs (documented as intended).
