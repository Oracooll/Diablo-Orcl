# 2026-10-06 - Audit round 123 (v1.12.412)

**Date:** 2026-10-06. Debug only. All 954 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks:**
- a regression review of v1.12.411 (no P1 or P2);
- the monster combat core: attacks, the hero's defences, statuses, kill credit and loot.

## Fixed (P2)
1. **Diablo's Apocalypse wrapped to 1 damage (my round-122 regression).** The scaled damage went into `var3`, an `int8`, so it wrapped past 127: Torment at 3.5, or a rift Diablo at a high tier, made every Boom deal 1 HP. `var3` holds the base 40 again, and `MonsterRangedSpecialAttack` lays the damage percent on at the release, in an int. The rift boss's extra shots ride the same value.

## Fixed (P3)
2. **The Counselor's Firebolt** (round 120's fix) is scaled at the release too, in `MonsterRangedAttack`, not stored in the `int16` `var2`. A zero damage stays zero; the other ranged attackers' missiles roll their own.
3. **A minion mage's Charged Bolt** carries its overflow, which now matters for a revived Torment Magistrate.
4. **The end of a stun is no guaranteed attack.** `MonsterDelay`'s `M_StartStand` left `var1 = Delay`, which the skeleton, snake and Counselor AIs read as "strike now". A stunned monster's stand now records `Stand`.
5. **Iron Maiden** no longer returns a blow from a dead hero, as Thorns and Retribution already do not.
6. **A charge at the army** takes Might, as the charge at the hero does.
7. **Aegis Slam's knockback rule** asks for the paid, usable armed swing (`PaidArmedRfa12Melee`). Armed but unpaid, the blow landed plain and lost the item's push too.
8. **`SyncObjectAnim`** clears a missing object sheet, as `SetupObject` has since round 122.
9. **The `PackAdjustedDamage` comment** no longer says "clamped".

## For later / design
- **A newer `oracool.mpq` with more icons** still overruns the width table inside `CelToClx` before the count check fires. Only an older build against a newer archive hits it.
- **Freeze does not stop a charge in flight.** `StunMonster` excludes the charge by design.
- **The petrified death guard** checks `mode == Death` only. No caller can kill twice today.

## Checked and fine
- **Melee to the hero:** armour, block, Dodge/Evade, the elemental share with resistance, Mana Shield, the damage-taken floors, Vampiric/Devouring and the reflections.
- **`MonsterAttackMonster`'s** army rules.
- **Every AI** checks for a stand; freeze and chill skip the tick.
- **`SpawnLoot`** has one caller.
- **Minions, companions and conjured copies** pay no XP or loot.
- **Overflow plumbing:** int64 throughout, and no realistic `int` overflow.
