# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 97 (v1.12.381)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 946 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks (three):** a regression review of v1.12.380, the Monk's actives, and quests with the townspeople.

## Fixed: regressions in round 96's own fixes
1. **The Ball Lightning and Lightning Clone door stop did nothing (P2).** `SightLineClear` (LineClear) never tests its end tile, and both moved one tile at a time, so the closed door one step ahead always passed. They now use a per-step test, `LineStepBlocked`: wall, closed door, or a diagonal between corner-touching walls.
2. **A stagger during a key's bow shot lost the held skill (P2).** The held button's own bow skill was lost again by this route. The stash now keeps what the held button had under the key's latch (`HeldArrowUnderKeyShot`, `HeldBowSpellUnderKeyShot`).
3. **Lightning Rod's catch-burst still struck the lightning-immune (P3).** It now uses `LightningStrikableWithin`.
4. **A failed Ball Lightning cast could evict a live field (P3).** It now refuses before taking a field.

## Fixed: Monk (and every line skill)
5. **Line skills struck through closed doors (P2).** Long Thrust, Heaven Splitter, Dragon's Wrath and every other `MonstersOnLine` caller, across all classes, cut through closed doors and diagonal wall corners. `LineOfTiles` and `TilesBetween` now stop on `LineStepBlocked`.
- **The same flaw in the landings:** `LastClearTileToward`, where Vault, Leaping Crane, Shoulder Gate and Heaven's Descent land, also never tested the end tile, so a closed door could be a landing. It now stops on the step test too.
6. **Staff of Echoes never echoed while the Monk kept swinging (P2).** It has one slot, and a new swing under a second apart overwrote the waiting echo. The waiting echo now lands first (`FireStaffEcho`).
7. **Leaping Crane's and Shoulder Gate's landing blows reached across walls (P3).** They could hit a monster across a wall corner or a door; they now need a clear step.
8. **Retribution played its cue on a magic-immune attacker (P3).** It now strikes, and cues, only an attacker it can hurt.

## Fixed: quests
9. **A quest entrance tooltip showed inside a set level (P3).** `ForceQuests` had no set-level guard. Back in a set level through a Town Portal, the Chamber of Bone showed Poisoned Water's "To A Dark Passage" and snapped the cursor there. It is now skipped on set levels and for unavailable quests.

## Reported, not coded
- **Sealed-Map arena chests:** `IsQuestSetLevel` counts the arenas, so their chests, if their maps place any (not verified), pay like a quest level's (always full, magic). Whether arenas should is the user's call.
- **Everything else in the quests track is clean:** rift copies of quest bosses, quest items in rifts, double or never completion, level order, quest marks, windows, new-game state.
