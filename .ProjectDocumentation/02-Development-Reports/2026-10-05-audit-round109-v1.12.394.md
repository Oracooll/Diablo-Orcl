## Fixed: the reward keystone
1. **Duplication with the pack and the stash full (P2).** On a level change, a reward with no room anywhere stayed on the cursor and was still owed. Dropped on another floor, it was out of every check, and the next rift refunded a second one. Dying with it held and no room dropped it at the corpse, with the same result.
   - **The fix:** the reward held on the cursor now counts as kept. The settle clears the debt, every level change settles, and so does death, before the held item is put away.
   - The exit save still puts a held item away.
2. **An open Cube or workshop refused every Guardian Rift (P3).** The message asked the hero to pick up a keystone that may have been gone. The window check is removed: the reward reaches the grid or the bench only by way of the cursor, which now settles it.
   - **Test:** `OracoolAudit108.TheRewardKeystoneOnTheFloorIsStillWithinReach` now also covers a held reward settling.

## Save format (no P1/P2)
Nothing in the window changed the save format.
- **Item format:** still 17, reading 13-17.
- **Elixirs:** the only new player fields, transient by design. They never reach the saved stats.
- **The keystone debt:** it reuses chunk 21, clamped on load, and every single-player save settles before it writes.
- **Unreadable stash or page files:** these refuse placement, so a debt stays owed rather than landing in a file never written.

**Small fixes:**
- the `_pOracoolOwedKeystoneTier` and chunk 21 comments now describe the reward debt too;
- a `uint8_t` cast is now `uint16_t`;
- a test feeds chunk 21 a tier of 0xFFFF and expects 255.

**By design, for the record:**
- elixirs running at quit are gone in the next game;
- gear worn only thanks to an elixir's Strength loads drawn red until the requirement is met again.
