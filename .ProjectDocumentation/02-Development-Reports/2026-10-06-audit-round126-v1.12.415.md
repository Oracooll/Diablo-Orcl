## For the user (design calls)

- **Several put-away paths reach the town stash from the dungeon:** gold auto-placement, death, the pad's close, the displaced item, the exit save. This amounts to remote stash access, consistent and deliberate.
- **The belt accepts any usable 1x1 item by hand** (scrolls, oils, runes). Only automatic placement is limited to potions.
- **Cube grid and workshop items are lost at exit** with a full pack and stash. This is logged in red; the exit writes no world.
