# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 14 (v1.12.239)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 14 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of rounds 10-13 (v1.12.235-238);
2. runtime format strings (a mismatch throws, and the game ends);
3. the affix engine (Loot 2.0), third pass;
4. shrines and objects meeting the fork's systems;
5. the Necromancer's army, corpses and curses, third pass;
6. resource and performance hazards.

Every finding was verified against the code first.

## Fixed: regressions of round 13

- **The companion and army headers stopped answering a click.** Round 13 made them HUD, and the HUD branch had no
  handler for them. They are answered there now.
- **Staff of Echoes and Tragedy's share paid the damage passives twice.** Their damage is a share of a blow that
  already had them, and round 13's `Strike` added them again. Such shares now skip the passives.
- **RfA-12 spells counted as melee on an adjacent target**, under round 13's "adjacent means melee" rule. Only a
  swing's extra blows are melee now, as `MonsterMHit` treats every spell.
- **The waypoint rows' right 7px were dead.**
- **Galvanizing Ward's clock** now restarts before Chord of Warding, not after it.

The format-string track found no reachable throw. It checked all 723 `fmt::runtime` sites, and they match their
arguments under fmt 11. It also compared the placeholders in all 21 translation catalogs.

## Fixed: items

- **Uniques that set their armour or damage lost their base tier.** This covers 27 set armour and 5 set damage. A
  Torment Demonspike Coat had Normal's armour at Torment's price. The set value now takes the tier.
- **Cube rerolls and crafts rolled a fresh base tier.** A Torment base came out of Reroll Rares at Hell or lower four
  times in ten. They keep it now, as Ennoble, Recast and the Mystic already did.
- **A Decay weapon's record decays with it,** so a rework of another row no longer restores the first roll.
- **The affix level floor is capped at 25.** From item level 62, a ring's bonus affixes could only be Movement Speed
  or Faster Cast. A Gillian Reroll on a ring that held both offered nothing, after the gold was taken.
- **A Primal keeps its perfect to-hit** through a rework.
- **The Decay and Crystalline tooltip lines** read their own share, not the whole item's damage.

## Fixed: shrines and objects

- **Religious Shrine** no longer writes over a socketed Zod or repairs an ethereal item, and it recalculates the hero.
- **Hidden Shrine** stays below 255 durability (255 is the indestructible value), and an ethereal item gains only
  maximum durability.
- **Gloomy Shrine** now covers the six worn slots.
- **Glowing Shrine** keeps the level floor in its low-level branch too.
- **Enchanted Shrine** skips Town Portal.
- **Armour stands and shrine choice in a rift** use the rift's floor, not its set-level number.

## Fixed: the Necromancer

- **P1: a minion's corpse could index `Corpses[-1]`.** A type loaded mid-floor has corpse id 0, and its direction
  bits made the tile non-zero. The corpse is not laid now, and the draw ignores such tiles already saved.
- **A Revived Stalker or Unseen** fought its whole life invisible. The hidden flag is cleared now.
- **The army dies with the Necromancer**, as in Diablo II. It had re-formed whole on the next floor after a Respawn.
  **The user's call to reverse.**
- **A corpse a scavenger ate** leaves the corpse table.
- **The hero's own swing** never lands on his minions.
- **Frenzy of the Dead** now reaches the Skeletal Mages' bolts.
- **Soul Harvest** pays Essence only for the souls it actually tore.
- **The army's deaths** are no longer counted as kills of their kind.

## Fixed: resources

- **The slot background lookup** allocated a string twice per grid cell per frame. The map now uses a transparent
  comparator.
- **Hero sheet boxes** reuse one buffer instead of copying 40 KB per box per frame.
- **Sprite dye copies** no longer copy the lit tables. The mixer's worker could copy a table the main thread was
  still building (a thread race).
- **`exportdun`** refuses a file it cannot open. It had crashed on the null handle.
- **A base with no short name** no longer reaches fmt, which throws on a null string.
- **The Greek catalog's charge string** had a stray brace.

## Not changed (open)

- **A rework erases oils.** A proper fix needs the oil bonuses recorded on the item.
- **Other rework drift:**
  - Shop armour's base armour moves within its range.
  - A staff keeps doubled charges after Plentiful is rerolled away.
  - Offers ignore the good/evil theme.
  - Gillian's lock survives a Cube reroll. The rule is a design call.
- **Shrines and books that raise spell levels** ignore the Rule of Rangs. A design call.
- **Mana shrines for a Barbarian**, who runs on Rage. A design call.
- **Shrine log lines** state changes that the 0-255 clamp removed.
- **Minion armour** only matters against missiles. A design call.
- **Skeleton, mage and golem specs** do not scale with difficulty. A design observation.
- **Performance leftovers:**
  - Tint tables are rebuilt per sprite.
  - Mixed sheets are kept for the session.
  - Skill sounds are cached per row, not per file.
  - Menu backgrounds are held during play.
  - Telemetry opens its file for every row.
