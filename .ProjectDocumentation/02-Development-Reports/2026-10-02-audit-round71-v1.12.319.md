# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 71 (v1.12.319)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit 4f44ca62.

Four read-only auditors ran:
- a regression review of v1.12.317-318;
- Paladin and Barbarian (8th pass);
- monster AI and the army (7th);
- the save pipeline (7th) with rifts (9th).

## Regression review of v1.12.318
- **Minion armour:** v1.12.318 took the armour off the attacker's raw to-hit. The hero's odds add +30 and the level gap first, so most monster blows on the army fell to the 5% floor and the army was close to unkillable in melee. It now uses the hero's formula: +30, then 2 x (attacker level - owner level), less the armour, clamped to 5-95.
- **Cleave side blows:** they keep the per-target parts of `OnAnyHit`, Resolve's debuff and the physical mark. The `burst` return of v1.12.318 had skipped them.
- **Empty tree positions:** they draw a bezel only, as before v1.12.317. The Unspent plate made them look like skills to spend on.

## Monster AI and the army
- **A charge** (Horned Demon, snakes) strikes its aimed target or the other side only. A charge drawn by a Decoy or Attract had struck any packmate in its path, unmissably, and the kill paid loot and experience.
- **Attract against archers and casters:** a hostile's shot now strikes the Attract-cursed monster. Before, shots flew through it, as two hostiles count as one side, and on into the skeletons.
- **When Conversion, Confuse or Attract ends:** the monster and everything aimed at it pick a target afresh (`ReaimMonsterAfterSideChange`). Before, a standing melee AI swung once more at its former enemy, now on its own side.

## Paladin
- **The swing price latch** (`LatchPaladinSwingPrice`):
  - Smite's bonus, its forced hit and the on-hit settle now read one answer, taken as the hit frame began.
  - Before, a mana steal in the blow could charge an unboosted Smite. Peril under Mana Shield could give a boosted, forced Smite for nothing.
  - A blow that could pay at the front and lost the mana since takes what is left, as `SettleSkill` does.
- **Oathbrand:** it neither brands nor spends a charge on a magic-immune monster.
- **Wrath of the Heavens:** its pillars pick from the monsters they can hurt.
- **Zeal's tooltip:** it reads "Swings: N, in one attack's time", as Double Swing and Frenzy do.

## Save pipeline and rifts
No new defects. The write and read order, the item format gates, the crash-safe writes, the level saves and the rift lifetime were all traced and hold.

**For the user (design, not a bug):** a keystone spent on entering a Guardian Rift is lost if the game is exited or crashes inside it, since rift state is never saved. That is how Diablo III behaves. The alternative is to refund the keystone on exit from an unfinished rift.

## Tooling
- The edit scripts' `ed()` now merges a second entry for the same file. Two entries each read the file from disk, and the second write dropped the first entry's edits; this round's charge fix was lost that way, and the link failure caught it.

## Left open

- **Pad:** no gamepad on backpack pages 2-10 or on the belt.
- **Level files:** they are trusted on load.
- **Oil:** the to-hit tooltip.
- **Zeal:** the missed opener.
- **Belt:** whether it may refill a scroll slot.
- **Shop:** the fallback row acts on press.
- **Abilities window:** no keyboard or pad focus.

- **Meteor:** the impact takes about 1 s.
- **Crafted Rares:** a crafted Rare keeps its name after a reroll.
