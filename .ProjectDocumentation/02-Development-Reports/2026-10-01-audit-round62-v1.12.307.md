# 2026-10-01 - Audit round 62 (v1.12.307)

**Date:** 2026-10-01. Debug only. 902 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest.

## Regression review of v1.12.306
- **A stagger between the leap and the blow keeps Leap Attack's paid blow.** Clearing it on every disarm had charged the blow twice. Another skill armed, or a new game (`ForgetLeapAttackPrepaid`), clears it. The leap itself never reads it.
- **The stagger stash is replaced whole when a latch is armed.** Merged field by field, a quick-cast between two staggers could arm two latch families at once. With nothing armed (a second hit in recovery), it is kept.
- **A dead Skeletal Mage's bolt taps no life.** Passing null to Life Tap meant "the hero struck", which healed him.
- **A failed hero creation** removes the file the writer may still have published. That file would now block the slot for good.
- **The "game" record** has 512 KiB too. It is reached only from the multiplayer and format-mismatch paths.

## Rifts, 8th pass
- **A rift floor rebuilt after its guardian rose raises him again** (`FinishRiftLevel`). The rebuild follows a failed level save that un-marked the floor. The slot he held is an ordinary monster on the rebuilt floor, and `IsRiftGuardian` took it for him: a trash kill dropped the guardian's pile and keystone.

## Monster AI, 5th pass
- **A golem hit mid-step is not snapped back.** It does not flinch, so its walk ran on from the tile it left, and the walk's end cleared its own grid mark: the Necromancer's walking golems became unclickable and unhittable.
- **Decrepify's end or replacement takes back only its own chill** (`ClearChillUpTo`). Before, it also wiped a longer chill laid by a Clay Golem or Rigor Mortis.
- **A charge's push-back** honours knockback immunity and moves the target's light.
- **Left as is:** `MonsterHeal`'s int16 counter is unreachable in play.
