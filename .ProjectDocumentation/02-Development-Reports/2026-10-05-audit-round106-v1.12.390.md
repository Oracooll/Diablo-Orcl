# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 106 (v1.12.390)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 949 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks (two):**
- a regression review of v1.12.389, the reward keystone tracked by its seed;
- the cost of the new sight and path checks.

## Fixed: the reward keystone
1. **A duplicate through the rift's end (P2).** `rewardOwed` and `rewardSeed` lived in `RiftState`, which every rift end wipes (the town rebuild, `EndRift`, a new rift) while the owed tier stays. A reward carried out on the cursor, then put away in town, was refunded a second time.
   - **Where the debt lives now:** file-local `RewardOwed` and `RewardSeed`, cleared only by a new game.
   - **Settling in town:** the settle runs there too, once a second. The exit save and the carry home settle at once.
2. **A use-after-free when a stash keystone opens a rift (P2).** The refund `OpenGuardianRift` may give can grow the stash's vector, and the used keystone was a reference into it. Its tier and seed are now read before opening, and the stash code reads "is it a keystone" before the call.
3. **An older debt lost while a Nephalem Rift was open (P2/P3).** The refund-before-opening now runs whatever rift is open. It settles first, and skips only a reward actually held on the cursor. A reward left on an ended rift's floor is refunded, then forgotten.
4. **A rebuilt cleared floor re-laid a reward held on the cursor (P3).** It no longer does.

## Fixed: cost (estimates from the code; nothing measured)
5. **The guardian's spot search.** Two fixes:
   - **The order:** it asks whether his type is loaded before searching. The refusal came after the search, and `ProcessRift` retried it every tick.
   - **The path budget:** at most 32 reachability paths. A hero by a lava lake could see hundreds of unreachable tiles, each a failed 300-node path, a hitch of tens of milliseconds (hundreds in Debug).
6. **Companion targeting.** It now measures distance before sight. With a full army in a Hell pack it was about 450 sight lines a tick.
7. **`NearestTo` while a cast's sight is set.** Distance comes before sight. It traced a line to every monster on the floor per cast.
8. **`SightLineClear`.** It reverses its recorded tiles when needed instead of sorting them, about half the cost of every call.
9. **The Guardian spell's arc search.** It looks for a monster on the tile before tracing a line, which was 192 lines per Guardian every 16 ticks.

## Checked and cheap
- The auras, cries, curses, Army pulse, `MonstersWithin`, `HeroInReach`, the Fire Golem pulse and Whirlwind all check distance before sight.
- The rift boss blink and the minion spawn are bounded.
