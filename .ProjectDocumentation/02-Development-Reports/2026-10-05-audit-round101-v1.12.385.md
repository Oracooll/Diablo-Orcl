# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 101 (v1.12.385)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 947 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks (three):** a regression review of v1.12.384, item effects since v1.12.347, and the monster behaviour the fork added, checked against doors and walls.

## Fixed: rift boss signatures through walls
1. **The slam, the chill and the timers ran through walls (P2).** `HeroInReach` now needs the boss's sight to the hero. It gates:
   - Biclops and Na-Krul Unchained's slam (1.5 blows and a stagger), which hit a hero two tiles off through a wall or a closed door.
   - Zhar'ul's cold-ring chill.
   - Every "engaged" timer: raises, broods, rings, blinks, burrows.
2. **Blinks landed past walls (P2).** Malignus, the Shadow of Terror, Vael and the Defiler Empress could land in the next room or in a sealed pocket. `MoveMonsterInstantly` now takes an optional `seenFrom`, and `BlinkNearHero` lands only on a tile the hero can see; with none, no blink.
3. **A petrified boss stays inert (P3).** He no longer answers blows with fire rings, and his life thresholds (rings, guard calls, splits) wait until the stone ends.
4. **Summons appear on the boss's side first (P3).** Raised dead, broods, escorts and copies try a tile in his sight before any other.
5. **A petrified split copy left a stone corpse when it expired (P3).** `DespawnMonster` now takes it out of stone first.

## Fixed: text
6. **Conversion's tooltip:** "Uniques, bosses and the magic-immune refuse." Since round 100, rift bosses refuse.
7. **Hellfire's Elixirs of Magic and Vitality:** they also refill mana or life, and their tooltips now say so.

## Kept as they are (reviewed)
- **Frozen Orb stops at lava:** that is its behaviour before round 99 (it is not a sight line).
- **Bone Wall arms pass a single wall corner:** this follows the line rule.
- **A spear with a shield wears the mace-and-shield body:** the user's "staff + shield, same combo" rule; tested.

## Reported, not coded
- **An open shop's red/white requirement colours:** they are not refreshed when an elixir runs out mid-visit.
- **The vanilla Rage spell:** it recalculates without the requirement pass. Probably unreachable in this fork.
- **The Guardian's arc search (micro-optimisation):** it could test for a monster before testing sight.
