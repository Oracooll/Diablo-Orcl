# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 25 (v1.12.250)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 25 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.249;
2. the hero sheet against combat (second pass);
3. the Necromancer's army (fourth pass);
4. autosave and the save pipeline (second pass);
5. mouse routing through windows (second pass);
6. item drop generation (second pass).

Every finding was verified against the code first.

## Fixed: regressions of round 24

- **Dim Vision holds a rallied Fallen pack again.** v1.12.249 exempted every goal but Normal; only a retreat (and a
  faded-out Counselor) is exempt now.
- **Levski's hand-picked salvage** refuses an item with stones in it, as the plates do. Free the Sockets first.
- **Making room for a reward** leaves every socketed white alone, empty sockets included, as the user's rule on
  socketed whites says. v1.12.249 had let empty-socket whites go.
- A Portal level floor that had no effect is removed.

## Fixed: the hero sheet

- **An unusable bow** (under its requirements) no longer switches the sheet to the bow formulas. The hero swings it in
  melee, and the sheet showed ranged to-hit and halved Strength damage. This affected the sheet, the odds bar and the
  hero-select column.
- **Movement speed on the sheet** keeps the fraction of a tick the feet carry. It read 125% for 115% and 166% for 150%.

## Fixed: the army

- **The Fire Golem's burn and the Iron Golem's thorns** flinch their targets as a monster's blow does. Through the
  hero's hit path they turned the golem's attackers onto the Necromancer, the opposite of a tank.
- **An enemy's shot at the army** is blunted by Weaken and Decrepify, as its blow is since round 20.
- **A Skeletal Mage's bolt** is credited to the mage that fired it, not to whoever holds its slot now.

## Fixed: saves

- **The save's final swap retries** a sharing or lock refusal (an antivirus or sync client opening the fresh file). One
  refusal after the hero file was published had left a new hero beside an old stash.
- **diablo.ini is written when a menu closes**, not on every Space press, HUD-menu entry or well click, each of which
  wrote and flushed the file on the main thread.
- **Autosave no longer marks** "the archive holds a game" after saving the character only.

## Fixed: mouse routing

- **P2: the HUD menu's Character and Quests** entries refuse over a store dialog, as the C and Q keys do. The sheet had
  drawn over the shop panel while the hidden shop took its clicks: a right click bought the item under it.
- **The right button** respects the modal chat log and help text. It had drunk, equipped or readied what lay hidden under
  them.
- **The help text** closes every window when it opens, so the wheel no longer scrolls a hidden Cube or book.
- **Quest text** takes a click before the left panels it is drawn over.
- **A targeting cursor put away over a window** does nothing else with that click.
- **The HUD menu's Inventory entry** closes the full-screen books first.
- **The Cube's recipe book** takes the wheel only with the cursor on it.

## For the user

- **Champions still drop as scripted uniques.** SpawnItem and the Necromancer bases test "unique" before "champion",
  so every champion drops a guaranteed magic-or-better item at 8 times the unique odds. The round-23 fix covered the
  treasure bonus, item level and rift credit only. Whether champions should be that generous is a balance call; the
  2026-08-30 note ("they seem to drop uniques very generously") suggests not.
- **Chests, barrels and weapon racks** draw bases and unique eligibility from twice the area level, a vanilla leftover.
  So they can hold a unique whose minimum level is above the item's stamped level.

## Not changed (open)

- **Glass Cannon's +15%** is applied but not shown on the sheet. Mana steal and the Barbarian's armour pierce read as
  general but are melee only. Thorns-aura return and percentage damage reduction appear on no default sheet.
- **Minion armour** (Bone Plating, golem armour) counts against missiles only, as vanilla. Raise and Golem spend before
  checking that a minion record is free.
- **The stash's flush** still runs after the hero is published. Only the rename retries now.
- **Moving many items through the stash** saves once per item. Every level change copies the hero archive twice.
- **Town Portal scrolls** still drop from chests and barrels. The Unique drop chance option truncates to 0% for ordinary
  drops below 50%.
- **Right clicks** pass through click-away popups (the Monument menu, the HUD menu, the skill picker).
- **Companion headers** are clickable under docked shop and Cube pages. The HUD menu is tested before the Monument menu
  drawn over it.
