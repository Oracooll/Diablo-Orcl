# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 98 (v1.12.382)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 946 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks (three):** a regression review of v1.12.381, the Paladin's actives against walls and doors, and the Necromancer's army and curses.

## Fixed: regressions in round 97's own fixes
1. **Lines stopped at every one-tile doorway (P2).** The diagonal rule in `LineStepBlocked` stopped a step when EITHER side was a wall. Screen N/S/E/W are tile diagonals, so a line through any one-tile doorway stopped, and a hero standing in one could not aim Vault, Leaping Crane or Heaven's Descent at 45 degrees. It now stops only when BOTH sides are walls, as its comment said.
2. **Shoulder Gate's rush still ended on a closed door's tile (P2).** `RushEndToward` now uses the step test.
3. **A Staff of Echoes echo that killed was re-armed on the corpse's slot (P3).** It no longer is; the swing still pays.

## Fixed: sight through closed doors (a systemic one)
4. **`SightLineClear` never tested its own end tile (P2).** Everything that asked it about an adjacent tile, or about a closed door as the target, got a yes. It now refuses a closed door at the end and the one diagonal step squeezed between two walls. Adjacent strikes covered by this: Crusade, Heaven's Descent's landing, every `MonstersWithin` user.
5. **Holy auras and Sanctuary reached through closed doors (P2).** Holy Fire, Freeze and Shock pulses, Static Field, Thunder Storm and Sanctuary used `LineClearMissile`, which sees through a closed door. They now use `SightLineClear`.
6. **Cries and Conversion reached through closed doors (P2).** Conversion, War Cry, Weaken, Dirge, Grim Ward (`ForEachInEarshot`) and Redemption's corpse search: the same change.
7. **Fist of the Heavens through a door (P2).** It landed past a closed door, or on one and fanned out of the doorway. It now refuses, unpaid, before the mana.
8. **Holy Lance through a door (P2).** It struck through a closed door or a thin wall in front of the Paladin: its line started on that tile. It now stops there.
9. **Aegis Slam's side tiles (P3).** They reached across wall corners and doors.
10. **Hammer of Faith's splash (P3).** It reached across wall corners and doors.
11. **Blessed Shield's bounce (P3).** It could pick a monster behind a closed door and lose the bounce on it.

## Fixed: Wrath of the Heavens
12. **Pillars and mana spent on nothing (P3).** A cast with nobody in reach is now refused, unpaid. A pillar is spent only on an enemy, and the storm passes after twice its time.

## Fixed: Necromancer
13. **A Revived Winged Demon froze on its first step (P2).** The Gargoyle AI's perch flag (`MFLAG_ALLOW_SPECIAL`, set by `InitMonster`) holds the animation, and the Revive did not clear it.
14. **Iron Maiden turned a pack fighting the army onto the Necromancer (P2).** A blow returned from a strike on a minion now flinches the monster as a monster's blow does and keeps its target (MinionHurts' rule, round 25).
15. **Revive raised a boss with a boss's life (P2).** It no longer revives a Dread boss or a rift's guardian.
16. **Attract pulled monsters through walls and doors (P3).** It now needs sight, the taunt's rule.

## Reported, not coded
- **The Fire Golem's flare:** a golem hit while rising still skips it (cosmetic, reported in round 95 too).
- **Ball Lightning's fallback:** its no-art fallback is now unreachable code.
- **`ForceQuests`' set-level guard:** added in round 97, it is redundant (both callers already gate `!setlevel`). The Chamber-of-Bone tooltip came from another path, not yet found.
