## v1.12.334

### Monk

### Regression review of v1.12.333
- **Cursed converts:** a converted monster that is also cursed (Weaken or Decrepify) lays no acid puddle. `MonsterDeath` now reads the convert state before the curse's release clears it.
- **Two-hander rule:** the right-hand two-hander rule now applies only when the left hand is empty. Otherwise a shield-for-shield quick-swap was lost.
- **Cursor fallback:** the quick-equip cursor fallback keeps life.

## v1.12.335: the oil to-hit (closes the open item)
- **The problem:** the Oils of Accuracy and Mastery put their to-hit into `_iPLToHit` with no line of their own.
  - A rare's or magic item's tooltip never showed it. A white sword with three Mastery oils had +12% to hit, unstated.
  - King's, Dull and Doppelganger rows take their to-hit as "the item's, less the plain to-hit rows", so they showed the oil as their own roll. A Cube rework then kept it as theirs, and the oil measure read 0.
- **The fix:** a new `Item::_iOracoolOilToHit` field, item format 17. It is an int16 gated tail; -1 means unknown, for older items.
  - The oils add to it, a fresh base zeroes it, and the oil measure reads it.
  - Reapply sets it.
  - Both damp-share derivations (the tooltip and the rework's share) leave it out.
  - It prints as "chance to hit: +N% (oils)".
- **Test:** `LoadSaveItemFormats` now covers 17 -> 16 -> 15 -> 14 -> 13.
