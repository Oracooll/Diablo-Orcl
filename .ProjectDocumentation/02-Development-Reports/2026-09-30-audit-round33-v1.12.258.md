# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 33 (v1.12.258)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 33 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.257;
2. rifts (fifth pass);
3. the Necromancer end to end (fifth pass);
4. the hero sheet against combat (third pass);
5. every path an item moves between containers (fourth pass);
6. readied skills, the skill picker and hotkeys (third pass).

## Fixed: regressions of round 32

- **P2: closing the stash with gold or a charm in hand recalculates.** Round 32 made "put away" the normal path, and it
  skipped the gold total and the stats a floor pickup used to trigger: a gold pile put away did not count until something
  else recounted. Gold now goes through the gold path.
- **The HUD menu answers while chat is open.** It is drawn over chat, and the round 32 chat gate swallowed its clicks.
- **The town refusal** ("I can't cast that here") speaks once, like the seven round 32 fixed.

## Fixed: charms

- **P2: the three live charms are the first three on the page**, page 1 first, then pages 2-10, by where each sits. They
  were the first three in the list, which every removal reshuffles: using up a scroll, paying with a gold pile, salvaging or
  spending a keystone could change which charms counted without anything moving on screen, and without a recalculation.
  A new test pins grid order over list order.

## Fixed: skills and hotkeys

- **P2: a Scroll or Staff choice survives a reload.** Readying the Scroll or Staff cell of a spell the hero also knows came
  back as the mana cast: only the spell was saved and the learned spell won the re-derivation. The type now rides in the
  save entry's two free top bits; older saves read as before.
- **F1-F8 do nothing behind a store dialog**, as A, S, I, C, Q, D and B since round 20. An aura key lit its aura behind
  Adria's counter.
- **Clearing a spell from the hotkeys** sweeps all twelve slots, so an old save's slot 9-12 binding cannot linger beside
  its F-key.

## Fixed: the hero sheet

- **P2: Glass Cannon's +15% shows on the sheet.** Round 20 moved it out of the weapon totals into the damage passives, and
  it left every damage reading with them. It is back on the weapon damage and the readied spell's range.
- **The hero-select list** shows no buffs from the last game: a Shout cast before Save & Exit gave every hero in the list
  its armour.

## Fixed: items

- **A displaced item on a pickup** goes to the stash before the floor; a quest item reached a rift floor that way.

## Fixed: rifts

- **P2: no rift guardian is immune to fire, lightning and magic at once.** Round 32 fixed Na-Krul; the Skeleton King's
  Hell row is all three as well, so on Hell and Torment half of all Nephalem Rifts fielded a guardian a caster could not
  touch. Every guardian now resists instead.
- **P2: a guardian's keystone (and a Dread boss's sealed map) never lands beside a rift's exit.** It was placed on the
  corpse tile, and a guardian dying at the arrival put it where a pickup walk ended on the exit - and in a cleared Guardian
  Rift the rest of the pile was lost with it.
- **A missing keystone at the gate says so on screen**; the log alone was closed by default and the click did nothing.

## Fixed: the Necromancer

No P1 or P2: no minion record leaks or frees twice, and no bone skill hits the army.
- **Raise Skeleton, Raise Skeletal Mage and the golems ask for a free record first,** as Revive does. Dying bodies hold
  theirs; a Raise ate its corpse for a cast that fizzled, and a golem cast dismissed the old golem with nothing to replace it.
- **Decompose on a poison-immune monster** fizzles instead of being paid for nothing.

## Not changed (open)

- **Rifts:** the guardian's spawn spot has no reachability check; the way home may be laid next to (not on) a standing Town
  Portal; the guardian's pile can be cut at the item cap; the monument menu does not show an open rift before replacing it,
  and an unentered Guardian Rift can be re-turned to reroll its guardian; two voice lines on one keystone refusal.
- **The Necromancer:** Final Service and Rathma's Shield count every rift as the same floor; the army panel shows the group
  cap, not the rank's; kills by golem burn and thorns fire the hero's on-kill passives while skeleton kills do not; ring
  positions go stale after some releases.

- **Sell All** sells page-1 charms, gems, runes, shards and signets (the user's call on drop-only items is still open).
- **Made items and salvage materials** can be lost when every container is full; `DropItemBesidePlayer` can fail silently
  at the item cap. **Workshop Imbue** sees only page 1. **A pad drop while walking** can lose the item (vanilla).
- **Aura keys** survive a refund or respec, invisibly. Auras cannot be bound or badged in the Abilities window. Quick Cast
  is ignored by F1-F8. Badges match on the spell, not its type.
- **The sheet:** negative skill bonuses round one lower than combat; a refused melee skill still shows a boosted number;
  Advanced Stats hides Heavenly Strength's slower swing; the odds bar can use the last attack's weapon mode; the critical
  hit and the chill's slower actions appear nowhere.
