# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 27 (v1.12.252)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 27 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.251;
2. the options (second pass);
3. the HUD's drawing against the game's state (second pass);
4. integer widths and overflow (second pass);
5. the stash and the backpack pages (third pass);
6. item tooltips (fourth pass).

Every finding was verified against the code first. The options track also caught v1.12.251's window-close save sitting
in a branch the X button never reaches; that was fixed before v1.12.251 was committed (see round 26).

## Fixed: regressions of round 26

- **P2: Recast's readiness check uses the recipe's own list.** v1.12.251 kept Recast within the item's class in the
  recipe only, so 18 set pieces (every set weapon, the rings and amulets alone in their set) showed Recast ready, and
  Transmute did nothing and said nothing. The auto-pick could choose that dead Recast over a recipe that works.
- **A Monk's staff cleave** stands aside only when Sweeping Reed or Wheel of Heaven can actually be paid for. Armed but
  unpaid, the swing hit neither the skill's side tiles nor the cleave's.
- **Murphy's Shrine** passes over an item at 1 durability. Kept at 1, the shrine did nothing, said "halved", and spared the
  gold.
- **The test-side Cube auto-pick** follows the same Reforge tie rule as the one the game uses.

## Fixed: the HUD

- **P2: the right well draws through the left well's code.** It drew a readied spell itself and had drifted:
  - a book spell with a tree row (most of the Sorceress's book) kept full colour at 0 mana while the click was refused;
  - a staff spell got the tree plate instead of the orange one, and no charge count;
  - the well did not sink while held down, against the press/release default.
- **P2: Furious Charge shows its cooldown on the left well too.** Only the right well darkened while it cooled.
- **"Game Saved"** sits beside the speed readout, not on it. It hides with the clock during chat and under the windows.
- **The Portal button is dimmed in town,** where its click is refused.
- **The rift bar** moves to the map's top when the mini-map is off, instead of hanging under an empty space.

## Fixed: tooltips

- **P2: a charm says whether it is active.** The three-charm cap counts in pickup order, which the grid does not show.
  Four Charms of Vigor all promised +20 life; now the fourth reads "Inactive - over the 3-charm cap" in red.
- **P2: a loose rune, gem or jewel states the level it imposes.** "Its item will require level 69" on a Zod, red when
  above the hero. A socketed Zod had turned a level-20 hero's sword red with no warning.
- **P2: attack-speed lines off the bow** say "(with your bow: arrow speed)" while the hero shoots one: on items, set
  bonuses, runes and runewords. With a bow those flags only quicken the arrow.
- **A broken item** says it gives nothing until repaired (or mended, for ethereal).
- **Ethereal** reads "no smith repairs it; the Cube's Mend does".
- **Cain's identify screen** prints Zod's indestructible stamp as "Indestructible", not "Dur: 255/60". Its requirements
  now match the item panel: Hel and Ease lower them, and the level and class rules are added.

## Fixed: the stash and the backpack pages

- **P2: a stash record emptied at load** (it failed validation) is removed with its cells, as the backpack pages' are
  since round 23. Kept, it looked free and was not, and SORT counted it and refused the stash for good.
- **P2: a backpack page whose grid disagrees with its count refuses the page file**, as every other inconsistency there
  does. Emptied with the file accepted, the next autosave made the loss permanent.
- **Ctrl-click moves recompute the hero:** into the stash, out of it, into Levski's grid, and an auto-picked charm. A
  banked charm kept giving life, a charm taken out gave nothing, and a last scroll moved away stayed readied, until
  something unrelated recalculated.
- **Closing the stash with an item in hand and no room anywhere** drops it beside the hero after making room. It ended the
  game with a fatal error.

## Fixed: options

- **P2: Unique Drop Chance Percent** gates fresh unique rolls by a seed hash instead of narrowing the window. An
  ordinary drop's window is two tickets, so 25% and 10% came out at "never" and 75% the same as 50%. No extra random
  draw, so saved items rebuild the same.
- Texts that did not match the code: Auto Pickup Gems also takes jewels; the Reset Stats ini comment names the list
  sheet; the unique percent's header note and the game-speed step count.

## Fixed: overflow

- **P2: the damage passives and curses** add their percentages in 64 bits and clamp. A high-level Fireball (spell level
  65 and up) is about 32 million in the engine's 1/64 units, and "+40%" wrapped int: the hit landed hundreds of thousands
  off, and Life Tap's share came out negative and drained the Necromancer it should heal. Nine sites: the missile hit,
  the four damage curses, Life Tap, the RfA-12 strike, the melee strike and the aura pulses.
- **Peril's double damage** stays inside the int limit after Jester's clamp.

## Not changed (open)

- **The skill picker** tints only for bindability (right-button-only, missing shield). A curse at 0 Essence is gold in
  the picker and red once bound. Whether the picker should show "castable now" is a design call.
- **The orb values** can overflow the glass at 10000/10000, and 0 Rage reads in danger red although it is the
  Barbarian's resting state.
- **Refresh Until with timeout 0** can run 100,000 generations and freeze the window. The option is off by default and
  its tooltip says so.
- **Every readied-skill change rewrites diablo.ini.** It is safe now that every exit saves, but still a main-thread
  write per F-key swap.
- **Duplicate flags on runewords** (a rune's knockback and the word's own) print twice. Set rungs whose speed flags do
  not stack are all green. Light radius reads in two units on one set piece. A runeword's type line falls into the card
  body.
- **Narrow margins, not reachable today:** the Gargoyle heal amount in an int16 (1.46x headroom), mana-to-life items in
  int16 fields (1.47x), and Etherealize lasting hours at spell level 70.
- **Gold held at death** goes to the pack as a pile rather than the stash (harmless; gold is recounted on load).
- **Spin blows now advance the per-hit counters once per target** (Cadence, Mythic Rhythm, the momentum countdown, Deep
  Wounds rolls). This matches the RfA-12 sweeps, but a Wheel of Heaven through eight enemies moves them eight times as
  fast. A design check for the user.
- **Demo playback** can write its option overrides into the player's ini if Esc ends it early. Developer-only.
