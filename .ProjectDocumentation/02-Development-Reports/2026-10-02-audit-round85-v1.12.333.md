# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 85 (v1.12.333)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit f61025a1.

Three read-only auditors ran:
- a regression review of v1.12.332, which found nothing (one comment corrected);
- equipment slots and equipping (1st pass);
- monster specials and variants (8th pass).

## Equipment
- **Quick-equip with a two-hander in the right hand:** quick-equipping a one-hander or shield now swaps out a two-hander left in the RIGHT hand.
  - Heavenly Strength's grip can leave one there when the passive comes off.
  - Before, Shift/Ctrl-click said "I have no room", while dragging the item worked.
- **Lifting or quick-swapping a +Life item at low life:** this no longer kills.
  - Picking an item up (`CheckInvCut`) and quick-equip (`AutoEquip`) now recalculate with `CalcPlrInvKeepingLife`, as the paste path does.
  - Which gesture moved the gear no longer decides whether a +Life loss kills.
- **Checked and clean:**
  - the six extra slots;
  - two-hander and shield pairing;
  - requirements with Ease and Hel and level-free;
  - `_iStatFlag` refresh;
  - swap rollback;
  - ring placement;
  - set bonuses counting only worn, usable pieces;
  - gear-look reload;
  - outgrown reseating.

## Monsters
- **A converted charger** (Horned Demon, snake) no longer strikes the hero's skeletons, companions or other converts in its path. Its 500 is unmissable, and the round-71 side test let a convert through because it is berserk.
- **A converted Acid Beast** lays no acid puddle on death. Once its corpse was removed, the puddle's live source was no longer a convert, so it burned the hero and the army.
- **A charger's glow** (Luminous or unique) is reset to the tile it stopped on. It had stayed half a tile on.
- **Checked and clean:**
  - steal potions and steal mana (a Barbarian's Rage is a separate pool);
  - Apocalypse;
  - Bloodstar;
  - scavengers and the corpse table;
  - variant lights;
  - Fallen fear;
  - Leoric's skeletons;
  - the Gargoyle lift.
