# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 90 (v1.12.339)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit 30a94caf.

Three read-only auditors ran:
- a regression review of v1.12.338;
- readied spells and bindings (1st pass);
- the Necromancer's curses (16th pass), which found nothing new.

## Bindings
- **An F-key bound to a scroll or a staff's charges** now switches to the learned spell instead of going dead.
  - Example: F3 was bound to Fireball scrolls. The hero then read the book and used the scrolls up. The key did nothing and gave no message, though its badge still showed. A reload fixed it, but only then.
  - Fix: `ToggleSpell` and the left F-key path now re-type the binding through `BindingTypeFor`, which is now exported.
- **An empty staff** drops its Charges binding.
  - Before: a staff of the same spell with no charges kept the binding, still drawn as usable, and every click said "I can't do that".
  - Fix: `CalcPlrStaff` now re-checks the readied buttons against the narrowed mask.
- **Checked and clean:**
  - 16-bit save and restore;
  - refunds and respecs;
  - scrolls emptied, moved, sorted or on pages 2-10;
  - Regular Attack on the right button;
  - aura F-keys;
  - right-button-only skills.

## Regression review of v1.12.338
- **A right click with an item in hand** now does nothing in a shop. On a shop without its painted art, the click fell through to the row buttons. There it armed the Repair cursor, which dropped the held item on the floor.
- **Debug `spawn` and `spawnu`** take a fresh monster-type slot. A second spawn of another type had taken the first one's slot.

## Curses (16th pass)
Clean. `CurseDamageTaken` is applied once per blow on every path. The other checks:
- Life Tap checks immunity before it heals.
- Iron Maiden cannot loop.
- Bosses are spared Dim Vision, Terror, Frailty and Confuse.
- Weaken and Decrepify are undone cleanly.
- Death Mark chains end on their own.

Two latent notes, Eternal Torment only:
- Decrepify's chill caps at 65,535 ticks.
- Releasing an Eternal Decrepify clears every chill.
