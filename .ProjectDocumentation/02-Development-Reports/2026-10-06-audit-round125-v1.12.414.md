## Fixed (P2)
1. **Round 124's throw-queue guard never fired.** The four disarms above it clear the throw latch too, so `ArmedJavelinThrow()` was always empty by the time it was asked, and a double-clicked throw still queued a plain melee swing. The guard is now asked first in the javelin branch. Weapon Throw, with its own latch, gets the same guard.

## Fixed (P3)
2. **A teleport keeps an action that finds its own way.** A queued attack, shot or spell survives; the walking ones go with the path. Round 124 dropped every queued action, so Turning Pike's mid-swing pivot lost the next click's attack.
3. **An unpaid hotkey bow spell's release** looses a plain arrow, as its comment says. Since round 124 it fired and paid for the held button's restored arrow skill on the same shot.

## For the user (design calls, Hellfire)
- **Lester's reward, the Auric Amulet, does nothing in single player.** Gold stacks are already at the 100M save limit. Its tooltip still says "Doubles gold capacity", and it cannot be salvaged, sold or crafted. A single-player effect, a new reward, or at least a corrected tooltip?

Farmer's Orchard pays Lester's reward only through the rune bomb, and Grave Matters stays open in the log for the whole game. A fix would complete them when the bomb or the map is picked up.
