# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 116 (v1.12.402)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 951 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks:**
- a regression review of v1.12.401;
- the hero's allies: companions, the Necromancer's minions, golems and converted monsters.

## Fixed: regressions in v1.12.401
1. **The pad's close sent the held item to the town stash (P2)** when backpack pages 2-10 had room: the put-away skipped the extra pages. With the stash full it fell to a rift's floor. The put-away now goes to the belt (potions), then page 1, then pages 2-10, then the stash. The same gap in `CloseStash` is closed too.
2. **The Rift Monument menu** backs out when a Cube or bench refused to close, as every other opener does.
3. **The waypoint list:** choosing an Act also leaves no row chosen, so Enter cannot travel by accident.
4. **The pad's close** also disarms the Cube's salvage hammer and the shop service cursors. The hammer stayed armed under a plain hand, so the next repair click salvaged instead.
5. **The class melee strike** passes the burst flag to `OnRfa12Hit`, so Sovereign Measure skips a spin's side blows too.

## Fixed: allies
6. **The XP-remaining counter** counted the four idle golem slots: plain bodies worth 2,000-4,000 XP each from Nightmare, so a cleared floor never read 0. They are skipped now, as in `quests.cpp`.
7. **Gamepad auto-targeting** no longer locks onto a converted monster, as the mouse already did not.
8. **Conversion and Confuse released at a level save** now re-aim the monster, as the live release does. Saved mid-swing at its pack, it struck a packmate on the revisit.

## For the user (design calls)
- **Companion blows pull aggro onto the hero.** A hard hit sets the struck monster's target to the hero; minion blows deliberately do not.
- **The Decoy and Spirit Guardian taunts hold bosses**: Diablo, uniques, Dread bosses and rift guardians. Conversion, Berserk and every stagger spare them.
- **A convert's kills pay no XP.** Army kills do.
- **Revived ranged and caster monsters fight in melee.**
- **The Necromancer's damage helpers would hit converts**, if skills ever cross classes.
- **Army of the Dead** is paid in town.
- **Hunters aimed at a withdrawn minion** step toward a stale slot once on a revisit, then correct.

## Checked and fine
- **The hero's damage paths** spare every ally kind.
- **Allies never fight each other** or the hero.
- **No reward from an ally's death:** no XP, kills, loot, rift credit or quest credit.
- **Town has no slots**, and every summon refuses there.
- **The slot and record lifecycle** is clean on death, a new game and a level change.
- **Timed life** ticks once a tick.
