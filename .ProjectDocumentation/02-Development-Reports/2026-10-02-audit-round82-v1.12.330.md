# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 82 (v1.12.330)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit 95dcd3de.

Three read-only auditors ran:
- a regression review of v1.12.329;
- the companions and the army as AI actors (15th pass);
- the hero's death and respawn (1st pass).

## Companions
- **Death:** companions now die with their master (`DismissCompanions` in `StartPlayerKill`, next to `DismissMinions`).
  - Before, the Ancients, Valkyrie, Decoy and Spirit Guardian fought on through the death animation, and their kills dropped loot and moved the rift bar.
  - They also stood beside the hero in town after a Respawn and re-formed on the next floor.
- **Return from the stairs:** a returning companion is never placed behind a wall (`SpawnInDungeon` needs sight of its anchor).
  - A Decoy or a Hold-stance guard has a leash of 1000, so it never regroups. Placed in the next room, it stayed there for its whole life.
- **Decoy and guard pulls:** they no longer reach through walls (`CompanionTauntTarget` needs a clear line).
  - Before, a monster in the next room dropped the hero and walked the long way round to the Decoy.
- **Reused slots:** a reused slot's regroup watchdog starts fresh.
  - A new skeleton in the slot of one that died past its leash was teleported beside the hero on its first think.
