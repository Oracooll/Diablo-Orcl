# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 77 (v1.12.325)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit 1015d02c.

Three read-only auditors ran:
- a regression review of v1.12.324;
- the Barbarian and Paladin (9th pass);
- rifts, arenas, variants and Torment (10th pass).

## Regression review of v1.12.324
- **A spin with nothing in front counted nothing.** v1.12.324 made every side blow a burst, so with the front tile empty or missed, no blow of the swing counted. Mythic Rhythm, Combination Strike and Hot Pursuit never moved. Now the first side blow that lands counts for the swing:
  - `SwingCounted` in rfa12_actives;
  - `counted` in the class spins.
- **Exploding Palm's death burst** is a burst whichever blow made the kill. From the front blow's kill it had counted each enemy it hit. `SideBlow` now restores the flag it found.
- **Reaping Point** never strikes a front monster that is walking into the tile beyond.

## Barbarian and Paladin
- **Aegis Slam** pays only for a side blow, a shove or a stun that happened. A lone boss shrugged off both shove and stun, and every swing paid anyway.
- **Doom Procession:** a converted ally or a companion does not put out its burning tiles.
- **Rage tooltip:** it says "Generates N Rage per blow that lands", which is how `SettleSkill` pays.
- **No change to Barbarian Rage:** v1.12.324's side-blow bursts did not alter it. Weapons Master and Righteousness pay before the burst return, and generator settles count landed blows.

## Endgame
- **A Sealed Map's arena** has no monster immune to fire, lightning and magic at once, as no rift guardian is. The Ember Vault's Obsidian Lords (boss and escort) on Hell and Torment were, so a caster could not earn the map's charm.
- **A Gilded kill's bonus drop** clamps its item level to the ladder top, 64. At the top tiers it rolled 65-66.

### Not changed, on purpose
The auditor also proposed demoting every all-three Hell row on Torment: Obsidian Lord, Soul Burner, Advocate, Arch Lich and Reaper. `OracoolAudit.TormentNeverLeavesAMonsterImmuneToEverything` pins the opposite: Torment is never softer than Hell, and an all-three row authored that way stays so. The change was made, failed that test, and was reverted. Only the arena got the demotion. This one is for the user to decide.
