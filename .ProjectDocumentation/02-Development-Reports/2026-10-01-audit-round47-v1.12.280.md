## Fixed

### Corpses on a revisited floor
The table (`oracool/corpses`) emptied on every level load, while the drawn bodies (`dCorpse`) were saved with the level, so Raise, Revive and the corpse explosions found nothing beside visible bodies.

- **Saved with the level:** the table is now written with the dungeon level, as a tagged tail at the end of `SaveLevel`'s non-town block ("CRP1", a count, then each entry with the dead monster's own numbers). It is read back after `dCorpse` in `LoadLevel`.
- **Restoring:** `RestoreCorpseTable` drops bodies no longer on the floor. A missing or short tail gives an empty table, never the last floor's.
- **Dead uniques:** `RestoreUniqueCorpsesAfterLoad` also clears the floor tiles of an unclaimed unique's body. It isn't drawn, and it must not be raised unseen.
- **Test:** `TheCorpseTableComesBackWithItsLevel`.

### Round 46 slips

### Passives
- **Haste bursts** each keep their own clock; the fastest one running counts. Hot Pursuit's +20% had kept Tactical Advantage's +40% alive for the whole fight.
- **A debuff's damage cut and armour cut** keep separate clocks. Resolve's every hit had kept Pressure Point's armour cut alive.
- **Static Field** takes at most its stated share. The damage passives had taken 20% to about 43%.
- **A weapon's fire or lightning burst** is melee to the passives. It had taken the spell passives and rolled Deadeye again per burst.
- **The still passives' counter** winds back by whole beats past its cap. Held there, they stopped after about 14 hours.
- **Hover wording:**
  - Sixth Sense names cold;
  - Mana Attunement says spell and arrow damage;
  - Ambush says "75% or more".
- **Comment:** the passive slots open at 10/20/30/40.
