# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 120 (v1.12.406)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 952 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks:**
- a regression review of v1.12.405 (clean: no P1 or P2);
- the out-of-combat item life cycle: tooltips and the card, identification, names, prices, durability, sockets, runewords and shards (clean: no P1 or P2).

## Fixed: tidy-ups from the review of v1.12.405
1. **No Lightning segment** is spawned on the closed door it stopped at (a cosmetic flash).
2. **A Town Portal scroll right-clicked in town** says "I can't cast that here" at once, instead of failing silently at the cast.
3. **The level-save aim test** restores the minion's `enemyPosition` too.

## Fixed: items (all P3)
4. **Removing a shard at the Mystic could raise maximum durability.** The strip floors the maximum at 1 and the restore adds Tempering back in full, so on a worn-down tempered item a cheap removal bought up to +60. The maximum now never ends above where it was.
5. **Decay no longer eats a broken or unusable weapon.** At -100 it deleted the weapon, sockets and all.
6. **The item card** no longer takes an unidentified magic ring's blue "Not Identified" for its subtitle.
7. **The Mystic's bench** accepts an unidentified white item. With Auto Identify off every drop is unidentified, and the bench refused plain gear that Cain will not even list.

## For the user (design calls)
- **White items' tooltips:** with Auto Identify off, an unidentified white item shows no Tier, Item Level or "basic X" line. The printer is chosen by "identified" alone; plain items have nothing to hide.
- **Required Level on unidentified items** counts the affixes and a unique's minimum, so it hints at quality (as in Diablo II).
- **Refinement's minimum:** its "at least one point" gives life and mana only 1/64.
- **Selling** ignores socketed stones and durability (as in Diablo II).
- **The card's stone bullets** can land on the wrong line when a Barbarian views a mana-only stone (cosmetic).

## Checked and fine
- **Prices:** no unidentified leak, no gold faucet, 64-bit or capped everywhere.
- **Repair:** refuses ethereal items, and Zod's stamp holds.
- **Identification** paths respect `_iIdentified`.
- **Sockets and runewords:** computed from the sockets every time, with no duplication on Free Sockets.
- **Shards:** limits enforced, and the ledger restored once per rebuild.
- **The card and the panel** print the same lines.
- **Names:** craft words stripped, and the name pool seeded.
