# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 113 (v1.12.399)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 951 tests pass (one new). The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks:**
- a regression review of v1.12.398;
- item affixes and special item effects against their tooltip lines.

## Fixed: regressions in v1.12.398
1. **Dead Ground's cooldown was started by bursts that never got its bonus (P2).** The Exploding Arrow ring and the Grenadier fireball lost the bonus in round 112 but still started the 6-second cooldown through `OnRfa12Hit`, as a weapon's fire or lightning burst always had. `OnRfa12Hit` now takes a burst flag and starts no cooldown on one.
2. **Weapons Master paid Rage on every Earthquake pulse:** about 20 Rage a quake on five foes, for a 10-Rage skill. It also paid on a cast blow's side targets. Bursts now pay none.
3. **A running Earthquake struck with a bow** taken up after the cast. Its pulses strike nothing while a bow is in hand.
4. **Conversion ties** went to the top-left monster. It now takes the monster under the cursor first, then the nearest, with ties going to the straighter line.
5. **Small fixes:**
   - The character sheet's attack-speed comment is corrected.
   - The refusal message now says "in your pack or stash".
   - **Test:** `OracoolAudit113.CastWeaponBlowsRefuseABowAndBowSkillsNeedOne`.

## Fixed: items
6. **Blood Craft on an "of the leech" Rare (P2)** added a second "hit steals 3% life" row. Steal percentages are flags, so the tooltip showed 3% twice and the hero stole 3%. A steal the item already has is no longer added; the craft's +life still lands.
7. **Thunderclap's lightning placeholder.** In single player, its Hellfire placeholder (flag set, 2 to 0) burst an unadvertised lightning blow on every hit. The weapon's lightning burst now needs a real lightning range.

## For the user (design calls)
- **Skill attacks and on-hit item powers (P2 if unintended).** Life and mana steal, Shadowhawk's random steal, triple damage to demons (Civerb's Cudgel, the Pul rune), Devastation, Jester's, Doppelganger and melee knockback are read only in `PlrHitMonst`, the plain swing.
   - **Who misses out:** every skill weapon blow skips them: Cleave, Whirlwind, Seismic Slam, the Monk's casts and the rest. So do bow skills, for triple damage to demons and knockback.
   - **The choice:** Diablo II leeches on skill attacks. Either move those powers into a shared helper both paths call, or say on the tooltips that they act on plain attacks only.
- **Acolyte's Amulet and Gladiator's Ring** fix their mana/life transfer at generation time (vanilla). A Barbarian, who runs on Rage, gets nothing from Acolyte's and loses life to Gladiator's, while both lines still show to him.
