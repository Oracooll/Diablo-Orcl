# 2026-09-20 - Audit pass 3: the rifts, Levski's Cube, level requirements, the portal loader and the save format (v1.12.063-064)

**Date:** 2026-09-20 - Debug only - green: 828/828 (build 42; 40 and 41 re-pinned two tests to the new rules). User: "run a few audits". Four read-only
audits on the day's work, each finding verified in the code before a fix; the fixes ranked by severity.

## The rifts (v1.12.063, commit ab886a2e)

- **CRASH** - a rift floor on any tileset but the Caves loaded no object sprites. `BuildRiftLevel` faked
  `currlevel` only around `CreateDungeon`; `InitObjectGFX` registers sprites by currlevel's range and
  `InitObjects` reads it too, so with the set-level id (9/10) in it only the Caves' range loaded and a
  Cathedral sarcophagus or a Hell torture rack was an unloaded sprite - `DrawObject` dereferences it. The
  generation floor now stays in currlevel through the whole build and through `CreateThemeRooms`.
- **HIGH** - after a death the gate glowed but had no portal: `AddStonegateObject` relit it during the town
  branch of `LoadGameLevel`, four lines before `InitMissiles` cleared the missile list, and the entry check
  lives in the portal missile. The relight now happens from the first town tick (`RelightStonegateIfNeeded`).
- **HIGH** - a revisit re-rolled the monster roster: `GetLevelMTypes` picked the five types with whatever RNG
  state the build left, which differs between a fresh build and a revisit, while `LoadLevel` restores each
  survivor's type INDEX. The pick is seeded from the rift now (`RiftRosterSeed`).
- **MEDIUM** - the guardian could not rise in sight: `CanPlaceMonster` refuses any tile the hero can see, so in
  a lit room nothing within twelve tiles passed. `SpawnRiftGuardian` uses its own test.
- **MEDIUM** - the way home was laid ON the corpse tile, where the pile and the keystone land; stepping there
  mid-pickup warped home and (with the old rule) ended the rift. The trigger goes on a free neighbour, and a
  cleared rift now ends only when the hero walks out through it, not on a death; a revisit re-lays the trigger
  and portal (neither is in the level save).
- **MEDIUM** - no arrival safe zone (a floor's stairs get one through their triggers): a 5x5 `Populated` ring
  around the landing tile.
- **LOW** - the HUD sat inside the event log's and the chat's frame under the mini-map: moved left of the map,
  bottom-aligned. The entry tile is validated with the gate's tile. The keystone's vendor value is 0.
- Not changed: theme rooms never get skeleton/goat variants on a rift (the types are picked after the
  themes); a save taken inside a rift cannot be resumed (V1 has no Continue).

## Levski's Cube (v1.12.064)

- **HIGH** - the Cube skin never loaded: `levski_cube_skin.h` spelled its three paths with ONE backslash, which
  the compiler eats ("uicube_bg.png"). The Cube wore the Roar's painting all of v1.12.057-063; the bezel list
  and the recess were dead code. Fixed, and a test now checks the three strings and the archive, as the sound
  paths are checked.
- **HIGH** - Griswold's Forge tab drew an empty stock grid: the tab column dispatches `StartStore(tab)`, and the
  window-opening case lived in `SmithEnter`, which no menu reaches. `StartStore` intercepts the tab now.
- **HIGH** - the Safety craft INCREASED damage taken: `SaveItemPower` subtracts IPL_GETHIT's roll, so the
  {-3,-1} range added 1-3. Now {1,3}. Blood's steal-life keys on param1 == 3 (a 4 or 5 was still 3%): now a
  fixed 3. Safety's resist-all moved onto its lowest table row (10-15).
- **MEDIUM** - Rejuvenation could eat six potions and then find no room: potions stack, so a consumed slot is
  free only when the stack is spent. The room question is asked on a copy that has paid.
- **MEDIUM** - the crafts rebuilt socketed and imbued items and lost the stones and shards: a plain wearable
  with sockets or imbuements is no longer a craft target (the tier recipes' own rule).
- **MEDIUM** - Unbind the Level was undone by Recast, Consecrate and Recolour, which rebuild through
  `InitializeItem`: `RebuildKeepsake` carries the flag now.
- **LOW** - the Cube's lid parted for Griswold's and Ogden's windows too: the Cube host only.

## Level requirements and experience (v1.12.064)

- **HIGH** - a new Barbarian could not wield his Spiked Club: the only starter base that is not IDROP_NEVER
  (drop level 4). The club is handed out unbound (the Work of Cathan flag), the one explicit exception.
- **MEDIUM** - vanilla's own level-gap factor (a tenth per level, ZERO ten levels down) still multiplied the
  kill before Diablo II's brakes, so a hero past 80 earned nothing from most of Hell and the table's last
  thirty levels were out of reach. The factor is gone; Diablo II's two brakes are the whole rule (and there is
  no bonus for a monster above the hero, as in Diablo II).
- **LOW** - the multiplayer cap indexed the table with level 99 (99 entries): clamped. The material floors
  matched the TRANSLATED name: the untranslated one now.
- Not changed, noted: a magic item's vanilla affix pair asks the lowest row of its power (the roll is not
  recorded for the pair; the 3/4 rule applies fully to Rare and Primal records) - a Roadmap note; Griswold's
  premium shelf can carry a base tier above the hero (drawn red, buyable, as vanilla); an old hero's
  experience surplus is clamped on the first tick (V1 New Game makes this moot).

## The portal loader and the save format (no change needed)

Every delivered PNG missile sheet measured against its `animFAmt`: only the two portal sheets have a count
other than 1 or 16, and both are 2-row sheets the new `rows = animFAmt` reads correctly (the old `1 : 16`
would have split them into 16 rows of 16 px without complaint). Dir 1's animation tables, the standing
`_mirange = 1` and the light release on every path were confirmed. Format 13 is uniform across every item
container (one SaveItem/LoadItemData pair, the record size counted); the shared stash and the tab files of
the previous format are rejected cleanly and rewritten empty, per the standing rule. The compact multiplayer
pack carries neither the keystone's tier nor the level-free flag (V1 reads the full record instead).
