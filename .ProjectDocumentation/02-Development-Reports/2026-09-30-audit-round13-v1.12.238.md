# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 13 (v1.12.238)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 13 ran six read-only tracks:
1. the debug console;
2. UI geometry: drawn rects against hit rects;
3. progression math;
4. the Sorcerer's, Rogue's, Monk's and Necromancer's passives;
5. the standing UI rules and keyboard reach;
6. monster population.

Every finding was verified against the code first. The UI changes are logic-level, and none were seen in play. **These
need a look in the game:** the plate's clear band over the world, and the eighth shop tab.

## Fixed: the HUD and windows

- **The plate's clear top was a dead zone over the world.** The plate art is 388x108, but its top 40 rows are
  transparent, and the belt run stays transparent down to the belt bar. The whole box answered as HUD, so a monster in
  that band could not be hovered, clicked or cast at. Only the painted part answers now, the XP bar included.
- **The HUD drawn over a docked shop, workshop or Cube page** now takes its own clicks. With an item on the cursor, a
  click on the LMB well over a shop page sold the item.
- **The companion and army headers** no longer pass clicks through. A click there dropped the held item.
- **Stash cells** are hit on their own 28px pitch. The first pixel row and column of each cell belonged to its
  neighbour.
- **Waypoint rows** answer only within their own width. A click on the scrollbar travelled.
- **Eight shop tabs fit** (gap 2 instead of 3). With every shelf on, the eighth tripped the Debug build's assert.
- **The event log's X** no longer answers from under the Crafting book. Opening the inventory, the Abilities window or
  the spellbook by key now closes the book.
- **Gillian's Imbue list scrolls.** The ledger holds up to 20 shards and the list showed 6, so rows 7-20 could not be
  picked for Remove.
- **The withdraw box:** Escape closes only the box, where it used to close the stash and the backpack too. The cells
  under the box no longer show their items.
- **The Refresh Until prompt** has a red X. A mouse user could not leave it.
- **The workshop's recipe wheel** answers only over the page.

## Fixed: passives

- **RfA-12 skills and the Sorcerer's two auras skipped every damage-dealt and on-hit passive.** This affected Power
  Hungry, Conflagration, Spreading Malediction, Paralysis and the element marks, among others. `Strike` (87 callers)
  and `AuraStrike` now apply them. The Sorcerer's and Necromancer's spells will hit harder with those passives slotted.
- **Commander of the Risen Dead** saved nothing, because the minimum-mana floor gave the saving back. The cut now
  applies after the floor. Chant of Resonance gets its full half as well.
- **"Monsters near" counted the hero's own minions, companions and converts.** Draw Life healed off the army, and an
  adjacent Spirit Guardian lit Determination.
- **Life from Death** counts every death within six tiles, including the army's kills.
- **Galvanizing Ward** halved every blow while Energy Shield absorbed them. It halves only the next blow now.
- **Resolve and Numbing Traps** weaken a monster's missiles as well as its blows.
- **Perfectionist, Glass Cannon, Brace and River Stance** give armour as a percentage, as their texts say. They were
  flat points.
- **Paralysis** spares champions and Diablo, as every stagger in the fork does.
- **Grenadier's text** now says "every fourth shot".

## Fixed: progression

- **The Glowing Shrine** never takes experience below the start of the hero's own level. At level 97 it took 55% of a
  level and left the hero 148 million under his level's floor.
- **A book the Rule of Rangs refuses** is drawn red.
- **A "+" click that hits the 255 cap** keeps its points. Two clicks in one tick had spent points that were never
  applied.

## Fixed: population

- **Theme rooms get their monsters.** At the default densities the scatter filled to the cap, and every theme room
  shared the last ten slots.
- **A rift's arrival is out of sight**, as a floor's stairs are. Packs landed three tiles from the hero.
- **Rift champions** can now be the uniques native to floors 9 and 10.
- **A Skeleton King rift's skeletons** are raised by the King only, not scattered from floor 1-6 types.
- **Waypoint arrival** looks for a spot 15 tiles clear, a stair's sight radius. It was 8.

## Fixed: the debug console

- `givequest -1` and `questinfo -1` read and wrote before the quest array.
- `restart 5 x` threw and ended the game.
- `restart` on the current level wiped the level and then refused.
- `setspells 0` left every book spell known at level 0.
- `rift guardian N` is clamped to 1-255.
- `dropu` cleared the game's record of found uniques.
- Debug set pieces now get a seed, an item level and a base tier.
- The `givewp` help now says 1-24.

## Not changed (open)

- **Button feel** (the press/release default): the Abilities window, the red X everywhere, the skill picker, the HUD
  Menu popup and the Crafting book's buttons act on the press. Several buttons also lack the 2px sink and the hover
  sound: the stash's, the runeword filters and the workshop X. This is a UI pass of its own, left for the user to
  schedule.
- **Keyboard reach in game** (rule 1 was written for the front end): the Abilities window, the skill picker, the
  Crafting book, the waypoint list, the Cube, the workshop, stash paging and shop tabs have no keys. **The user's
  call.**
- **The skill-points frame covers the bottom-left backpack cell** at 960x720, and the level-up icon covers the stash's
  last cell. The user asked on 2026-08-28 for these to stay visible over windows, so moving them is **the user's
  call.**
- **Glass Cannon's +15%** reaches weapon damage only, so a Sorcerer's spells get nothing.
- **Search has a book but no band**, which retires the Monk's Inner Sight and Spirit Ward rows. A design call.
- **Valkyrie arrows feed the Rogue's on-hit passives.** This may be intended.
- **Serration measures from the hero, not the flight.**
- **Minor:**
  - The burger menu and the skill picker anchor 40px above the painted plate.
  - The plateless row floats up when plate art is off.
  - The shop refusal banner overlaps the service buttons.
  - "Game Saved" sits over the speed readout.
  - The recipe list takes clicks on its pads.
  - The workshop grid's 1px seams are dead.
  - The debug `spawn` command's type slot.
  - Several other debug help texts.
  - `talkto` rerolls the town's stock.
