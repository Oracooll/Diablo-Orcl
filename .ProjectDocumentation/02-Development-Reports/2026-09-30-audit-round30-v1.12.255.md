# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 30 (v1.12.255)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 30 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.254;
2. Paladin and Barbarian skill hovers against their effects;
3. monster variants, champion affixes, pack auras and Dread bosses (third pass);
4. quests end to end (second pass);
5. the standing window rules and keyboard reach (second pass);
6. level generation (third pass).

Every finding was verified against the code first.

## Fixed: regressions of round 29

- **P2: the one-item sale recalculates the hero** (the Sell list's Confirm and Sell All both come through it). v1.12.254
  added the recalculation to Sell All only, and its comment claimed every other sale already did it.
- **P2: a melee RfA-12 skill blow runs the shared on-hit rules once.** It reached them through the melee passives and again
  through the spell hook, so Momentum's three empowered blows went two at a time on a Leaping Crane or Exploding Palm.
- **Respawn sets the 1 life after the totals,** so a recalculation made on the death screen (a buff expiring while dead)
  can no longer leave the lit aura's life out and then add it on top.
- **The Mystic's pick takes the very item its line was printed from,** so a new King's row keeps the to-hit it showed.
- **Zod's stamp** counts as indestructible for the durability rows, as the tooltips already treat it.

## Fixed: Paladin and Barbarian texts

The mechanics are signed off; only the words changed, to match the code:
- **Shout, Divine Fortress, Aura of Protection and Tough as Nails** add flat armour; they said "%". Shout's hover line too.
- **Inspiring Presence** names the three warcries it doubles (Shout, Battle Orders, Battle Command).
- **Weapon Throw** flies as an arrow: the weapon's damage and half the Strength bonus, not "full damage".
- **Stun** (+0.2 s a rank), **Howl** (farther with every rank), **Conviction** (fire, lightning and magic), **Vigilant** and
  **Battle Hardened** (everything that is not steel, cold included), **Endurance** (base life), **Throwing Mastery** (all
  non-melee damage).

## Fixed: windows

- **The books, the skill picker and the HUD menu close on a level change.** A click beside the 944-wide runeword book
  reached the stairs, and the book was still open on the next floor.
- **The mouse wheel** scrolls the skill picker and the stash only with the cursor over them, and never zooms the world
  through a window it had nothing to scroll in (the Cube, the workshop, the inventory).
- **The stash chest and Levski's Cube close when the hero walks off,** three tiles as at every counter. Both stayed open
  across town, and items moved in and out from anywhere.
- **The Cube closes the left panels** its pages cover: the hero sheet stayed open under it, took the wheel, and reappeared.
- **Typing a chat message closes the skill picker,** which was hidden under chat but still took clicks.

## Fixed: monsters

- **Shove** leaves an Unyielding monster alone and restores the facing when a wall refuses the move: it slid and faced the
  wrong way.
- **Berserk** clamps the berserked monster's damage to the byte: Hell and Torment damage over 212 wrapped to almost nothing.
- **A Luminous skeleton rising from a sarcophagus or barrel** brings its light: it rose dark, its light in the map corner.

## Fixed: level generation

- **P2: a Skeleton King rift** no longer grows low-level skeleton champions and their escorts. His rift loads a floor 1-6
  skeleton for him to raise, and eight of those types carry a champion row; in a rift, champions come only from the types
  the level scatters.
- **The way home's fallback** keeps off the loot pile, as the search before it does.

## Fixed: quests

- **The Defiler killed first** (a waypoint kept from an earlier game) no longer taunts on 17 and 19, and his quest no longer
  reopens in the log.
- **The quest log's finished list** shows only quests of this game: in a cow game, the Farmer is set done to open the Hive,
  and Farmer's Orchard was listed as finished.
- **The Chamber of Bone's +1 Guardian** obeys the book rank rule; every new game added one more, up to 30.
- **Guaranteed quest drops make room** on a full floor: Zhar's book, Gharbad's gifts, Na-Krul's four items and the Slain
  Hero's reward, as round 15 did for the rest.

## Not changed (open)

- **The red X** neither sinks nor waits for the release on most windows. This is the parked press/release pass.
- **Esc** closes every open window at once, not the top one. **Death** leaves Advanced Stats' underlying windows open behind
  the death screen.
- **The gold and stack-split prompt** has no red X and no mouse way out.
- **The shops take the wheel** from anywhere while a vendor tab is open.
- **Leap Attack** still pays twice (to leap, then for the blow). **Inspiring Presence's doubling** reaches only the three old
  warcries, and Throwing Mastery does not reach War Cry.
- **The Thunderous champion's burst** hits the hero's army and companions and reads as "a trap". **Devouring** drains from
  the melee blow before cold resistance. Monster traits and armour do not apply against the army's melee (vanilla).
- **Rifts** never roll Skeleton Rooms or Goat Shrines (the roster loads after the themes). A theme room can fill the landing
  room. The guardian's spawn spot has no reachability check.
- **A waypoint arrival** has no monster-free zone, and the sigil can sit on floor loot.
- **A quest item dropped in a rift or an arena** is gone for the game; the Staff of Lazarus that way seals level 16 until the
  next game. Refusing the drop there, or returning such items when the rift closes, is the user's call.
- **Quest log "Reveal All"** plays Diablo's line meant for after Lazarus, and four class-voiced quests play the Warrior's
  voice until their level is built. **Towners** answer a quest the hero is already ahead of one click late (vanilla).
