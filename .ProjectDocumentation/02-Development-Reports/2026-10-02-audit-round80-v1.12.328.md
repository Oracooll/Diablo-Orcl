# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 80 (v1.12.328)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit e8e76440.

Three read-only auditors ran:
- a regression review of v1.12.327;
- the Rogue's bow skills and arrows (12th pass);
- dungeon objects: shrines, chests, barrels, doors and traps (1st pass).

## Objects
- **Flame-hole trap and exploding barrel:** both now spare the hero's minions and companions. They called `MonsterTrapHit` directly, so round 35's rule for trap missiles never reached them. Skeletons standing on a flame hole burned to death, and barrel chains staggered the army.
- **Quest spawners on a full floor:** these now make room for their guaranteed reward instead of doing nothing, with no text and no quest start:
  - the Blood, Blind and Steel books;
  - the Lazarus stand;
  - the mushroom patch;
  - the inn-sign chest;
  - the pedestal. It makes room before it takes the Blood Stone.

## Rogue
- **Thrown weapons:** a non-Rogue's thrown weapon takes its full stat share. The arrow path halved the share for every non-Rogue arrow. The sheet halves it only on a bow, and a thrown axe flies as an arrow. The elemental arrow lines follow the same rule.
- **Exploding Arrow's ring:** it is part of the arrow that caused it (`ArrowBurstInFlight`). Each monster in the ring had paid Night Stalker again, rolled Sharpshooter and Deadeye again, and fired Archery and Thrill again, which made up to nine arrows from one.
- **Freezing Arrow's freeze:** it skips cold-immune and unhittable monsters.
- **Shock Arrow's arc:** it is no second arrow (`BowStrikeScope { false }`).
- **Barbed Shaft:** it does not bleed the monster it killed. That monster's slot could pass the bleed to its next occupant.

## Regression review of v1.12.327
- **Bone Wall on a pillar:** a centre clamped onto a pillar steps back to the last open tile instead of refusing a clear room.
- **Bone Wall diagonal arms:** a diagonal arm does not squeeze between corner-touching walls.

## Left open
- **Closed doors and bow skills (unconfirmed):** area bow skills may see and strike through a closed door. Their sight test is `LineClearMissile`, which reads the tile's missile-blocking flag, and arrows also stop at objects that block missiles. A unit test with a closed door would settle it.
- **Torment all-three immunity:** the user's call.
- **Press-time controls:** waiting for the press/release pass.
- **Workshop wells:** an empty transmute plays the success sound.
- **Tint table:** it is rebuilt every frame.
- **Guardian Rift keystone:** whether to refund it.
- **Earlier rounds:** the items listed in the round 75 report.
