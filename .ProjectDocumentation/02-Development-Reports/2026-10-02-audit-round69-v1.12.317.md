# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 69 (v1.12.317)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit f2ce8ec3.

Four read-only auditors ran: a regression review of v1.12.315-316, stores and town (5th pass), class passives (5th) with the HUD and options (7th).

## Regression review of v1.12.315-316
- **CRASH, from v1.12.316:** reading a scroll from backpack pages 2-10 ended the game. Round 68's page test sat on the backpack branch of `ConsumeScroll`'s if/else chain, so a page-2 read fell through to the fatal "invalid item index". The test is now inside the branch.
- **Stale scroll binding, from v1.12.316:** a readied scroll's binding, kept while the scroll is in hand, went stale once the scroll left the hand for the floor, the stash or a bench, and a click then cast nothing. `ProcessRfa12ActivesTick` now asks `EnsureValidReadiedSpell` each tick while a scroll is readied.
- **Perched gargoyle:** a swing that lifts one carries no class skill either (`LiftedThisSwing`). Fend and Wheel of Heaven had gathered the just-lifted gargoyle into their spin, struck it and paid.
- **Absolute Zero:** the vortex's light is freed when its art is ended early.

## Passives and HUD
- **Weapon cleave side blows:** they now run the on-hit passives (Leech, Dark Reaping, Righteousness, Weapons Master's Rage, Life Tap), as the RfA-12 side blows have since round 65.
- **Empty slots:** passive and tree slots draw their plate in its tint. Nothing had been drawn, so an open slot read as a locked one.
- **Asset reads:** fixed-grid blits clamp their source rect to the PNG (`ClampToAsset`), so a smaller or recut asset can't be read past its buffer.
- **Book rows at spell level 0:** an item can bring a book spell to level 0. Such a row is not usable: no blue, no "0" badge.
- **Debug art reload:** it also drops the loose PNGs.

## Stores and town
- **Griswold's Sell plate:** an item dropped on it sells.
- **Ogden's gem, rune and jewel steps:** with nowhere to put the result, nothing is taken. Before, the stones went and the board said "nowhere to put it".
- **Gillian's reroll:** charged only when the wheel has something new on it. Before, a slot whose pool was used up paid for four copies of its own affix, and doubled the next price.
- **The crafting book's three buttons:** they act on the release inside them (`ReleaseCraftingMenuButton`).

## Left open
- **Shop control row without art:** with the painted canvas missing, the shop's fallback control row still acts on the press.
- **Abilities window:** no keyboard or pad focus.
- **v1.12.315 frames:** the frames may lie a few pixels over Griswold's painted bands. This needs a screenshot.
- **Light pool:** a Blizzard plus a Meteor shower can run out the 64-light pool. That fails safely: some later lights stay dark.
- **Meteor impact burst:** it now takes about 1 s rather than 0.5 s, since the doubled frames play one per tick. The user may want it faster.
