# 2026-10-01 - Audit round 45 (v1.12.278)

**Date:** 2026-10-01. Debug only.

**Tracks:**
- regression review of v1.12.277;
- item generation and the affix engine (5th);
- Rogue / Monk / Sorcerer actives (6th);
- HUD and rendering (4th).

## Fixed

### Regressions of round 44
- **Monster drains:** they read `LastPlayerLifeLost`, the life `ApplyPlrDamage` took before any heal it set off. Mercy or a cheat-death save inside the call made the drain read about 0.

### Actives
- **Line skills on the hero's own tile** go the way he faces (`CastFacing`, set for the cast). They had struck South while turned South-West: Long Thrust, Heaven Splitter, Dragon's Wrath, Piercing Shot, Harpoon, the javelins.
- **`LastClearTileToward`:** one straight-line walk to the last open tile (Blight's) now places:
  - Plague Javelin's cloud, which landed in the next room;
  - Vault, Leaping Crane, Shoulder Gate and Heaven's Descent, which moved through walls, as Ride the Lightning was fixed in round 40.
- **The sight gate:**
  - refuses a wall tile as a placed target (a sentinel or mine set there was a dud);
  - now covers Rain of Arrows, Wave of Light, Valkyrie's Spear and Seven-Sided Strike, whose art and cue landed in the next room on a paid cast.
- **Frozen Sentinel** targets only what it can see from its own tile.

### Items
- **Cube crafts** that join a Rare's row of the same kind add only what fits under the item-level ceiling. +15-25 life on +60 made a level-60 row on a level-30 item.
- **A staff reworked at Gillian** takes its charges without the old Plentiful/Bountiful multiplier and with the new one's.

### Rendering
- **The countdown column** stops above the mana orb and continues in a second column to its left.
- **The Cube's live grid** reuses one scratch surface; it had created one per item per frame.

## Open, or the user's call
- **Seed-changing fixes (the user's call, as the earlier unique-order change was):**
  - a unique's level is checked against the roll level, not the stamped item level, so chests and unique monsters can roll uniques above the item level;
  - curse rows coexist with positive rows of the same stat family (only exact twins are paired).
- **Two fields on one tile** share their standing art; the first to end hides the other. This needs per-field art tags.
- **Storm Crucible** strikes an 8-way ray, not the true line between its conductors.
- **The 32-field cap** overwrites a field but leaves its art.
- **Hover texts** of the Monk's stuns and knockbacks omit "uniques shrug it off".
- **Smaller item gaps:**
  - a reworked staff's price loses its spell's share;
  - rerolled uniques lose their oils.
- **Rendering perf:** `TintedTable` rebuilds per tinted missile per frame (under a millisecond); a unique's tinted or fading missile would skip its TRN (none ship today).
- **Still awaiting the user:**
  - the 255 cap redesign;
  - spells past level 30;
  - scrolls and charges at a fixed level;
  - passives and +skills;
  - Barbarian mana potions;
  - Murphy's Shrine;
  - keystone tiers across difficulties;
  - the Hive and Crypt quest texts.
