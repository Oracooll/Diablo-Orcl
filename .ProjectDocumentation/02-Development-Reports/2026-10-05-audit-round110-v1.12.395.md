# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 110 (v1.12.395)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 950 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks:**
- a regression review of v1.12.394;
- the Sorcerer's and the Monk's skill effects against their hover text: cold, lightning and fire, staff, fists, spirit, and both passive pages.

## Fixed: the reward keystone
1. **Round 109's rule lost the reward (P2).** Held on the cursor, the reward counted as kept everywhere. It was settled within a second, so an exit with a full pack and stash, or a death with no room, lost it with no refund. Before, the debt stayed and the next game refunded it. The cursor is never saved, so that was no duplicate.
   - A held reward now counts as kept only on a level change, the one place round 109's duplicate came from (`SettleRiftRewardKeystone(player, heldCounts)`).
   - The settle at death is removed.
   - **Test:** `OracoolAudit108` checks both rules.
2. **A test leaked state.** The chunk test left an owed tier on the shared `Players[0]`; it now clears it.

## Fixed: Sorcerer
3. **Thunder Storm (P2)** picked its bolt's target before the immunity test, so pulses were wasted on lightning-immune or converted monsters. They are now left out of the pick, as round 96 did for the RfA-12 strikes.
4. **Cinder Touch** charged full price for a burn on a fire-immune monster. Such a monster no longer counts as struck.
5. **Ice Blast, Glacial Spike, Frozen Armor** now also test the lesser affix before freezing. *Correction (round 112):* defensive only. Every lesser unique already counts as unique (`PrepareUniqueMonst` sets `uniqueType`), so none was ever frozen solid.
6. **Immolate's text** said "nine times"; the code burns ten.
7. **The active skill hover** showed rank 1 where gear with minus spell levels (Bovine Plate) cast a one-point skill at level 0. It now shows the level the cast uses.

## Fixed: Monk
8. **Temple Bell (P2)** was paid when no undead heard it: every monster in earshot counted. Now only the undead it strikes count (round 55's rule for Howl and Taunt).
9. **Tranquility** healed once less than its seconds. It now uses the Immolate rule `(ticks + 1) % 20`.
10. **Open Palm and Bash (P2)** pushed along the monster's own facing: a side-walker slid sideways, and one facing away was pushed into the Monk. The monster is now turned to face the hero first and restored if the push is refused, as `Shove` does.
11. **Rearward Reach (P2)** fizzled with a mouse. The cast turns the hero toward the cursor, so clicking the foe behind him struck the empty tile behind his new facing. It now strikes the clicked foe when that foe stands beside him.
12. **The cast weapon blows** now count as melee blows, as Leaping Crane's and Exploding Palm's already did: Long Thrust, Rearward Reach, Mountain Pole, Bamboo Rain, Dragon Tail Sweep and Whirling Kick, Heaven Splitter, Thousand Reeds, Seven-Sided Strike, Dragon's Wrath. They took Mythic Rhythm's "spells and arrows" bonus and missed Counterstroke, Combination Strike and the leech passives.
13. **The same casts and Leaping Crane** are refused with a bow in hand. They struck with the bow's damage, while the swung skills refuse a bow since round 19.
14. **Hundred Fists and Pressure Point** were free when the first blow killed. A landed blow now pays.
15. **Staff of Echoes:** the echo no longer strikes across a wall corner or door.
16. **Text corrections:**
   - Inner Sight reveals items on the ground, not objects and traps.
   - Spirit Ward is no longer "against magic" only.
   - Perfect Vessel no longer claims to empower the Body skills.
   - Sixth Sense lists acid.
   - Paralysis says "not uniques or bosses".
   - The `passives.h` reset documentation is corrected.

## Fixed: attack speed (P2)
17. A skill's fast-attack flag (Alacrity, Rhythm, Fanaticism, Weapons Master, Swift Harvesting) on a hero with a Fastest Attack item made the swing **slower**. Vanilla's frame skipper reads Fastest together with Quick or Fast as fewer skipped frames. Fastest now wins outright.

## Reported, not coded (design calls)
- **Bleeds merge as two separate maximums**, so a short strong bleed and a long weak one give the strong rate for the long time (Tiger Claw then Exploding Palm).
- **Iron Robe** adds +2 armour a level on top of the engine's built-in +2 a level for an unarmoured Monk.
- **Iron Fist's level-up damage** applies with any weapon, while the passive itself needs unarmed or a staff.
- **Temple Bell, War Cry, Sound Shock:** the cries' damage takes none of the damage passives (Seize the Initiative and others).
- **Paid with nothing to act on:** Serenity, the mantras and Astral Projection are paid on a recast at full duration (the cries refuse one), and Flame Ring, Ashen Brand, Chill Touch, Ice Needle and Ice Lance are paid on a whiff.
- **Lightning Ball, Lightning Rod, Faraday Ring:** their strike damage does not grow with rank.
- **Arcane Dynamo** misses the instant spell that triggers it.
- **Seven Reeds and Hundred Fists** need ranks from items to reach seven blows.
- **Running buffs** outlive Adria's respec at their old rank.
- **Guardian's Path:** the hover's staff condition sits above the slip line.
