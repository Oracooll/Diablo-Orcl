# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 66 (v1.12.313)

**Date:** night of 2026-10-01/02, auditing non-stop at the user's word. Debug only. 903 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` changed during the run because the game was open (the user's own save; Keymapping and Width intact). Commit f8ab12d7. oracool.mpq NOT repacked: the open game holds it; the rebuilt Ember Mine sheet waits for the next repack.

Five read-only auditors ran: a regression review of v1.12.310-311 (the lightning family) and of v1.12.312 (the fire redesign), Paladin+Barbarian 6th, Necromancer 11th and item tooltips 4th. Every finding was checked against the code before any change.

## Regressions caught in v1.12.310-312
- **Sheets drawn 64px too low:** Holy Lance, Long Thrust, Reaping Point and Chill Touch shared the anchor case that v1.12.312 changed for Furnace Mouth. They have their own case again, and the Lightning Rod's anchor no longer captures the body-overlay group above it.
- **Strikes one tick short:** they are laid during the players' turn, and `ProcessCensusEffect` took a tick before the first draw. So 2-tick strikes showed once at half strength, and Storm Crucible's arc blinked off every third tick. `AddLightningStrike` adds the tick back.
- **Light pool:** a strike's body light is kept for arcs only (the fork already lights the hit), and a meteor's impact light is radius 4 instead of 8.
- **Lights left behind:** the Static Charge/Conduit buff light was dropped on death and on a new game without being freed. It is now freed first.
- **Fields:** `MaxFields` went from 64 to 128, because four quick Meteor showers could evict a live field, such as a running Lightning Clone. The Lightning Clone's field no longer shows a countdown.
- **Meteor shower:**
  - Each rock's countdown length is now the cast's, so late rocks no longer put a timer by the mini-map.
  - Rocks land only in the cursor's sight, so they no longer hit the next room.
  - The text says the rocks fall over the next two seconds.
- **Funeral Spiral:**
  - It is no longer refused for a cursor past a wall (it fires from her).
  - Each fireball keeps its animation frame through a turn.
  - The balls end with the field.
  - Without the Fireball sheet, it strikes around her with the ring.
- **Skill sounds:**
  - The three picks of 2026-10-01 are recorded in `tools/skill_sound_card_picks.json` and `skill_sounds_data.inc` is regenerated. The hand edits were lost on regeneration, and the file's CRLF had been broken.
  - The generator's rules match the new names (Flame Wave, Funeral Spiral, Lightning Clone).
- **Ember Mine sheet:** rebuilt without the double premultiply, which left a bright fringe.

## Paladin and Barbarian
- **Cleave:** an axe or two-handed Barbarian's Cleave struck each side enemy twice. The "skill already swept" guard bracketed only the Monk's condition.
- **Whirlwind:** started on exactly its 5 Rage, it struck once and stopped. The first second is paid, so empty Rage no longer stops it within that second.
- **Holy Lance:** no longer pays for magic-immune targets.
- **Zeal:** its accuracy needs a Zeal that can be paid for. The `ZealToHitLadder` test now gives the Paladin mana and checks the 0-mana case.
- **Battle Command:** a recast while it is nearly full is refused. Its own +1 had made every recast look like another rank.
- **Leap Attack:** its prepaid blow is forgotten on a level change and on death.

## Necromancer
- **Leoric raising the army's skeleton kind:** a skeleton type loaded mid-level only for a minion (`AddMinionBody`) is marked `CMonster::minionOnly`. Neither `AddSkeleton` nor a placed Skeleton King's minion pick takes it. Before, Leoric could raise the army's own kind as enemies, and a level saved with one held a type index the next visit never rebuilds.
- **Command the Dead:** refused with no living army or in Passive stance.
- **Frenzy of the Dead:** counts living bodies, not the records still waiting in town (`LivingMinionCount`).
- **Grisly Tribute:** also heals from the Fire Golem's burn and the golems' thorns.
- **Bone Prison's tooltip:** says uniques shrug the hold off.

## Item tooltips
- **Steal lines:** print each row's own roll. A crafted 3% beside a rolled 5% says "(the 5% applies)". Test: `Item.StealLinesPrintTheirOwnRoll`.
- **Store lists:** no longer call every ring and amulet "Indestructible".
- **Runeword cards:** keep their type line (it is in the socketed grey, not the gold title colour).
- **Magic-find charm:** its line says "+N%".
- **Unidentified items:** show their sockets and stones, shards, ethereal state and breakage, which apply either way.

## Left open
- **Oil of Accuracy/Mastery to-hit:** it is applied but never printed, and it swells a to-hit+damage affix's line. Fixing it needs its own stored field (an item format bump). It is planned as its own change.
- **Gargoyle lift:** a blow that lifts a perched gargoyle still counts as a landed hit for Rage and passives (minor).
- **Rage spenders:** one can be charged at settlement after Rage arrived mid-swing (Bloodcall or Weapons Master on the front blow) without carrying the bonus. It needs the front blow's latch carried to settlement.
- **Zeal:** a missed first swing loses its burst. This may be by design; Double Swing and Frenzy arm "landed or not".
- **Shared tile:** a Lightning Rod and a Storm Crucible conductor on one tile end each other's art (edge case).
