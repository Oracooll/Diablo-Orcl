# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 108 (v1.12.392)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 950 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks (two):**
- a regression review of v1.12.391;
- hero death, respawn, town portals and level changes.

## Fixed: the reward keystone, two duplication paths
1. **The reward dropped on the town floor (P1).** The settle looks only in the pack, the tabs, the belt and the stash. With the reward lying on the town floor (or in the open Cube grid or on the workshop bench), using any spare keystone fell through to the refund, which made a second one. Round 107's cursor refusal could never fire in play: a keystone cannot be used while an item is held.
   - **The fix:** `RewardKeystoneStillReachable` refuses a Guardian Rift while the owed reward is on the cursor or this floor, or while the Cube or the workshop is open. The message is "Pick up the keystone your last rift left you first."
2. **The reward carried to another floor on the cursor (P3, gamepad).** On a pad, a hero can walk out of the rift and down the stairs holding the reward and drop it on level 1, outside every check. Every level change (`InitLevelChange`) now puts the owed reward from the cursor into the pack or the stash and settles it. With no room anywhere it stays held, and the refusal still sees it.
   - **Test:** `OracoolAudit108.TheRewardKeystoneOnTheFloorIsStillWithinReach`.

## Fixed: cost
3. **The guardian's spot search.** Once the 32 path tries are spent, it no longer traces a sight line for every remaining tile. It also traces sight once per tile, not twice.

## Checked and fine
- **Death:** single player drops nothing; a held item is put away; minions and companions are dismissed.
- **Respawn:** Rage, Essence, Cold Armour, healing, cries, buffs and timed missiles are reset. Elixirs survive death on purpose.
- **New game:** every new static resets (the reward debt, the boss run, the bow, Strafe and javelin latches).
- **Rift bosses:** the conjured and raised lists are read only inside the rift and reset on a rebuild; a revisit keeps the same monster slots.
- **Rift portals:** every town portal into an ended rift is closed.
- **Autosave:** it waits while the hero is dead, mid level change, holding an item, or in the Cube or the workshop.
- **Windows:** every Orcl window is closed on a level change.
- **The Cube rollback and the gamble price (v1.12.391):** sound. No recipe changes anything outside the grid before an empty return, and a gamble slot's type always matches its base.
