# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 74 (v1.12.322)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit 016134d3.

Three read-only auditors ran:
- a regression review of v1.12.321, which found no regressions;
- Levski's Cube, the workshops and the stash;
- item stats, affixes, sockets and charms (6th pass).

## Cube and salvage
- **Free the Sockets on an ethereal item holding Zod:** it now frees every stone but Zod.
  - Before, one Zod refused the whole recipe, so the gems and runes beside it were locked in for good.
  - The Cube also no longer stops at such a host when a second socketed item stands behind it in the grid.
- **Salvage** never eats Imbuement Shards. This covers the plates, the hammer ("Its shards would be lost - Cleanse it first.") and the salvage charm on pickup. Filled sockets were protected; shards were not.
- **Tier recipes and crafts** take only an item with an affix pool (`ItemTakesAffixes`). The Auric Amulet showed the recipe ready, then refused, and jammed every item behind it in the grid.

## Items
- **Tempering shards near the cap:** a shard whose durability did not fit under the cap after a rebuild comes off the ledger. Before, "Tempering x2" stayed on with none of its +20, and a later Cleanse took 20 the shards never gave.
- **Locked backpack pages:** a page the hero cannot open carries no live charm.

## Drops
- **A refilled hand:** a dropped item put back while the hand was refilled meanwhile goes to the nearest free floor tile. Before, it was lost.

## Tooling
- **`ItemTakesAffixes`:** its first two placements landed inside `items.cpp`'s anonymous namespace (2046-3715) and failed to link. It now sits after that namespace.

## Left open
- **Workshop wells:** an empty transmute result at Gillian's and Ogden's wells still plays the success sound and blanks the board.
- **Extra blows:** the RfA-12 and class-skill extra blows each count as a swing for Cadence, Counterstroke and the combo counters.
- **Guardian Rift keystone:** whether to refund it on exit from an unfinished rift is the user's call.
