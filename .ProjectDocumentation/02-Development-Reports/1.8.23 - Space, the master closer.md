# 1.8.23 - Space, the master closer

## The rule

> "also a game-wide rule - space bar to close any and all windows regardless of when they were
> introduced in the game. space bar should supersede everything by design and act as master windows
> closer."

The companion to the red X shipped in 1.8.22: the X closes one window, space closes all of them.

## What space did before

Two things, neither of them this. A keymapper action, **Hide Info Screens**, defaulted to SPACE and
closed the side panels, help, chat log, the spell list and the automap - but not the stash, not the
event log, not Levski's Roar, not the gold prompts. And a hardcoded `case SDLK_SPACE` further down
`PressKey` that closed the event log only. Two partial closers, neither of which knew about the
other, and both of which would have gone on missing every window added after them.

## What it does now

`CloseAllWindows()` in `diablo.cpp`, sitting directly beneath `ClosePanels()` and calling it as its
first line. Deliberately a **superset** rather than a rename: `ClosePanels()` is called from a dozen
places that mean "put the side panels away" - entering a store, taking a waypoint, starting a
cutscene - and none of those should also be dismissing the help screen or the automap. The new
function means what the player means when they hit space.

Beyond `ClosePanels()` it closes: the stash, the gold-withdraw and gold-split prompts, Levski's Roar,
the event log, any open store, help, the chat log, the spell list, quest text, an active chat line,
the automap, the current message, and the Doom overlay.

Space is now **reserved outright**, intercepted in `PressKey` ahead of
`sgOptions.Keymapper.KeyPressed(vkey)` on exactly the terms F1-F12 already are - so no ini row can
take it, including the Hide Info Screens default a settled install still carries. That action stays
registered and rebindable to some other key; it is simply unreachable on space, and it was a strict
subset of the new behaviour anyway. It is swallowed on the release edge too, the same fix the F12
double-screenshot needed.

Everything that legitimately wants a literal space still gets one, because it has already returned
before the interception: chat typing is consumed by `control_presskeys`, and the gold-amount and
Refresh Until prompts by their own key handlers.

## Two deliberate exceptions

- **Stores** go out through `StoreESC()`, the same path Escape uses, so a nested store menu steps
  back one level rather than being torn down from outside. Space in a store behaves exactly as
  Escape does there, which is the behaviour that has been tested for years.
- **Levski's Roar can refuse.** With a full backpack `CloseLevskiRoar()` logs *"Your pack is full -
  Levski's Roar keeps what it holds"* and stays open, because its grid is not save state and closing
  would destroy the contents. This is the one window space cannot force, by design rather than by
  omission.

## Verification

Debug build clean. **454 tests, the usual two** (`Drlg_l1.CreateL5Dungeon_diablo_3_844660068`,
`Timedemo.WarriorLevel1to2`). Run the suite serially - under `-j` the loadsave tests collide over a
shared save path and produce a phantom third failure.

## To look at in game

1. Open several windows at once - stash plus character sheet plus the event log - and press space.
   All of them go.
2. Press Enter, type a sentence with spaces, and confirm the words still separate.
3. Open a store and press space: it should behave exactly as Escape does, not slam shut from a
   sub-menu.
4. With a full backpack and something in Levski's grid, press space - the window stays and says why.
5. Check the Keymapping screen: Hide Info Screens is still listed, and binding it to space no longer
   does anything different from binding it to nothing.
