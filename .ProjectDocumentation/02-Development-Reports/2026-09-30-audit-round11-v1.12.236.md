# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 11 (v1.12.236)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 11 ran six read-only tracks:
1. champion and lesser-unique affixes, and monster variants (second pass);
2. every path that creates a dropped item;
3. sockets, runes, runewords, charms and Imbuement Shards (second pass);
4. every fork option in both of its states;
5. time: what runs during a pause, a menu or a level load, and at other game speeds;
6. integer widths and overflow.

Every finding was verified against the code first. None of the six found a crash or save corruption.

## Fixed: time

- **Charge ran on the wall clock.** A pause, the Esc menu, alt-tab or a level load used up its 2 s dash and 3 s
  cooldown, and a faster game speed shrank the cooldown. Both now count game ticks (40 and 60).
- **Clicking the floor mid-Charge** kept the sprint for up to 2 s, with no swing, so no cooldown started. The dash now
  ends when a walk order replaces the charge.
- **A Venomous bleed froze in town** and drained again on the next trip down, minutes later. Town now clears it.
- **The two halves of a chill ended at different times.** Cleansing, Juggernaut and the RfA-12 shortener cut the walk
  slow but not the action slow. Both are shortened alike now, and stairs clear the walk slow along with the chill.
- **Telemetry's time-to-kill** included pauses and moved with the game speed. It now counts game ticks, written out as
  50 ms each.

## Fixed: options

- **Monster Variant Chance** was clamped at 28%, Torment's own rung. On Torment, every value from 100 to 300 was the
  same, and on Nightmare and Hell, 150 to 300 were. The cap is now half the monsters.
- **The Primal default of 1** rounded to zero in its 5% band. The per-mille chance now rounds to the nearest value.
- **A value the ini's list does not offer** now falls back to the default. Before, the menu showed the first entry
  while the game acted on the bad value.
- **Monster Range Highlight and Wall Outline** no longer outline the hero's own golems, companions and army in red.
  Hovering one still outlines it.
- **Save failures** are now also shown on screen. With the Event Log off, the log had been their only channel.
- **Escape in the Refresh Until prompt** now cancels the edit. Before, what was typed stayed and reached the ini.
- **Stale descriptions rewritten:** Game Clock, XP Counter, XP Gain Indicator, Remaining Monster XP, Event Log,
  Lesser Unique Density, the three tier chances, Unique Drop Chance Percent and Reset Stats Button.

## Fixed: champions and variants

- **Vampiric, Devouring and Venomous did nothing on ranged monsters.** They were wired to the melee path only. A
  monster missile that lands now fires them.
- **Searing and Voltaic** shoot their element, as Glacial already did. On an archer or a caster they had been only a
  recolour.
- **The Might pack aura** now reaches arrows and special attacks. The health bar already showed it on them.
- **Diablo (and the other never-spawned types) and the hero's own minions** are never Giant or Runt. Diablo had been
  75% or 120% in about one game in eight.
- **A charging variant** keeps its tint during the charge, and a Luminous charger takes its light with it.
- **Ranged champions** now report their own name on death and in the odds panel, not the borrowed one.

## Fixed: the item endgame

- **Item Repair, the two durability oils and the Hidden Shrine switched off a socketed Zod.** They wrote over its
  durability stamp while the rune stayed socketed. They now leave a Zod host alone. The host now reads
  "Indestructible" rather than "Dur: 255/60".
- **Fire and lightning from sockets, shards and Enchant never reached a bow's arrows.** Arrows now take them, as the
  melee blow does.
- **The Mourning Token** granted fire and lightning only, and its line named the fire half alone. It now grants all
  four resistances, as its own row says.
- **Cube rebuilds with Tempering** gave a free repair, or left a "broken" item with durability. The wear is now the
  item's own as it went in.
- **Tempering** no longer repairs an ethereal item, and it mends a broken one.
- **Shard of Ease** is accepted on an item that asks only a level.
- **The runeword book's "Possible" filter** reads the extra backpack pages.

## Fixed: drops

- **Diablo as a rift guardian** dropped an ordinary monster's pile: mostly nothing and gold. He now drops as the other
  three guardians do.
- **The named encounters' charms** (Chapel, Mourning, Vault) fell off ordinary monsters from Nightmare on. The
  encounter now pays them alone.
- **The drop tail** (Magic Find, sockets, ethereal, the log) was missing on several paths. The fork's own gear bases,
  the Gilded drop, Nightmare+ quest uniques, and Na-Krul's and the Slain Hero's items now get it.
- **Discarded retry rolls** no longer use up a unique's one drop.
- **Celia's and the Hork Demon's amulets** were level 13 on every difficulty. They now scale with difficulty.

## Fixed: integer widths

- **Life and mana were capped at 2000 every tick.** This was vanilla's anti-cheat ceiling for level-50 heroes. A fork
  hero whose maximum was higher could never fill the orb. The cap is now the hero's own maximum.
- **Backpack gold was summed in an int every tick.** Past about 2.1 billion it wrapped negative. It is now summed the
  saturating way.
- **Monster ranged attacks and runes** squeezed missile ids through `int8_t`. The fork's ids run to 147. This could
  not happen yet, but it would have become a crash.

## Not changed (open)

- **Warrior and Barbarian double damage:** the chance is the hero's level, `GenerateRnd(100) < level`. It was at most
  50% in vanilla and is 99% at the fork's level 99. Capping it at 50 would cut melee damage by about a quarter at the
  top. **The user's call.**
- **A charm does not say whether it is one of the live three.** The panel does not know where the item sits.
- **Nothing tells the player that runewords need a basic host.**
- **Hel's 60% cap** is not shown when four Hels read -80%.
- **Inventory Tab Level Gates** turned on lock pages that already hold items.
- **Event Log off** still leaves a menu entry that toggles a hidden log.
- **Demo playback** does not pin the density or drop options. This is a developer tool.
- **A quest boss's special treasure on Nightmare and above** misses the unique +3.
- **A variant's identity** follows the ini dial when the game reads it, while its stats were fixed at spawn. V1 always
  starts a New Game, so this cannot happen across sessions.
- **Cleanse at the Cube** is free, while the Mystic's Cleanse costs. This is a design question.
