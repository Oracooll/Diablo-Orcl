# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 40 (v1.12.268)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 40 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.267;
2. the Sorcerer's, Rogue's and Monk's actives (fifth pass);
3. item names and the tooltips of recently changed items;
4. monster AI of the special types;
5. the drop-only items (charms, shards, signets, maps, keystones, salvage);
6. a crash-risk sweep.

## Fixed: regressions of round 39

- **P2: auto-pick skips a recipe the grid cannot run.** Round 39 said Free the Sockets on an ethereal Zod host was skipped,
  but only the explicit path asked. Ogden's table gave a blank result with the success sound.
- **P2: Oil of Permanence survives the Mystic.** The oil-work sum could not reach the indestructible value and came back
  254: Zod's stamp shape with no Zod, which Make Ethereal then halved.
- **The item-drop rules name the Orcl objects** (the sigil, the Cube, the Monument). By selFlag they barred every closed
  door, barrel and shrine in the dungeon.
- **A warcry recast within 90%** still takes its new rank to the sheet.
- **The oil sums** are held to their fields' ranges. Two stale comments were corrected.

## Fixed: items

- **P2: Zod goes only into a host with durability.** In jewellery it did nothing, while the ring said "indestructible" and
  took Zod's level 69.
- **P2: a quest's own reward is never salvage.** A Uniques charm turned Arkaine's Valor, the Butcher's Cleaver, the
  Harlequin Crest, the Veil of Steel, Griswold's Edge and Bovine Plate into Encrustments on pickup.
- **Imbuing to the cap at the workshop** pays the milestone, as in the pack.
- **A charm pushed out of the live three** by a paste or SORT keeps the hero alive.
- **The save message** names the held item as shown. **The Monument menu** reads the keystone tier as it opens.
- **A missing short name** is guarded.

## Fixed: skills

- **P2: the field skills set down at the cursor need the hero's sight:** Meteor, Funeral Star, Ancestral Court, Frozen
  Sentinel, Lightning Rod, Ember Mine, Storm Crucible, Brittle Ground. They landed on the next room's pack.
- **P2: Ride the Lightning** flies and strikes the same line, to its wall. She flew to the cursor through walls while the
  strike walked another ray.

## Fixed: monster AI

- **P2: Terror leaves a hidden monster to its own AI.** A faded Counselor or Unseen stayed hidden and unhittable for the
  whole curse, a day under Eternal Torment.
- **P2: a visible Counselor walked back by a repel can be hit** (Howl, Grim Ward, Daze, Blinding Flash). Vanilla's own
  retreat is always hidden, and the unhittable rule was written for it.
- **P2: a converted monster's shot spares the hero's side.** A converted Succubus's Bloodstar struck the skeletons in its
  line.
- **One body a tile in the corpse table.** A record left behind by a scavenger or a corpse skill came back with the next
  death on that tile, so one body could be raised or burst twice.
