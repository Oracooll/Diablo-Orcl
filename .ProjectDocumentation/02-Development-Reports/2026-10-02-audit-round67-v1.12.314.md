# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 67 (v1.12.314)

**Date:** night of 2026-10-01/02. Debug only. 903 tests pass. Commit 8cc77426. The game was open throughout (the user's DiabloOrcl.exe), so the exe link failed with LNK1168. Everything else built, and ctest ran on its own. **DiabloOrcl.exe still holds v1.12.312's code until the game is closed and the tree is rebuilt.** The same goes for the oracool.mpq repack still owed from round 66.

Four read-only auditors ran: a regression review of v1.12.313, Sorcerer/Rogue/Monk actives (9th pass), monster AI (6th) and the save pipeline (6th).

## Regression review of v1.12.313
No bugs. Tidy-ups:
- **Zeal's ladder test:** it gives mana before its first check (it passed at 0 mana for the wrong reason).
- **Battle Command:** a recast one rank higher (its own +1) is the same rank, while two higher is a real rank-up and applies.
- **Storm Crucible:** arcs no longer overlap after the strike's extra tick, so one arc and one light at a time.
- **Text:** a stale comment and a translation marker.

## Actives
- **Hunter's Claim:** takes bosses, so Diablo and the endgame bosses (`FightsAsUnique`) count. Its arrows no longer pass through them as if "ordinary".
- **Heaven Splitter and Dragon's Wrath:** cost nothing on an empty line, as Seismic Slam since round 28.
- **`NearestStrikable`** (new): the nearest monster a blow of the type can hurt, under the cast's sight rules.
  - Arc and Chi Wave pick their first target with it, and their hops skip the immune.
  - Shock Arrow's arc leaps from the struck one's sight (round corners, as Arc) onto one it can hurt.
  - Phantom Volley leaves out the magic-immune, so a volley at only those is not paid for.
- **Summons:** Valkyrie, Decoy, Spirit Guardian and Ancestral Call join the cast-sight refusal list. A Decoy past a wall had held the closed room.
- **Low Branch:** its row no longer claims "without staggering it" (the swing flinches as any blow does).

## Monster AI
- **Illusion Weaver:** a repelled one (Howl, Grim Ward, Daze, Blinding Flash) can be hit. Only its own Sneak retreat hides it now, as with the Counselors in round 40.
- **Follow-up missiles:** take their parent's side and spawn serial. An acid splash tests the splash's own side, so a dead caster's slot taken by another monster no longer flips the side.
- **Conversion:** ends when the converted monster dies, after its puddle check. Its flags had stayed on the corpse, and the slot's delete then put out a Luminous variant's own light.

## Save pipeline
- **Extra inventory pages:** a page needs its whole grid and count in the file. A file cut inside the last page had been read as an empty page and then saved over the items.
- **Timed minions:** one whose clock ran out mid-death keeps its record until its slot is freed, so the level save's withdraw sees it.

## Left open
- **Unchecked level-file counts and indices:** `LoadLevel` trusts every count and index in the file (inherited from vanilla). It is reachable only with a corrupt temp file the game itself never writes.
- **Oil of Accuracy/Mastery to-hit display:** from round 66.
- **Rage spender settlement latch:** from round 66.
- **Gargoyle lift counted as a hit:** from round 66.
- **Zeal's missed first swing:** from round 66; may be by design.
