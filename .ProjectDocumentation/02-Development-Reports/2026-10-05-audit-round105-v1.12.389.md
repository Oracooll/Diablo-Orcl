## Fixed: the owed reward keystone, tracked by its seed
Round 104 settled the debt when "no keystone lies on the rift floor". The review found duplication and loss paths in that rule:
- **Duplicate:** drop a spare keystone on the floor, then pick up the reward. The spare kept the debt alive while the reward sat in the pack, so the next game refunded a second one.
- **Loss, cursor:** the reward held on the cursor counted as carried.
- **Loss, failed drop:** a reward that could not be laid on the floor cleared the debt on the next tick.
- **Loss, rebuilt floor:** a cleared floor rebuilt after a failed level save lost the reward lying on it.
- **Duplicate, exit save:** a game-menu exit in the same frame as the pickup saved the keystone with the debt still open.
- **Loss, older refund:** a refund that had found no room was overwritten by the next rift's entry.

The redesign:
1. **The reward is known by its seed** (`State.rewardSeed`, recorded by `DropKeystoneAt`). The debt clears only when THAT keystone is in the hero's pack (any page), belt or stash (`HeroKeepsKeystone`, `SettleRiftRewardKeystone`). It is checked each tick, after carrying the floor's keystones home, and in `SaveOnExit` after a held item is put back.
2. **A reward that could not be laid stays owed** and comes back next game. The log says so.
3. **A rebuilt cleared floor lays the reward again** where the boss fell.
4. **Opening a Guardian Rift first gives back any older owed keystone.** With no room for it, the rift is not opened: "Make room in the pack or the stash for the keystone you are owed first."

## Reported, not coded (backpack, stash, options: nothing lost or duplicated)
- **Locked inventory pages are skipped for placement but can still be drawn from.** A belt refill can come from a hidden locked page, and a scroll on one can be read. Only reachable when the level-gate option is turned on with items already on those pages.
- **The split prompt checks the amount, not which stack it is splitting.** Latent; no current path compacts that list while the prompt is open.
- **Single-value int options are not clamped on load.** Their readers clamp, so no impact today.
