# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 119 (v1.12.405)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 952 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks:**
- a regression review of v1.12.404 (clean: no P1 or P2);
- the vanilla spell and missile engine as the fork changed it.

## Fixed: tidy-ups from the review of v1.12.404
1. **The Barbarian's classic sheet:** the mana-steal row reads "-", and Advanced Stats drops the "No mana" curse row for him.
2. **The level-save aim test** now also checks that a minion of the hero's, hunting the released monster, is left alone.

## Fixed: spells and missiles
3. **Lightning, Chain Lightning and Inferno passed closed doors (P2).** Their tile checks read only the tile's missile flag, while a closed door blocks through its object. Chain Lightning, which aims at every monster in reach with no sight check, struck rooms beyond a door. Monster casters (Storm Riders, Advocates) reached the hero the same way. A new `ClosedDoorAt` stops all three, and Fire Wall and Lightning Wall growth too (round 99 checked only the anchor).
4. **A rune that found no place was still spent.** `AddRune` now reports failure, and the five rune casts fizzle, so the rune is kept: round 16's "nothing happened, nothing is paid".
5. **Bone Spirit and the Elemental** no longer home on a converted ally, which they flew through for nothing.
6. **A converted ally no longer sets off a rune,** whose blast could catch the hero beside it.
7. **A Town Portal scroll or staff charge is refused in town,** as the spell has been since round 84. Scrolls and staves never reach `CheckSpell`. This was latent: neither drops now.
8. **The `CheckSpell` comment** no longer claims it covers scrolls and staves, and notes `RiftForbidsTownPortal` is inert.

## For the user (design calls)
- **Paralysis rolls once per damage tick.** Lightning, Inferno and Lightning Wall strike every tick they overlap, so one bolt stuns about 62% of the time at level 1 and 93% at level 20, against the hover's 15%. The same per-tick firing spends Momentum and stacks Crescendo. One roll per missile would match the text.
- **Stone Curse still petrifies rift bosses,** while every other crowd control spares them. Round 101 made the boss tick handle stone, so it looks accepted.
- **Teleport can land on a closed door's tile.** Teleport, Phasing and Apocalypse work in town.
- **A cast is free if its staff or scroll is moved mid-cast** (vanilla).

## Checked and fine
- **Fizzle refunds**, the payment paths and `CheckSpell`'s order.
- **`AddMissile`** is unbounded.
- **Who hits whom**, the damage math and the town guards.
- **Golem, Town Portal, Stone Curse, Mana Shield, Teleport and Phasing** are unpaid when they fail.
- **Spell lifetimes across a level change.**
