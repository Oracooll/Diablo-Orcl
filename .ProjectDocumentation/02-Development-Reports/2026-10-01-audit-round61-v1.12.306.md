# 2026-10-01 - Audit round 61 (v1.12.306)

**Date:** 2026-10-01. Debug only. 902 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest.

## Regression review of v1.12.305
- **Leap Attack leaps were free after the first one.** A disarm with nullopt left `LeapAttackPrepaid` set, so the next leap read it as paid and set it again.
  - The leap now pays its own price through `CanPaySkill`/`SettleSkill`.
  - Any arming but the Leap Attack swing itself clears the prepaid blow.
- **The stagger stash:**
  - It is kept only while a button is held (`sgbMouseDown` or `ControllerActionHeld`).
  - It is merged across staggers in hit recovery; a second hit had wiped it.
  - It is forgotten on a pad press (`Interact`, `PerformSpellAction`), where a stale stash had turned a later plain pad attack into a paid Bash.
- **Ground Stomp:** Earthen Might's Rage counts only what the stomp stunned. A free stomp beside a boss had been filling the pool.
- **Town Absolute Zero:** a second vortex fizzles unpaid.
- **The XP readout** uses the bar's hover, chat included.

## Save pipeline, 5th pass
- **A new hero never overwrites a hero file.**
  - A slot whose file exists is never "free" in `pfile_ui_get_first_unused_save_num`.
  - `pfile_ui_save_create` refuses to publish over one.
  - Before, a hero file briefly locked (by a sync or a scanner) went unlisted, and a new character could be written over it.
- **The level record buffer is 512 KiB.** A full Hellfire floor was 258,467 bytes, 3.7 KiB short of an app_fatal at the next item-format growth.
- **The save log** names each failed file.
- **Left as is:** a level record that exists but fails to decode still ends the session in LoadLevel (file damage only).

## Necromancer, 10th pass
- **A Revived acid beast** lays no acid puddle when it dies; the puddle hurts only players, so it hurt its own hero.
- **Every rift is its own floor** for "once a floor" passives (Final Service, Rathma's Shield): `FloorStamp` mixes in the rift's seed.
- **Bone Prison** holds its 3 s; the last pulse had held to 4.25 s.
- **A Skeletal Mage that died while its bolt flew** gets no Life Tap share, which had put life back into a body in its death animation.
