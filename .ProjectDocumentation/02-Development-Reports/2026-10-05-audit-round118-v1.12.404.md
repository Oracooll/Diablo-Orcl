# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 118 (v1.12.404)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 952 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks:**
- a regression review of v1.12.403;
- the hero's stats and sheet against the formulas combat uses.

## Corrected: round 117's seed claim
1. **The "repeating rifts and arenas" premise was false.** `SaveLevel` redraws the town seed every time the hero leaves town, so each town visit starts from a new generator state. Round 117's clock-and-count mix stays as harmless extra entropy. The round-117 report and both code comments now say "defensive only". Had the repeat been real, the rift boss and tileset picks would still have repeated, since they draw from the game generator.

## Fixed: regressions and gaps
2. **The level-save aim no longer touches the hero's own.** A companion hunting a Confused monster was re-aimed at the hero (harmless, since a minion never picks a player, but wrong). The hunter loop now skips `isPlayerMinion`.
3. **Hunters of a withdrawn minion** are aimed at the hero at the level save. On a revisit, the pack used to walk to the skeleton's old tile (`AimHuntersAtHeroForLevelSave`).
4. **The round-117 test** restores the hero's position and also checks the released monster's enemy and the hunter's aim.

## Fixed: stats
5. **A piece breaking at low life could kill the hero (P2).** The break ran at the end of a hit recovery, when life is low. Its +life (and items switched off by a lost +Strength) went with a plain `CalcPlrInv`, and life fell to 0 with no blow landing. Both the armour break and the weapon decay paths now use `CalcPlrInvKeepingLife`, the fork's "a bonus ending never kills" rule.
6. **The Barbarian's sheet** no longer shows mana steal or "+N mana per kill". He has no mana pool.

## For the user (design calls)
- **The block chance** is the equal-level reading. In combat, 2 × (monster level − hero level) comes off it. On Torment at 2.0 the real chance is about 120 points lower, usually 0. The line could read the last attacker, as the armour bar does.
- **The Warrior's and Barbarian's double-damage chance** (character level %) is never shown. At level 90, 90% of blows double.
- **The stat share is halved twice on a bow** for the Barbarian and the Bard. The sheet and combat agree.
- **"+N armor vs demons/undead"** counts against melee only, not monster arrows. Either add it to the missile path or say "melee".
- **The Rage spell** (Hellfire) skips the item-requirement recheck when its cooldown takes Strength away.
- **The "Attack frames" value** is the first swing's. A held repeat swing skips fewer.

## Checked and fine
- **Damage, to-hit, armour and resistances** come from the very function combat calls, as do hit recovery, movement speed and cast speed.
- **Elixirs, life and mana:** level-ups, refunds, steal and Rage are consistent.
- **Overflow:** all clamps hold at level 99 with stacked gear.
