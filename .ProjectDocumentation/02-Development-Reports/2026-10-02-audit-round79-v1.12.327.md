# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 79 (v1.12.327)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit 4c6498a6.

Three read-only auditors ran:
- a regression review of v1.12.326;
- the Necromancer's bone, poison and corpse skills (14th pass);
- items on the ground (drops, pickup, labels; 1st pass).

## Regression review of v1.12.326
- **Chilling Armor:** it now answers each monster missile once (`Missile::coldRetorted`), not each tick a missile overlaps the hero. v1.12.326 excluded Inferno and Lightning by type, which was the wrong model:
  - an Advocate's bolt carries all its damage in `Lightning` segments, so its retort went silent;
  - a Storm Rider's `ThinLightning` still answered every tick.

## Necromancer
- **Bone Wall:** each arm grows out from the centre and stops at the first solid tile, or the first tile out of the centre's sight. The cast and the half-second tick use the same `BoneWallSegment`. A centre inside a wall refuses.
  - Before, the outer segments could stand in the next room, cutting and shoving monsters there twice a second.
- **Rigor Mortis:** it slows only what the bone blow damaged. A magic-immune monster took nothing and was slowed anyway.
- **Teeth's hover:** "N down the line" is at most 5. The line's first tile is the fan's middle, which was already toothed.

## Ground items
- **Monster-dropped runes:** they now wait for the hero's step before auto-pickup takes them. `StartDropTumble` stamps the landing tile, as `RespawnItem` already did. Every rune a monster dropped had kept the default, so a rune was picked up mid-tumble at the end of a step: the user's original "they appear straight into my backpack".
- **`RespawnDeadItem` on a full floor:** it gives up the cheapest ordinary floor item instead of destroying the new one. That covers:
  - outgrown items;
  - potions downgraded by a steal attack;
  - excess gold;
  - the cursor item on death after the pack, belt and stash were full.
- **Item labels:** they need an explored tile outside town. Unexplored loot showed its name and position and could be clicked to walk there.

## Left open

- **Press-time controls:** they wait for the press/release pass.
- **Workshop wells:** an empty transmute at a well still plays the success sound.
- **Tint table:** it is rebuilt every frame.
- **Guardian Rift keystone:** whether to refund it.
