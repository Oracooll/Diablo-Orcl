# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 103 (v1.12.387)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 948 tests pass; one is new: `OracoolRiftBosses.EveryRowsFloorHasAScatterType`. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks (two):** a regression review of v1.12.386, and rift level generation per tileset. A test was also written for round 102's empty-rift bug.

## Fixed
1. **The guardian rose where he could not be reached (P2).** He could appear on the far bank of a lava river (sight crosses lava, which is correct) or behind a closed door (the old `LineClearMissile` test saw through it). Neither boss nor hero could then reach the other. His first-choice spot now needs the hero's door-aware sight and a walk of at most 20 steps (`RiftGuardianSpotReachable`), the way home's own rule. The any-tile fallback stays.
2. **Theme objects on the rift landing (P3).** Barrels (explosive ones in the Caves), racks and crosses could stand on the hero's arrival tile. Theme barrels and the 3x3 and 5x5 theme objects now keep clear of it. The check applies in rifts only, so ordinary floors draw exactly as before.
3. **The rift HUD's boss-name check (P3).** It is now measured together with the clock that follows it, instead of a guessed 60px reserve.

## New test
- **`EveryRowsFloorHasAScatterType`:** for every one of the 42 rows, at its lowest and highest rung, the floor's monster type list (`GetLevelMTypes`, headless) holds at least one scatter type, so the bar can fill, and the boss's own type. It guards round 102's empty-rift fix. A test seam, `ResetLevelMonsterTypesForTest`, empties the type list and image budget as a level load does.

## Reported, not coded
- **Skeleton Room and Goat Shrine themes never appear in a rift.** `InitThemes` runs before the rift's monster types are loaded. Harmless, and deterministic.
- **The rift sprite load can now reach about 2.7x the old 4000 budget** (Balrog boss + escort + roster). It is compressed CL2, a few tens of MB, so fine on a PC.
- **Two harmless guards:** the roster's `picked == 0` fallback and the blink's final fallback cannot be reached.
