# 2026-10-01 - Audit round 60 (v1.12.305)

**Date:** 2026-10-01. Debug only. 902 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. v1.12.304 was built and never committed; the items fixes came after it.

## Regression review of v1.12.303
- **All three spell repeats stop on a cooling skill:** open ground, at a monster and at a player. The fix had only covered the first, and a cast at a monster is the usual one.
- **Town Absolute Zero:** one vortex at a time. A held button had laid a new one every cast.
- **The XP readout** shows only where the bar answers: with the bar option off, it showed on hover over nothing.
- **Test:** `AFreshHeroHasNoCooldown` uses the new-game clear, since a death now keeps cooldowns.

## Paladin and Barbarian, 5th pass
- **A held skill survives a stagger.** `StartPlrHit` clears the swing latches, and the held button's repeat only resent a plain attack. So after the first hit taken, a held Bash, Frenzy, Zeal or Cleave went on as plain swings: no bonus, no knockback, no Rage.
  - **The fix:** the cleared latches are remembered (`StaggeredSwingLatches`, not a Charge, not the throw), and the repeat re-arms them (`RestoreStaggeredSwingLatches`).
  - **A fresh press forgets them** (`LeftMouseDown`/`RightMouseDown`).
- **Leap Attack:** the leap's 14 Rage pays for the blow it lands on arrival (`LeapAttackPrepaid`). It had cost 28.
- **Whirlwind stops** under a window, chat or help, as every held action does.
- **A buff recast at another rank** is a paid cast. Before, a nearly full one took the new rank and fizzled unpaid. The same rank nearly full is still refused.
- **Ground Stomp and Split Ranks** are paid only for what they stun or move: `Stagger` and `Shove` return whether they took.
- **Sacrifice** takes 8% of the blow, as its text says (it was a twelfth).
- **Left as is:** Unforgiving's first Rage at 6 s matches the Rage test's design (5 s of fury, then a second).

## Items and crafting, 7th pass
- **An older shop item's armour difference** (format before 16, `_iOracoolOilAC` -1) counts as drift, not oil. The shop picked its base before its armour roll, so the difference had been carried on as "oil" for good.
- **Consecrate keeps a Rare's oils**, as Reforge, Retier and Ennoble do.
- **Gillian lends only what her well is short of.** With 2 Powder on the well and 3 in the pack, a 4-Powder Enrich had been refused.

## User decision
**The Guardian's Path (Monk):** the 15% slip needs a one-handed weapon and the other hand empty. It was dual-wielding, which only the Bard can do. The staff half is unchanged.

## Build script
The tests are now skipped when the build fails. v1.12.304's link error (two new functions inside an anonymous namespace) ran them against a missing `libdevilutionx_so.dll`, and each test showed a system error dialog.
