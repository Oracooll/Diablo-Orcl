# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 37 (v1.12.265)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 37 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.262;
2. the Sorcerer's, Rogue's and Monk's passives and masteries;
3. the Necromancer (sixth pass);
4. item generation and loot;
5. rendering and lighting;
6. sound, music and options.

## Fixed: regressions of round 36

- **P1: companions could strike quest speakers and hidden monsters.** In round 36 a comment was inserted mid-line in
  `Targetable`, and it swallowed the "can be hit" and "hidden" tests. A Valkyrie could kill Lachdanan, Zhar or Gharbad
  mid-speech. A scan of every line added since v1.12.257 found no other code swallowed by a comment.
- **P2: a held attack or walk stopped the moment the cursor touched the HUD's plate or orbs.** The guard now covers only
  the windows (side panels and floating windows), and only with the mouse.
- **A converted monster** no longer turns on a Decoy or a guarding companion (the taunt).
- The Sell All and salvage comments name all four quest jewels. The new test restores the store entries it sets.

## Fixed: the Necromancer

- **P2: curses reach only what the Necromancer can see.** A cursor on the room behind a wall cursed its pack and spent the
  Essence. Single curses are included.
- **P2: Decompose and Bone Prison** take only a monster in sight, as every other targeted skill since round 15.
- **Bone Wall** is not raised in the next room.
- **Bone Spear's and Teeth's bolts** fly down the line the strike runs, to its wall, not at the exact cursor.
- **Corpse Explosion** does not spend a corpse the hero cannot see.
- **Bane keeps its own second:** a recast within a second pushed its strike back.
- **A curse tags its monster:** kills by a Confused or Attracted pack are the hero's.

## Fixed: passives

- **P2: the weapon's fire or lightning** (Enchant, Fire Mastery, Lightning Mastery, Inner Fire) rolls to hit again. Under
  the SpellsNeverMiss trial it landed on every swing, including misses.
- **P2: Momentum's three blows** are not spent twice per swing by the weapon's elemental part.
- **P2: Prodigy** gives back at most half a spell's cost. A halved 3-mana Firebolt was free. The row and the hover say so.

## Fixed: loot

- **P2: Magic Find is capped at 75** for the white-to-Rare upgrade. At 100 every white weapon and armour came out Rare.
- **Town Portal scrolls** no longer come from barrels, chests or Adria's shelf while the portal is a built-in ability. A fresh roll only:
  a stored seed still rebuilds into its scroll (v1.12.264 swapped the replay too and failed the Hellfire pack golden).

## Fixed: rendering and sound

- **The cursor's saved backgrounds** are cleared with a new back buffer. Keyed by address, a new buffer at an old one's
  took its rect and wrote past the smaller buffer.
- **The outline cache** keys on the sprite's size too. A new sprite at a freed one's address took the old outline.
- **A black tile clipped exactly at its width** no longer passes a zero length to a fill that assumes one (Release only).
- **The half-transparent rects** refuse a negative size.
- **A skill cue whose file did not load** lets the vanilla cast sound stand in.
- **Refresh Until:** a half-typed edit is cancelled by every close but Enter, including the window's X, and never reaches
  the ini.
- **The key-binding screen** refuses F1-F12, which are answered before the keymapper. A binding there never fired and
  unbound the old action.

## Not changed (open)

- **For the user: a base's uniques drop in a fixed order.** Vanilla's CheckUnique throws its draw away and takes the LAST
  eligible unique, so the 33 ring uniques come from the table's end down, one per game each. A random pick among the eligible
  (the same single draw) was built in v1.12.263 and reverted: it changes which unique a stored seed rebuilds into (pack_test
  goldens, "Constricting Ring" came back "The Bleeder"). Taking it means updating those goldens.

- **Necromancer:**
  - Life Tap misses the killing blow on most paths.
  - Poison doses combine their longest time with their strongest rate.
  - Masteries are frozen into minions at raise time.
  - Serration counts the hero-to-monster distance.
  - Six curse rows omit "Paid in Essence".
- **Passives:**
  - Valkyrie's arrows feed the Rogue's on-hit passives.
  - Sharpshooter's "double" is additive.
  - The wording of the Paralysis, Soft Tread and Alacrity rows.
- **Loot:**
  - Jewellery base tiers multiply only the value.
  - Griswold's Magic-tab Oracool block gets no base tier.
  - The unique level gate uses the roll level.
  - Book replay.
  - Tiered staves carry no spell.
  - Shift-click belts any belt-able item.
- **Rendering:** latent sheet-rect reads, the RGB blit into an 8-bit surface, two decoder edge cases, and
  palette caches that ignore the colour-cycling band.
- **Sound:** the aura loop ignores the volume setting (latent: no Loop rows exist), and `sound_stop` does not reach the
  cached cues.
