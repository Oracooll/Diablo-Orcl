# 2026-10-01 - Audit round 42 (v1.12.275)

**Date:** 2026-10-01. Debug only. The user: "resume non-stop auditing."

**Tracks (five read-only agents):**
- regression review of v1.12.270-274;
- consumables and portals;
- stores and prices;
- options (first time);
- town objects (after the Cube's ProcessObjects lesson).

## Fixed

### Regression
- **Blight (P2):** since round 41 it landed 8 tiles out every cast, because `LineEnd` walks the full length.
  - The pool is now `Clamped(here, target, 8)`, kept when the line to it is clear and stopped short of a wall otherwise.
  - A cast on the hero's own tile lands there.

### Consumables
- **Oils (P2):** an oil was spent when its cursor was picked. A right-click, a click on nothing, or a level change threw it away.
  - `UseInvItem` no longer spends oils. The `CURSOR_OIL` click spends one (`SpendOneOil`) only when it lands.
  - `HasOilToSpend` drops the cursor if the hero no longer carries one.
  - Test: `AnOilIsSpentOnlyWhenItLands`.

### Options
- **Resolution drift (P2):** with Fit to Screen (on by default), the saved size was snapped by width plus height to the curated list on every launch, so 960p came back as 1050p and 768p as 800p.
  - A saved size with a curated height and a width between 4:3 and the 21:9 cap now loads as saved.
  - No ini round-trip harness exists, so there is no test.
- **Game Mode and Shareware (P2):** both are now hidden in Settings. Switching to Diablo or the shareware mid-session ran a half-broken game.
- **Keymapper:** refuses Space and Esc, which are answered before it. Hide Info Screens no longer lists Space.
- **Gamma keys:** the two descriptions read the wrong way round.

### Stores
- **Sell All:** keeps quest rewards (the salvage list), charms, Sealed Maps and Signets. One at a time still sells them.
- **Repair price:** at least 1 on the identified-magic branch too. A lightly worn set piece read "needs no repair".
  - The vanilla test `AddStoreHoldRepair_magic` now expects 1, with a dated note.
- **Stash gold:**
  - `TakePlrsMoney` never takes the pool below 0.
  - A loaded value past INT_MAX reads as 0, not INT_MAX.
  - Test: `ChargingPastAllGoldLeavesTheStashAtNothing`.
- **Belt sales:** a belt sale no longer counts a freed backpack cell, at both sell sites.
- **Cain's identify:** plays the gold sound.

### Cold spells
- **Frozen Sentinel:** the sink row looped five times past its field. `AddArtEffectFacing` now measures one play of its own row.
- **Ice Needle and Ice Lance on the hero's tile:** they strike the way he faces, where they fly.
- **Countdown column:** no longer counts Fire Wall or Lightning Wall missiles. Immolation Arrow, the Hellfire rings and Rune of Light raise the same missiles.

### Town
- **Rift Monument menu:** closes when the hero walks more than three tiles away. With keyboard or pad movement it could be used from anywhere.
  - `ProcessStonegate` moved from ProcessObjects, which never runs in town, to the town branch.
- **Portal arch:** no longer blocks item drops on three tiles beside the town-portal landing.
- **Latent guards:**
  - `SyncObjectAnim` dresses only `IsLevskiRoarObject` as the Cube.
  - `IsWirtCartObject` checks the town.
  - Town's `LoadLevel` keeps the object count just placed.

### Tests
- **Cube town test:** restores its state through an RAII guard.
- **Assert message:** the `\l` escape is fixed.
