# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 23 (v1.12.248)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 23 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.247;
2. the class tree's data and the symmetry of what it applies;
3. sound and music;
4. champion affixes, Dread boss traits and monster variants (second pass);
5. the hero's save round trip (third pass);
6. the vendors (third pass).

Every finding was verified against the code first.

## Fixed: regressions of round 22

- **Flat "damage taken" on fire walls, acid and Inferno** is back to vanilla's 1/64 of a point. That path is the
  damage per TICK, and v1.12.247's whole points floored every tick at 1 life under -3, or added up to 6 life a tick
  under Fool's Crest.
- **A Rogue arrow's burst** steps back until it is off the wall, a door counts as a wall, and a stepped-back Exploding
  Arrow burst includes the tile in front of the wall.
- **An item pushed out of the hand by a pickup** is placed after the pickup frees its floor slot, so a full floor cannot
  lose it. Gold goes through the gold path and the pack recalculates.
- **Making room for a guaranteed reward** never deletes a socketed white item (a runeword, its gems) or one with
  affixes of its own.

## Fixed: saves

- **P2: a rejected item on an extra backpack page lost the whole page two loads later.** The empty record kept its list
  slot and was saved with no grid anchor, so the next load found the page inconsistent and emptied it. The page now
  closes up behind a rejected item.
- **P2: a stash file that exists but will not open** (a damaged header, a lock held elsewhere) is refused, not read as
  missing. Read as missing, the empty stash was saved over every hero's.
- **A stash written by a newer build** is refused instead of emptied and saved over.

## Fixed: vendors

- **P2: the shop's Repair and Recharge hammers** do the work before taking the fee. A gold pile that the fee used up
  shuffled the backpack list, and the repair landed on an emptied slot: gold taken, item still worn.
- **P2: Sell All sells the cheapest items first.** The buyback holds the last 40 sales, and selling the dearest first
  pushed them off it for good.
- **Repair All** says when it stops for want of gold.
- **An item bought back** is checked against this hero at once, as every other purchase is.
- **A right press on a shop service frame** no longer fires Sell All or Refresh at the next left release.
- **A right click on the HUD over the shop panel** no longer sells the held item.

## Fixed: champions and the class tree

- **P2: champions paid and credited as scripted uniques.** Every champion carries a unique type, so the unique test
  answered first and the champion tier never ran. The champion now gets its own rates:
  - treasure bonus 2, not 4;
  - item level +2, not +3;
  - rift credit 3, not 5.
- **Pack auras** (Might, Defiance) no longer strengthen a monster turned to the hero's side.
- **The legacy Paladin aura migration** names Might's row, index 8 since Holy Bolt's row came out.
- **A skill's flat armour** is magic armour, not armour from gear. The total is the same; the sheet's split was wrong.
- **An aura's level-up line** says "(while lit)".

## Fixed: sound

- **Leap, Leap Attack and Vaulting Strike** play their own cues only, not Teleport's launch sound too.
- **Holding the button on the Rift Monument or a waypoint sigil** no longer re-opens its menu every frame. The select
  click had replayed, and each sigil re-open had scheduled a save.
- **A rift's Na-Krul** does not speak his floor-24 line over the guardian's arrival sting.
- **A rift Diablo** screams once when he dies, not twice.
- **A cleared rift's way home** plays its opening sound when it opens, not on every re-entry.
- **Town Portal refused in a Guardian Rift** logs once per cast, not every frame the wells check it.

## Not changed (open)

- **Left F-key bindings to staff charges or scrolls** are checked before the final item and skill-tree pass on load, so
  a staff made usable by a Hel rune or a +Magic passive can lose its left binding.
- **A readied staff or scroll spell** can reload as the learned (mana) spell. This is known from round 18.
- **Vampiric, Devouring and Venomous** read the swing, not what landed. **Warded and Warding** do not resist cold, and
  **a champion's level and to-hit** are its authored row's, not its floor's.
- **The Thunderous burst** strikes the hero's army and the champion's own escort, as a trap.
- **Sonnet of Sight** grows every fifth rank only. The row is the hidden Bard's.
- **The invest and refund functions** trust the Abilities window to block other-class and unbuilt rows.
- **The fork's one-shot sounds** play through a pause, and the dead aura-loop code bypasses the sound settings.
- **Adria's Supplies tab** can skip its setup after an empty Sold tab. The effect is display only.

## Tests

Two tests had pinned the champion-as-unique bug: TreasureBonusRewardsChampionsAndUniques and
RiftKillCreditWeightsChampionsAndUniques built their champions with no unique type, which the game never does, and
asserted a unique's reward for a unique type with an affix. Both now build a champion as the game does.

Debug build and ctest: 890/890. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged through the run. Nothing seen in play.
