# v1.13.057 - three dev notes: aura glow under roofs, Earthquake on empty ground, chilled tint (2026-10-14)

Source: two notes in `development.md` (v1.13.044 town, v1.13.049 rift) and one in the Release notes, `DiabloOrcl RTM\Saved_Games\development.md` (v1.13.017 dlvl 1). All three moved to `development-archive.md`, batch of 2026-10-14.

- **Aura glow under houses** (aura_ground.cpp KeepAuraGlowFloor / NoteAuraGlowCover / AuraGlowCoveredAt; scrollrt.cpp DrawGame). The ring itself was always drawn on the floor, under the houses. Its glow (DrawAuraGlow, since 2026-10-12) is light added after the whole world, so it lit roofs too. Now the floor under every noted ring is copied once the floor pass ends (after DrawFigureGroundPass), and after DrawTileContent each pixel that changed is marked as covered. The glow lights only uncovered floor, on the CPU path and in the card's splats alike. One box per frame (the union of the rings' 1.7-radius reach), cleared by BeginAuraGlowFrame. It also leaves the hero's own body and any wall in front unlit. That is a small change in look: before, the floor glow lit his legs.
- **Earthquake on empty ground** (rfa12_actives.cpp Rfa12CastFindsNoFoe; player.cpp StartSpell). The release-frame refusal with nothing in 3 tiles was already there (v1.12.417). But StartSpell had already played the Barbarian's whole cast animation and his cue (cast2), so it read as the quake going off. StartSpell now makes the same test (MonstersWithin his tile, 3 tiles) before the animation begins. Earthquake only.
- **Chilled enemies light blue** (scrollrt.cpp DrawMonster). This was not done before: chilled monsters only had frost motes (scene_fx DrawMonsterStatus). Now a chilled, not frozen, monster draws through Tint::Ice, the chilled hero's glaze from 2026-10-04, over its own lit colours. A unique's TRN is unlit, a variant's is lit. Frozen, stone and infravision still win. 32-bit only.

Tests: OracoolDev1057.AuraGlowStaysOffWhatCoversTheFloor (real ring art, a roof painted over half the glow, roof pixels untouched, floor lit), OracoolDev1057.EarthquakeWithNoFoeInReachIsNotStarted. ctest 1065/1065, diablo.ini md5 unchanged.

Needs a play test: the glow under a Tristram roof; a chilled monster's blue next to a frozen one's frost; the empty-ground Earthquake no longer animating. Two possible side effects to check. First, with the smooth floor off, the floor triangles at a wall's foot are drawn in DrawCell, so the glow skips them. Second, an item or corpse on the floor inside the ring now goes unlit.
