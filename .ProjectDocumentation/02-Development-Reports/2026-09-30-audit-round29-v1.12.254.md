# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 29 (v1.12.254)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 29 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.253;
2. the save round trip (fourth pass, everything changed since v1.12.240);
3. the affix engine, Loot 2.0 (fourth pass);
4. Sorcerer and Rogue skill hovers against their effects (second pass);
5. Monk and Necromancer skill hovers against their effects (second pass);
6. vendors and the economy (third pass).

Every finding was verified against the code first.

## Fixed: regressions of round 28

- **Respawn with a life aura lit** no longer heals: the warcry buffs were cleared with a recalculation while the hero was
  still dead, which left the aura's life out, pinned the 1 life, and then added the aura back on top. They are cleared once
  he is out of death, and the recalculation keeps the 1 life.
- **The rift's way home avoids a Town Portal** read from the missiles themselves. The per-tile missile flag was too broad
  (a fire wall or an arrow counted) and is cleared and re-set during each missile pass, so a portal later in the list was
  missed.
- **"Game Saved"** stays off the full map, the Runeword Book and the Crafting book, as it did before round 28.
- **Inferno from a caster that is gone** lands a trap's damage, as the other monster missiles do; it dealt almost nothing.
- **Cain's line** keeps a separator before the level ("No required attributes, Lvl 12"). The Essence check on the
  refusal line also asks whether the hero uses Essence (no change in play).

## Fixed: saves

- **P1: a stash the game refused is never written.** When the stash file could not be read, the game promised to leave it
  untouched - but the first purchase marked the stash changed, and the save built a fresh, empty archive over the damaged
  file. Every hero's stash was gone on the next game, silently. Both stash writers now skip a refused stash.
- **P2: left-button F-keys bound to a scroll or a staff on backpack pages 2-10** survive a reload. The keys were decoded
  before the pages loaded, found nothing to cast, and the left keys have no second source. They are decoded again once
  every page is in; the second pass only adds.
- **A refused backpack-page file says so** when a locked page is clicked, instead of "opens at level 0".
- **The hero-select preview's refusal** no longer leaks into a new hero, who skipped clearing the old page file and
  inherited it.

## Fixed: the affix engine

- **P2: "the ages" holds against a durability affix.** One pool let a Rare or Primal carry both, and whichever landed
  second undid the first: the item read "indestructible" and "Dur: 254/254" and wore down. A durability row now leaves an
  indestructible item alone. Keeping the two apart in the roll was tried and dropped: it changes what existing seeds
  rebuild into, which pack_test's golden items caught.
- **P2: the Mystic's offers print as the item each would make.** Printed against the bench, a damage, speed or life-steal
  offer read the bench's own fields: "adds 0 points to damage", "Another ability (NW)", a blank line.
- **P2: reworking a row keeps King's (and Dull's, and Doppelganger's) to-hit.** That half is not in the record, and the
  replay drew it again: reworking any other row moved King's +80% anywhere in 76-100.

## Fixed: Sorcerer and Rogue hovers

- **P2: Leech and Scent of Blood reach the RfA-12 skills.** Scent of Blood marked only what a plain swing wounded, so no
  bow, javelin or spear skill ever marked a monster; Leech healed nothing from Sweep's and Reaping Point's side blows.
- **P2: Nova and Lightning Fury show one ball's damage.** The table multiplied it by five, though only an enemy against
  the caster takes more than one ball.
- **P2: the six RfA-12 cold skills show Cold Mastery's share,** as their strikes deal it and as Ice Bolt's hover already did.

## Fixed: Monk and Necromancer hovers

- **P2: Virulence adds to small poisons.** It worked in whole points, so "+10%" of a 3-a-second poison was dropped:
  Virulence 1-3 added nothing to any rank-1 poison. The dose is now worked in the poison's own 1/64 units, and the hover
  prints a decimal.
- Row and spell-book texts that did not match the code: Vaulting Strike ("your next blow on it"), Seven Reeds and Hundred
  Fists (the first blow is at full damage), Tranquility (2 tiles), Teeth, Bone Splinters (a 4-tile line), Marrow and
  Rigor Mortis (Bone Spirit aside), Amplify Damage (+55% at rank 1) and Frailty (11%), Bone Plating (skeletons, mages and
  golems), the Clay Golem (not slow) and the Blood Golem (no shared life).

## Fixed: vendors

- **P2: Sell All recalculates the hero,** as every other sale does: a sold charm kept giving its life until something else
  recalculated.

## Not changed (open)

- **Sell All sells drop-only valuables** (shards, signets, sealed maps) from page 1, cheapest first, and the buyback list
  keeps only the last 40 sales. Sparing them as socketed items are spared is the user's call.
- **The Magic shelf's refill** on a town visit can push out an item the player already saw, when a refilled earlier slot
  takes its gap. **Buying out the Rare shelf** hides its tab, and its Refresh, for the rest of the game.

- **Vaulting Strike and Leap Attack** land no blow on the vault; the blow needs a second click, which pays again.
- **Stuns and knockbacks that skip uniques** (Mountain Pole, Shoulder Gate, Dragon Tail Sweep, Whirling Kick, Temple
  Bell, Bone Spikes, Bone Prison, Harpoon) do not say so; Dim Vision's hover lacks its "Uniques are not affected" line.
- **Descriptions of Chain Lightning and the Rogue's Lightning Strike** say the bolt leaps between enemies; it fires at every
  enemy within a radius. Lightning Fury bursts around the Rogue, not at the target. Several passives say "spell" or "arrow"
  damage but apply to all non-melee damage.
- **Numbers not shown:** Lightning Bolt's lingering, Blizzard's duration and area, Shiver and Chilling Armor's Ice Bolt,
  Immolation Arrow's fire wall, Frost Nova's radius, Frozen Orb's bolt count, the Valkyrie's 125% volley from rank 10;
  minion stats past 255 are clamped in play but not on the hover.
- **The affix engine:** a Blood craft beside a Rare's own life steal adds a dead row; vendor magic armour shifts its base
  armour once on its first rework; removing Plentiful keeps the extra charges; a Reforge that comes out unique spends that
  unique for the game; the Mystic's lock survives a Cube reroll; Griswold's Magic tab and Adria's plain staves get no base
  tier and skip the rarity gates; rerolls ignore good/evil themes; the ages at the Mystic drops the ethereal flag.
- **Round 28 left:** the stash-close fallback drop during a level change can land the item on the level being left.
