# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 114 (v1.12.400)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 951 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks:**
- a regression review of v1.12.399;
- the Orcl monster features: lesser-unique affixes, variants, scaling, endgame bosses and the monster-side statuses.

## Fixed: regressions in v1.12.399
1. **Weapons Master paid no Rage on a swing's side blows (P2).** Round 113's `!burst` took away the Rage round 69 gave the natural cleave's side blows, and Cleave's and Sweep's. Only a ground pulse (Earthquake, `Rfa12FieldPulseInFlight`) is now refused.
2. **Thunderclap's lightning burst still fired on Whirlwind blows (P2).** `PlayerStrikesMonster` now needs a real lightning range, as `DoAttack` does since round 113.
3. **Blood Craft could cut a Rare roll for a power that never came (P2).** The Rare keeps two of its rolls to make room for the craft's two. A kept "of the leech" made the craft skip its steal, so the item ended a slot short, or with nothing added. The kept rolls now pass over a steal the craft adds, so another roll keeps the slot.
4. **Small fixes:**
   - Sovereign Measure no longer counts a weapon's fire or lightning burst as a second blow.
   - The pooled arrow path passes the burst flag to `Rfa12DamageDealtPercent`.
   - The round-113 test now also checks that a bow skill needs a bow.

## Fixed: monsters
5. **A rift roster boss on an ordinary type could roll a monster variant (P2).** He would take Searing, Voltaic or Glacial (replacing his cold share), Frenzied or Fleet on top of his own speed, Venomous, Unyielding, or a Gilded extra item. `VariantOf` and `RestoreVariantTint` now exclude `FightsAsUnique`.
6. **The vanilla Berserk spell turned such a boss for good, and his escort fell on him (P2).** `AddBerserk` now refuses `FightsAsUnique`, the rule Conversion follows since round 100.
7. **The cries' shared monster loop** skips monsters Conversion turned. War Cry could have killed one, and Howl or Taunt could have turned it back on the hero.
8. **Telekinesis** no longer pushes a stone-cursed monster, which broke Stone Curse early.
9. **Unstable Anomaly's throw** pushes away from the hero (turned first, as Bash and Shove), and spares converted monsters.
10. **Doppelganger** no longer clones a rift roster boss's body.
11. **The health bar's damage line** includes a roster boss's overflow percent.

## For the user (design calls)
- **Torment damage cap (P2 in effect).** Monster damage is a byte: ordinary Torment damage is `(4x+6) × multiplier`, capped at 255. At the default 2.0, any type with a base of 32 or more is already capped. On those types the champion ×1.5, the Dread boss ×2, the escort ×1.2, Might, the Feral and Brutal variants and rift tier scaling add nothing; a Dread Black Knight swings like a plain one. Rift bosses solved this in round 95 with an overflow percent applied per blow. The choice: that pattern for champions and bosses, or wider damage fields with a save-format bump.
- **Thunderous's death burst** is fired as a trap. A death from it reads "a trap", Half Trap Damage halves it, and it strikes the champion's own escort.
- **A lesser unique's level and XP** follow the borrowed unique's home floor, not the floor it stands on. Dread bosses pay ×2 XP for ×8 life.
