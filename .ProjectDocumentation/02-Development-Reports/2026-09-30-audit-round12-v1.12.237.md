# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 12 (v1.12.237)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 12 ran six read-only tracks:
1. every way an item is used;
2. what the hero sheet says against what combat does;
3. reference, pointer and iterator lifetimes;
4. level transitions and portals;
5. vendors (second pass);
6. the hero's death and respawn.

Every finding was verified against the code first. Two findings were P1s.

## Fixed: items in use

- **P1: a Sealed Map used from the belt deleted a backpack item** and stayed on the belt, ready to open the encounter
  again. With an empty backpack it drove the item count to -1. The map now comes off its own list. Maps and keystones
  can no longer go on the belt, and a keystone already on a belt is still spent when the rift is entered.
- **On a gamepad, an oil its target refuses** now stays on the cursor, as it does with the mouse. The pad can also use
  items on backpack pages 2-10.
- **Using the last scroll on pages 2-10** now recalculates the readied scroll list.

## Fixed: the hero sheet against combat

- **A v1.12.236 regression:** a bow with both socketed fire and lightning fired a Spectral Arrow, which dropped both
  elements and the bow's own damage. It now fires one fire arrow whose hit carries the lightning as well. Spectral
  stays for the two Hellfire flags together.
- **The spell table matches the casts:**
  - Chain Lightning showed twice a bolt.
  - Elemental showed twice its halved hit.
  - Guardian showed a roll its Firebolts never use.
  - Healing was one short at both ends.
- **Multiple Shot, Strafe and Guided Arrow** now quote their bonuses, not the bare weapon dice.
- **Attack frames:** a bow's draw skips no frames in Hellfire, and Heavenly Strength's slower swing now shows.
- **Move speed** is now what the feet do, the run and the stride clamp included. Before, a Barbarian with Increased
  Speed read 103% and ran at 167%.
- **Charge's slot** shows its arriving blow (it read a dash), and **Smite** includes Towering Shield.
- **Thorns** shows the aura's and Iron Maiden's share. **Armour pierce** includes the Barbarian's extra eighth.
- **Advanced Stats:**
  - Life steal says it is melee only.
  - Mana per kill is hidden under No Mana.
  - "All resistances zero" now says the difficulty penalty still applies.

## Fixed: death and respawn

- **Respawn In Town recalculated the hero while he was still flagged dead**, so the lit aura counted for nothing. A life
  aura took his 1 life below zero, and he arrived dead in town to a second death screen. The aura's bonuses then stayed
  missing until the next re-equip.
- **"Slain by"** now names only a blow that killed. A blow the hero survived used to leave its source pending, so a
  later death to a burning cross or a Drain Life tick blamed a monster from minutes earlier.
- **The death menu** answers the mouse even when a gold prompt was open at death.
- **During the death animation, before the menu appears,** nothing acts: no Charge on the corpse, and no equip that gives
  the corpse life.
- **A Nephalem Rift closing** no longer carries a dead hero to town.
- **Final Service and the cheat-death saves** leave at least 1 life.

## Fixed: transitions and portals

- **P1: leaving any set level ended a cleared Guardian Rift the hero was not in.** This included an arena exit or the
  Skeleton King's lair. The pile and keystone waiting inside were lost to the next rift. Only the rift's own exit
  counts now.
- **The stash and the Rift Monument menu** close on every level load. A Sealed Map read with the stash open had carried
  the stash into the arena, where it moved items both ways mid-fight.
- **A Sealed Map is refused while a transition is under way.** Otherwise it was spent and the pending portal warp sent
  the hero back.
- **A town portal still standing into an old arena** closes when a new map for that arena is used.

## Fixed: lifetimes

- **A dead monster's missiles took their owner from whatever spawned next in its slot.** The to-hit, a Vampiric heal and
  the death name came from the new occupant, even the hero's own raised skeleton. Missiles now stamp their caster's
  spawn and land as a trap's once it is gone.
- **Telemetry:** a minion's kill clock no longer passes to the slot's next hostile.

## Fixed: vendors

- **Wirt's gambles** came out worn like drops. They are now whole, and a restocked slot must fit beside the rest.
- **Bought charms and tiered bases** lost their effect line and their Tier and Item Level lines for good. Only potions
  are reset now.
- **Adria's guaranteed staves and books, and the Set shelf,** now use the difficulty-lifted item level, as every other
  shelf does.

## Not changed (open)

- **Carried timed-spell missiles** (Etherealize, Cold Armour) keep sprite pointers across a level change. They are
  invisible, so nothing reads them today.
- **Adria still sells Scrolls of Town Portal** in her pinned slot.
- **The sale room check** counts belt and page 2-10 cells. This only matters with a stash at INT_MAX.
- **Adria's Supplies tab** can skip its per-open refresh after an empty Sold tab.
- **Oils and Identify, Repair and Recharge scrolls** are used up when chosen, not when applied. This is vanilla's rule.
- **Design questions:** dying in a Sealed Map arena loses the arena, and timed buffs survive a respawn.
- **Warrior and Barbarian double damage at 99%** is still the user's call.
