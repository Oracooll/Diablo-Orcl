# 2026-10-01 - Audit round 55 (v1.12.291)

**Date:** 2026-10-01. Debug only. 899 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest.

**Tracks:**
- regression review of v1.12.290 (item format 16);
- Paladin and Barbarian bugs (4th pass).

## Fixed

### My v1.12.290 change
- **Shop armour drifted on a rework.** A Mystic or Gillian rework (same seed) moved the base armour of anything bought from Griswold or Wirt. The oil measure now also returns the base's drift from its seed's first draw (`OracoolOilWork::acDrift`). A same-seed rebuild puts that drift back; Reforge, Retier and Ennoble, which roll a fresh base, do not.
- **Ethereal Tempering grew on rebuilds.** `GetItemAttrs` now clears the imbuement ledger, and every rebuild restores it. Make Ethereal had read a stale ledger on a bare base, and a rebuilt ethereal item gained up to +10 durability per 2 shards.
- **The "unknown" oil record is kept.** An older item's -1 survives a save and a load. Before, it became "known, none" after one save. A test covers the round trip.
- **Ethereal armour and Gloomy Shrine:**
  - Make Ethereal scales the oil record with the armour;
  - the Gloomy Shrine's +2 armour is recorded, so rebuilds keep it as they always did.

### Paladin and Barbarian
- **Fist of the Heavens:** the sheet quotes the ring's percent in the ring's lightning colour. The colour is the user's choice; before, the sheet showed the physical centre blast's 150%.
- **Howl and Taunt** cost Rage only when they move or turn something. A lone unique, a champion pack or a petrified crowd took 10 Rage for nothing.
- **Abilities window texts** now match the class tree: Battle Cry, War Cry, Inner Sight, Slow Missiles, Find Potion and Find Item.
- **Held auras** do nothing in town: Tranquility, Weaken, Dirge and Holy Freeze.

## Open, for the user
- **Charge's 10 mana** can buy a dash with no blow when the target dies or the walk breaks. Intended, or refund / start the cooldown?
- **Follow-up swings and Paladin cast percents** (Double Swing, Frenzy) do not scale the Strength part. This is the same open question as rounds 51-52: should Strength be pooled with skill and passive percents, the Diablo II way?
