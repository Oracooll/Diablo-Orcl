## Round 92 (v1.12.341-342)
Auditors: a regression review of v1.12.340, the Rogue's passives (13th pass), and consumables (1st pass).

- **Shock Arrow's arc:** it strikes with `SideBlowInFlight` and no longer rolls or spends the built-up Sharpshooter crit. Deadeye still rolls on the arc, because it holds no state.
- **Grenadier's grenade:** it is marked `Missile::burstShot`, and its hits, the explosion included, run as `ArrowBurstInFlight`.
- **Companions and Dead Ground:** companions no longer take Dead Ground's bonus. The Valkyrie had it on every arrow and never spent its cooldown. Now `IsCompanionBlowInFlight` is also set around `OwnerBlow`'s pool.
- **Gamepad in the class tree:** Y is no longer mapped to Return there, because with the chat line on in single-player it opened a text box a pad cannot type in.
- **Telemetry rotation:** it uses `ReplaceFileAtomically` and stops retrying after a refusal.
- **Debug set pieces:** they clamp to item level 64.
- **Mana potions on a Barbarian:** they are refused (v1.12.342), not drunk into a pool no Rage skill reads.
- **Durability oils:** they clear the Broken mark in the pack and stash.
- **Left open:** a scroll used from pages 2-10 on the gamepad (part of the gamepad package).

## Round 93 (v1.12.344)
Auditors: a regression review of v1.12.341-343, the Paladin's cast skills, and the Barbarian's hidden mana pool.

### Barbarian mana
- **The hidden pool:** a Barbarian had one he could not see. It grew from Magic points, Elixirs of Magic, +Magic and +Mana gear, and every refill. Book spells and Mana Shield ran on it.
- **The fix:** under the user's own Rage rule, `CalcPlrItemVals` now zeroes it for a Rage user, and spell books are refused (kept, not eaten).
- **Gamepad:** the pad's mana button now skips his refused mana potions, so it reaches a Rejuvenation.

### Waypoint ring (regression of v1.12.343)
- The ring now sits on the sigil's rim, inside its row. The viewport had cut off the top of row 0.
- The keys are left alone under Alt (minimap) and with the automap up.

### Paladin
- **Blessed Shield** skips magic-immune bounce targets.
