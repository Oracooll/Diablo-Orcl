# 2026-10-01 - Audit round 48 (v1.12.281)

**Date:** 2026-10-01. Debug only. 897 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest.

**Tracks:**
- regression review of v1.12.280;
- the Necromancer leftovers carried since round 41 (a patch plan, applied);
- rifts (7th pass).

## Fixed

### Necromancer
- **Army panel:** the tally's cap is the one the hero's rank allows (`PanelCap`, via `RaisedCountAtRank`), as Raise and Revive enforce and the tooltip says. It had shown the group's absolute cap.
- **Unholy Offering:**
  - reaches only a minion within 3 tiles of the cursor (`UnholyOfferingReach`) and in the hero's sight. One across the level or behind a wall had been unmade;
  - heals by the life the minion has, not its full life.
- **Raise and Revive** check the corpse with `PeekCorpseNearSeen` and take it only once the body stands. With no free tile, the corpse had been eaten and nothing rose. Revive also checks `CanAddMinionBody`.
- **Life Tap** now covers the Fire Golem's burn and the thorns minions return (`MinionHurts` takes the dealing minion). A minion on its own killing blow is skipped.
- **Bone Storm** follows the hero down the stairs: its field survives the level change, and its art is shown again on the next tick. A new game clears it.
- **Test:** `TheCorpseTableComesBackWithItsLevel` now checks that a peek takes nothing.

### Regression review of v1.12.280
- **Weapon bursts** skip Cadence and Counterstroke (a `burst` flag on `PassiveDamageDealtPercent`). The burst lands after the swing is counted, so the beat had fallen on the wrong blow's burst.
- **Haste:** four clocks, one for each of Hot Pursuit, Tactical Advantage, Illusionist and Fueled by Death. With two, a slower burst could evict a faster one.
- **Sixth Sense's facts line** names cold.
- **Shadow Step and Ride the Lightning** draw their art where the hero landed, not where they aimed.
- **Leap** lands no more than a tile past its reach. The search around the aim had reached up to nine tiles, or through a doorway beside the wall.
- **Comment typo** in missiles.cpp.

### Rifts
- **Sarcophagi and barrels** in a rift raise only the floor's own (scatter) skeleton types. In a Skeleton King rift they had raised the low-floor type loaded for the King, giving floor-1 skeletons at any rung as cheap bar credit.
- **The guardian** rises on a tile the hero can see when one exists. Before, the nearest free tile could be in the next room over a wall.
- **Stalled spawn:** when the monster table is full, the note says to thin the monsters out instead of "move to open floor".
- **The keystone:** a keystone that cannot be laid says so in red.
