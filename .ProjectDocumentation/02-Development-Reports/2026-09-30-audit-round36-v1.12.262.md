# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 36 (v1.12.262)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 36 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.260 and v1.12.261;
2. dungeon generation, set levels and quests;
3. the minion army and companions;
4. the save and load round trip;
5. HUD, mouse and keyboard input;
6. monster stats, scaling and AI.

## Fixed: regressions of round 35

- **P2: the service tabs' worn piece** is matched through the tab's own slot table. Round 35 read the list index as the
  body-slot enum, so only the helm was spared its comparison with itself. There is a new test,
  OracoolAudit.ServiceTabEntriesMapToTheirWornSlot.
- **A burn forgets its strength when it ends,** as the poison does. After the round 35 max rule, a lower-rank Cinder Touch
  later still burned at the old rank.
- **A damage-over-time effect's last part-second strikes again,** as before round 35: with Virulence, 6.25 s dealt 6
  strikes, not 7.
- **Cain's and the buy confirmation's wrapped affix rows** leave room for the "...".
- **Telemetry drops its rows** when the file cannot be opened; before, they piled up in memory.

## Fixed: input

- **P2: a held mouse button** no longer keeps walking, swinging or casting under a window. This includes windows a hotkey
  opened while the button was down, the lower half of the 340-wide side panels, and the floating windows.
- **Escape closes the Cube's recipe book** before the Cube.
- **Space closes a store outright.** It used to step a shop tab back to the vendor's dialog, which stayed up.
- **Alt+Enter** toggles fullscreen with the Rift Monument's menu open. Before, it activated the picked row, which could
  open a rift.
- **The Abilities window closes the crafting book,** as D and B do.
- **The Refresh Until prompt takes the keyboard from chat** (reachable only in Debug builds, where chat exists).
- **A held button on the stash chest** no longer re-opens the stash every frame.

## Fixed: monsters and minions

- **P2: a champion's escort** has 150% of an ordinary monster's life, as meant. PlaceGroup had already doubled it
  (vanilla), and the 150% came on top, to 300%: the champion's own life.
- **The hero's army and a monster Conversion turned** are one side. They fought each other, and a golem's kill of the
  convert paid its experience and loot.
- **The companion regroup watchdog's counters** are cleared with the level: they were statics in CompanionAi that
  outlived it.

## Fixed: items and quests

- **A Cube reroll clears the Mystic's lock** (Reforge, the Retier recipes, Ennoble). The lock kept naming the old roll's
  slot.
- **An old save's broken item** at negative durability loads broken.
- **The Auric Amulet,** a quest's reward, is neither salvaged on pickup nor swept up by Sell All.
- **The Blood Star book** makes room on a full floor before its stone, which it gives once.
- **The unique-drop log** names a rift or a quest level. It read currlevel as a floor there ("Nest -7").

## Not changed (open)

- **Press/release:** the wells, the skill-point frame, the burger menu cells, the Abilities window, the skill picker,
  SORT and the red X buttons act on the press, not on the release inside. This is parked for the user.
- **Input:**
  - Keys that still act while typing in chat (Debug only).
  - Right Ctrl as a click modifier toggles item labels.
  - The wheel is not gated for shop tabs, the quest log or the modal prompts.
  - With the burger menu open, the first click in a window only closes the menu.
- **Monsters:**
  - A variant is re-derived from the option dial at query time, while its stats were fixed at spawn.
  - A fresh spawn's first Stand sprite takes the slot's old size.
  - A Frenzied snake's attack is silent.
  - Six Torment types are immune to all three magic schools (design).
- **Minions:**
  - Bodies can re-form behind a wall.
  - A corpse or the old golem is lost when no tile is free.
- **Saves:**
  - Bench and Cube items are lost on exit when neither pack nor stash can take them.
  - The stash loader trusts page numbers.
  - Class-tree bytes are not capped on load (corruption only).
- **Quests:**
  - Quest-reward uniques (the Optic Amulet and the rest) can be salvaged or sold.
  - A waypoint could sit on a quest entrance.
  - Random farmer choice mirrors the Skeleton King cull.
  - Zone palettes read the set-level id (latent).
