# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 34 (v1.12.259)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word. The RTM play folder was refreshed from a Release
build of v1.12.258 at the user's request during this round.

Round 34 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.258;
2. Paladin and Barbarian code paths (bugs only; both classes are signed off);
3. the Sorcerer's, Rogue's and Monk's RfA-12 actives (fourth pass);
4. shrines and dungeon objects (third pass);
5. the crafting recipes one by one (fifth pass);
6. Wirt, the gamble and the curated shelves (second pass).

## Fixed: regressions of round 33

- **P2: the readied pair's Scroll or Staff choice survives a reload** - the half of round 33's fix that did not take. The
  pair was decoded before the items loaded (no scroll, no staff yet) and then decoded again from the old one-byte field. It
  is now decoded once more after the items are in, as the F-keys are.
- **P2: F1-F8 behind a store do nothing at all.** Round 33's gate let them fall through to the keymapper, where an old
  ini's F-key rows fired behind the store instead.
- **Glass Cannon** takes the flat +damage with the weapon part, as the blow does, and the hero-select damage has it too.
- **The guardian demotion** applies only to a guardian immune to all three schools, and keeps a poison immunity: round 33
  had stripped every single immunity.

## Fixed: skills

- **P2: Cleave and the Monk's Sweep** stand the vanilla axe or staff cleave aside, as Sweeping Reed does since round 26:
  each side enemy took both blows.
- **P2: Holy Fire, Holy Freeze, Holy Shock and Sanctuary** reach only what the Paladin can see, as Static Field since round
  8: they burned and repelled the next room's monsters through the wall.
- **P2: cursor-centred area skills** (Rain of Arrows, Seven-Sided Strike, Wave of Light, Hunter's Claim, the placed fields'
  first gather) take only monsters the hero can see, as the single-target picks since round 15.
- **A Frenzy or Double Swing chain** ends with a stand (a broken weapon), as Zeal's does.
- **A bow skill's kill** no longer lends the arrow flag to the burst it sets off (Ashen Brand, Exploding Palm), and the
  bow scope restores the value it replaced.

## Fixed: shops

- **P2: a unique bought from a shop is the one of the game,** as a found one is: the shelves restored the flags they rolled
  under and nothing set it on the purchase, so the same unique could be bought again and still drop.
- **The gamble's item level** stops at the ladder's top (64); a high-level hero gambled items no other source makes.

## Fixed: shrines and objects

- **P2: a trap's missile spares the hero's own side** - his army and companions - as his own missiles do since 2026-09-27.
  A Nova chest, an Oily shrine's fire wall or a wall trap killed his skeletons and companions.
- **Town furniture needs a hand on it:** Telekinesis opened the Rift Monument's menu, the Cube and the stash chest from
  across town.
- **The Religious shrine** clears the broken flag of a pack item it mends, as a worn one's is cleared.

## Fixed: the Cube

- **P2: Recast and Consecrate keep the item's base tier.** The piece was rebuilt on a new seed and its tier re-rolled from
  it: a Torment piece often came back lower.
- **Reforge** no longer spends a unique's one drop of the game when its roll comes out unique, as Ennoble and the shelves
  already guard.

## Not changed (open)

- **The Unique and Set shelves** are built once per game at the hero's level then, and never grow. The gamble and Wirt's
  item levels follow the hero, not the difficulty. A curated purchase can leave a later item unbuyable. A gamble slot's
  restock keeps the old base and price.
- **Skills:** Storm Crucible and Ride the Lightning strike an 8-way line, not the true line; recasting Funeral Star orphans
  the first; Hunter's Mark reaches every non-melee strike; three bow skills ignore Hunter's Claim; Shadow Step can land past
  a wall; Shiver Armor's retaliation skips Frostbite; a Whirlwind spin can inherit an RfA-12 latch armed mid-spin; Charge's
  mana is kept when its target dies mid-dash; Conversion and the corpse cries have no sight test from the hero; Earthquake
  always pays.
- **The chat gate** trusts the HUD menu, though the chat box can grow over it (developer-only chat).
- **Objects:** explosive barrels and flame traps still strike the army (they call the hit directly); object drops and quest
  objects at the item cap; the Barbarian's hidden mana pool meets mana shrines; negative mana from a -1 Magic (vanilla).
- **The Cube:** Reforge re-rolls the base tier (arguably its job); Make Ethereal halves Tempering's bonus; ties on slot count
  go to the lower recipe; Unbind and Punch Sockets try only the first candidate; a ready recipe that declines is silent;
  salvage does not spare imbued items or quest uniques.
