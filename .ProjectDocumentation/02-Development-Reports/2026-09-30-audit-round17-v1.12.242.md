# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 17 (v1.12.242)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 17 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.241;
2. rifts (third pass);
3. level generation (second pass);
4. the economy (second pass);
5. lighting and vision (second pass);
6. companions (second pass).

Every finding was verified against the code first.

## Fixed: regressions of round 16

- **Making room for a guaranteed drop** now skips everything that never drops at random. The Staff of Lazarus, the
  elixirs and the Guardian Keystone are misc-class and worth 0, so "cheapest first" had made them its first victims.
- **Dead Ground's cooldown** starts only on a strike that lands something.
- **The character sheet and the quest log** back out when the workshop or the Cube refused to close. They no longer
  open invisible under it.
- **The `CalcPlrInvKeepingLife` comment** now says what the function does.

## Fixed: rifts

- **P1: a pickup walk could end on the way home.** A pickup stops one tile short of its item, so a way home laid at
  distance 2, next to the loot ring, was where a walk to a ring item ended. That sent the hero home and ended a cleared
  Guardian Rift with its pile. The way home now lies 3 or more tiles out, with no item beside it.
- **P1: the keystone silently did not drop on a full floor.** It now makes room first, as other guaranteed rewards
  do.
- **A rift's theme rooms** get their share of the monster cap, as a floor's have since round 13.
- **A rift's arrival** gets no theme-room pack spawned on top of it.
- **A cleared Guardian Rift's refusal** says to walk out through its portal. It never closes on its own.

## Fixed: the economy

- **P1: Wirt's ring and amulet gamble minted gold.** Its price skipped the base-tier factor on the false belief that
  jewellery carries no tier. From about level 30 a gamble sold for up to 2.4 times its price, repeatable through the
  free Refresh. Every slot now takes the tier factor.
- **The Set shelf** never sells a piece for less than what it salvages into (three Set Engravings, 2,700). Pieces had
  kept their plain base's value (a helm, 40 gold), and the free salvage paid 675 gold, every game load.

## Fixed: companions

- **A companion that fell just before a level change** stays fallen. Its death sheet never finished, and the next level
  stood it up again. The usual trigger was dying next to it and choosing Respawn.
- **Melee companions** take only a monster within their leash and in sight. They had chosen a monster in the next room,
  or one past the leash, and then stood idle or rocked a tile back and forth.
- **A companion's blow or arrow** no longer steals the hero's focus target. One Talic spin tick had pulled the others
  off the monster the hero was striking.
- **Hunter's Claim** no longer makes the Valkyrie's arrows pass through her own target.

## Fixed: lighting and level generation

- **The light pool is 64**, not 32. Every champion, unique and Luminous monster keeps its light to the end of the
  level, and the default densities left a Torment floor about six free slots for bolts, walls and portals. This
  changes the full game save's light block, which V1 never reads back.
- **A berserked Luminous monster** no longer gets a second light on every revisit.
- **A stun that undoes a walk** brings the monster's light back with it.
- **A Sealed Map's population** is now its own on each entry. Each kind had given the same boss, trait, pack and pile
  for the whole game.

## Not changed (open)

- **The low-life aura swap:** a life bonus swapped off and on at low life still tops life up to the bonus plus 1. A
  proper fix needs a life-debt mechanism.
- **Thunderclap's single-player line** leaves out its 2 lightning.
- **Material ladder values:** Set Engravings (900) are worth more than Unique Encrustments (700). This is a one-time gain
  per material, not a loop.
- **Gillian's reroll on tiered jewellery** may pay back more than it costs. It is suspected, not measured.
- **Companions:** the hero can Teleport onto a companion's tile, and a spinning Talic with blocked steps never regroups.
- **Lights:** a charger's or a knocked-back monster's light lags a tile behind until it walks. These are vanilla paths.
- **Loading a save made inside a rift** doubles the exit portals. V1 never loads a game save.
