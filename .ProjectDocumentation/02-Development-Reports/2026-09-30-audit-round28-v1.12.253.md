# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 28 (v1.12.253)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 28 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.252;
2. resource systems: mana, Rage, Essence and potions (second pass);
3. monster attacks on the hero and the hero's defences (second pass);
4. rules computed in more than one place (second pass);
5. level transitions, portals, waypoints and the Rift Monument (third pass);
6. hero death and respawn (second pass).

Every finding was verified against the code first.

## Fixed: regressions of round 27

- **P2: an old backpack-page file no longer locks pages 2-10 for good.** v1.12.252 refused a page whose count and grid
  disagree, but builds before v1.12.248 saved exactly that shape for an item validation had emptied - the shape the load
  already repairs. The check now runs after the read and skips an emptied record; a real disagreement still refuses.
- **The Monk's staff cleave** stands aside for a sweep that actually fired, asked of the swing itself. Predicting it
  before the swing went wrong when the front hit stole or cost mana.
- **"Game Saved"** shows with the backpack open again (autosaves happen there); it still never prints over a left panel
  or chat.
- **Charm recalculations keep life,** the round 7 rule: banking a life charm could take the hero to 0.
- **Cain's line** puts the level in the requirement list ("Lvl 45") and the class rule in two words, so a long weapon line
  fits the store.

## Fixed: monster attacks

- **P2: a champion's Might reaches every monster missile.** Round 11 routed it through the missiles that read their
  launch damage only. Arrows, elemental arrows, Fireball (Torchants, the Advocate), Lightning Control (Storm Riders, the
  Counselor) and Inferno read the raw damage instead, while the health bar showed the raised range. They also read
  whichever monster held the firing slot; now only the live shooter counts, and a shooter gone lands as a trap's.
- **P2: Weaken lowers an archer's aim,** as it lowers a melee monster's. Only the damage half had reached missiles.
- **The Magma Demon's and Storm Rider's second blows** take the pack's Might and a minion's Frenzy, as the first blow
  does. The Counselor's bolt raises both ends of its range, not the minimum only.
- **Venom from a missile** is resisted once. The shot's resisted damage was poisoned and then resisted again, so at +75
  magic resistance a missile poisoned for a quarter of what melee did.
- **A charge that kills the hero** no longer shoves the corpse off its death mark.
- A reduction past -100% can never heal (not reachable today; a guard).

## Fixed: resources

- **Whirlwind's first second costs its 5 Rage once.** The start paid it and the drain began at once, so the first second
  cost 10. Each point the spin drains now counts as spent, so Bloodthirst returns it as life.
- **Short of Rage or Essence, the hero says "I can't do that",** not "Not enough mana". A Weapon Throw short of Rage says
  it once, not at the held button's repeat rate.
- **Seismic Slam on an empty line** costs nothing, as Rend and Ground Stomp.
- **Essence resets on respawn,** as Rage does.
- **A second gradual potion heals faster,** not longer: each potion added a further second of drip at one potion's rate.

## Fixed: rules computed twice

- **P2: the spell book readies a learned spell before a staff's charges,** as the F-keys, the saved wells and the skill
  picker do. The row click readied the staff, an F-key from the same row cast for mana, and a reload quietly changed the
  well to mana.
- **P2: Gillian lends a charm that Rework Charms takes.** She lent the first charm of any family, so a Charm of Salvaging
  first in the pack hid a Vigor behind it and the recipe never lit.
- **Items dropped by hand** stay off a rift's way home and arrival exit, as loot already does. Walking back for one ended
  on the exit and sent the hero home.
- **A held item** reads usable or red with the spellbook rule, as the backpack's items do.
- **Gillian's Reroll tab** hides an unidentified item's affixes, as its panel does.

## Fixed: death and respawn

- **A Leap or Teleport launched on the tick the hero dies** no longer carries the corpse, its light and the camera.
- **The warcries' buffs end on respawn.** Battle Orders' life rode into town on a running clock.
- A death is logged once even if a future path reaches the kill twice (a guard).

## Fixed: transitions

- **A rift's way home is never laid on a standing Town Portal.** The portal ran first and took the hero to town, the way
  home was never walked, and the Monument refused a new rift until he walked back out through it.
- **The Cube's salvage hammer ends at a level change** even when the Cube refused to close (pack and stash full). Still
  armed, it turned a later Item Repair click into a salvage.

## Not changed (open)

- **Waypoint loading screens** use the stair art: a waypoint to level 1 shows Tristram's screen, to 16 the Diablo gate.
- **The right well** lost Charge's fallback plate (only if the Paladin art is missing), and a spell loaded with an invalid
  type draws grey rather than red.

- **Companions (Valkyrie, the Ancients, Spirit Guardian, Decoy) survive the hero's death** and walk back in after the
  respawn. The army is dismissed at death, D2-style; whether companions should be too is the user's call (it was already
  on the open list).
- **RfA-12 buffs and passive clocks** also ride the respawn; clearing them needs a narrower reset than the one a new game
  uses. On a dead hero some effects keep running (curses, Army of the Dead) and some freeze (burns, poisons).
- **On-hit effects read the blow before absorption** (Vampiric and Devouring heals, venom, Iron Maiden): a Bone Armor that
  took the whole blow still feeds a Devouring boss. Needs ApplyPlrDamage to report what was lost.
- **The melee elemental share** can fall under the "a quarter always lands" floor with a stacked physical reduction.
- **Self-inflicted costs** (Blood Star, Bone Spirit, Sacrifice, Peril, Drain Life, the Rage restore) go through the
  incoming-damage defences.
- **A charge still staggers a hero it missed** (a dodge, a block, Etherealize) - vanilla for blocks.
- **The Thunderous champion's death burst** counts as a trap (Half Trap Damage, "killed by a trap", hits the army). Diablo's
  Apocalypse and a monster Charged Bolt ignore difficulty (vanilla).
- **The workshop's service buttons** light when the click would refuse (a locked row, an unidentified item, no shard to
  cleanse). Hand-picked salvage destroys a white with empty sockets, which bulk salvage spares.
- **A spell's cost is taken at the cast frame without a recheck** (vanilla): Mana Shield absorbing a blow mid-cast can
  leave mana below zero.
- **The Paladin's mana costs** live in two tables that agree today with no test pinning them.
