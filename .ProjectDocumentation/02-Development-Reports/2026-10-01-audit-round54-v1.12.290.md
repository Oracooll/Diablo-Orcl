## Fixed

### Phantom oil armour on shop armour
`MeasureOracoolOilWork` took the oils' armour as the item's armour minus a rebuild of its seed. That assumed the base armour was the seed's first draw, which holds for drops. Griswold's two shelves and Wirt draw the base index first, so a bought armour showed a fake "oil" difference.

Every Cube rebuild (Reforge, Retier, Ennoble, the crafts, the reroll recipes) put a new base on a new roll and added that difference, up to about ±60 armour on a Torment base, and kept it from then on.

The oils' armour is now recorded on the item:
- **The field:** `Item::_iOracoolOilAC`, an int16 gated tail in item format 16.
- **Where it changes:**
  - Hardening and Imperviousness add to it;
  - `GetItemAttrs` zeroes it;
  - `ReapplyOracoolOilWork` sets it after a rebuild;
  - the measure reads it.
- **Older items** load it as -1 (unknown) and keep the old difference.

### Other fixes
- **Make Ethereal** halves the base durability only, not Tempering's shards. Cleanse took the shards back whole, so ten maximum durability per shard was lost for good.
- **Wirt's gamble restock** stamps CF_BOY only on vanilla bases, as the stock and the result already do.
- **Bone Storm:** a recast at a lower rank keeps the stronger storm.
- **Revisited floors:** `RestoreUniqueCorpsesAfterLoad` forgets the records of the bodies it removes.
- **Test:** LoadSaveItemFormats now covers formats 16, 15, 14 and 13.

## Open, for the user
- **Uniques below their level.** A vanilla unique can drop on an item whose level is below the unique's minimum (chests roll at twice the level, unique monsters at +4), and it then asks for a higher level than where it was found. The Cube already gates on item level. Gating the drops too would change what fresh seeds generate; it can be fresh-generation-only, as the affix draw is.
- **Required level of crafted rares.** It can read slightly low when a merged craft value falls between two affix rows.
