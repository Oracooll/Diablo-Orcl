# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 81 (v1.12.329)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit 61b09286.

Three read-only auditors ran:
- a regression review of v1.12.328;
- options, the ini file, sounds and music (1st pass);
- Paladin auras, Charge, Zeal and the ranged skills.

## Paladin
- **Zeal's missed opener** is closed. It had been open since round 60.
  - Before: the burst started only when the opener landed, so a miss was a sped-up swing with nothing after it. With the button held, the Paladin swung free fast swings until one landed.
  - Now: `StartZealChainAtSwing` arms the burst at the opener's hit frame, landed or not. Mana is still paid per landed blow.
- **Charge** does not launch at a target behind a wall. The dash ran out on the long way round: the 10 mana was paid, the blow was plain, no cooldown started, and the next click paid again. It now swings at walking pace, as when out of mana.
- **Radiance's undead burn** stops at walls.
- **Conviction's immunity break** keeps poison (acid) immunity, as the other two demotion sites do.
- **Prayer and Meditation text:** they now say "while it burns". They work standing as well as walking.

## Sounds
- **Frozen Orb:** a cast no longer rings Ice Bolt's cast cue about thirteen times. Its shed bolts now launch silent.
- **Cold spells with a delivered cue** no longer also play the generic `IS_CAST2` chime. This follows the standing rule: a delivered cue replaces it and never stacks.
- **Options, ini and music:** checked and clean.
  - All 71 Orcl keys round-trip.
  - Keymapping is kept.
  - Every sound in the table resolves to a vanilla file.
  - Music is recomputed on every level load.

## Regression review of v1.12.328
- **Blood and Blind books:** a book that still owes its reward makes room before the quest moves, and refuses on a floor it cannot clear.
  - Before, on a floor full of items `MakeRoom` will not take, the quest advanced, the spawn failed, and the Blood Stone or the Optic Amulet was gone for good.
- **Pedestal:** it makes room only when the hero holds a stone. An empty-handed click on a full floor had deleted a floor item each time.
- **Mushroom patch:** it makes room only while unopened.
- **Freezing Arrow:** its cold-immunity test is removed. The engine has no cold immunity, so the test was dead code.
