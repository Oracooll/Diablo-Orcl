# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 88 (v1.12.337)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit 14426f55.

Three read-only auditors ran:
- a regression review of v1.12.336 (one consistency fix);
- stash and backpack-page persistence (1st pass);
- the companions' numbers and tooltips.

## Companions
- **Monsters' odds against companions:** they now use the same hero-style formula as the army: +30, plus the level gap, less armour, clamped to 5-95. A new `CompanionOwner` gives the level gap.
  - Before, companions were struck at the monster's raw to-hit. On Normal a Decoy or Spirit Guardian was missed 70-90% of the time; on Hell every blow landed.
- **Regeneration:** companion bodies no longer regenerate as monsters. The Golem slot's monster level made a Decoy heal by difficulty and Torment multiplier: about 2 life a second on Normal, about 25 on Torment x5. None of this was stated anywhere.
- **Melee companions** (Korlic, Talic, the Spirit Guardian) now take the damage passives' pool and Triple Demon, as the Valkyrie's arrows and Madawc's hammers already did.
- **The Valkyrie's text:** it now says "elemental resistances reach 90%". Her physical cap is 50%.
- **Checked and clean:**
  - every rank grows;
  - no uint8 overflow;
  - no double scaling;
  - XP goes to the hero;
  - the hero's spells spare them.

## Persistence
- **Backpack pages file:** a file with bytes left after the last page is refused instead of loaded. A torn tail, or pages a later build dropped, had been accepted, and the next autosave then wrote the shorter file over those items.
  - Checked safe: the writer's size is exact, and the codec decodes to the exact written length.
- **Stash page numbers:** a page index of 100 or more is refused. It had loaded onto a page nothing shows or scans.
- **Leaving with items in the Cube or workshop:** when they cannot be handed back, the exit now says they will not be saved. It had said they "keep what they hold", then lost them.
- **Checked and clean:**
  - a refused file is never written over;
  - newer item formats are refused;
  - stash gold is clamped;
  - the stash is shared and the pages are per hero;
  - autosave waits for an empty hand and no level change;
  - the publish order.
