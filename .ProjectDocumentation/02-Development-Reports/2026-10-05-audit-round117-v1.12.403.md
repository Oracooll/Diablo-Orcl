## Fixed: regression in v1.12.402
1. **Round 116's re-aim at a level save did nothing (P2).** The level save runs while the hero is mid level change. `UpdateEnemy`'s player loop skips him, and that loop is the one place that clears `MFLAG_TARGETS_MONSTER`, so the released convert or confused monster stayed aimed at its packmate. New `AimAtHeroForLevelSave` aims the released monster, and everything that hunted it, at the hero by hand.
   - **Test:** `OracoolAudit117.ALevelSaveReleaseAimsTheHuntersAtTheHero`.

## Fixed: generation
2. **Rifts and Sealed Map arenas probably repeated (P1, from the code; one in-game check would confirm).** Both seeds were drawn in town from the game's generator, which every town load reseeds to the same value. The next rift after a return repeated the last one's boss, tileset and map. A second map of a kind repeated its boss, trait, pack and pile, quietly undoing round 17's fix. Both seeds now mix in the clock and a running count.

## For the user (design calls)
- **Dying in a Sealed Map arena loses the map:** it was spent on entry, and a respawn lands in town. Rifts keep the gate lit after a death. A choice: remember the open arena and let a re-entry skip the map until its boss falls, or refund the map on a respawn while the boss lives.
- **An arena with no room for its boss** (no unseen tile) still spends the map. Only a log warning says so.
- **Random rift objects** (barrels, chests, shrines, traps) can land beside the arrival. The hero is stepped aside, so nothing is blocked.
- **The debug `/arena` command** reuses the last game's seed.
- **A rift landing walled in on all eight sides** lays no exit on foot. Town Portal still works.
