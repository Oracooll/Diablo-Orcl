# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 32 (v1.12.257)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 32 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.256, the one-window Escape included;
2. UI hit-testing against drawing (second pass);
3. Hellfire content (third pass);
4. monster AI by type (fourth pass);
5. lighting, vision and the automap (third pass);
6. sound and music (second pass).

It also closes the three bounds items round 31 carried.

## Fixed: regressions of round 31

- **P2: the companion and army panels answer only where they are seen.** v1.12.256 made the whole panel take clicks, and in
  town it lies under the Cube, the workshop and a store page, none of which is a left-panel window. Clicks on the Cube's
  top-left cells did nothing. The panels now stand aside under those pages too (the UI track's P2 of the same shape).
- **The game menu and death** sweep every window past a bench that refuses to close (a full pack and stash). The one-window
  loop stopped at it and left the rest open.
- **A stash closed with an item in hand** puts it away (belt for a potion, pack, stash) before the floor: closed by a level
  change, it had dropped the item on the new floor, a rift's or an arena's among them.

## Fixed: the HUD and windows

- **P2: with chat open, the hidden HUD takes no clicks.** The Portal cell cast a Town Portal, the left well opened a skill
  picker nobody could see, and the points frame opened the sheet.
- **P2: the hero sheet never opens over a store.** The level-up icon's path opened it over the shop, whose hidden page took
  the clicks under it (a right click bought an item). The C key and the HUD menu already refused.
- **The quest log** follows the Panel Docking option like every other 340x720 panel.

## Fixed: monster AI

- **P2: a repel no longer heals gargoyles.** Howl, Grim Ward and Blinding Flash sent a healthy gargoyle into its low-life
  retreat, which ends in a full heal; inside a Grim Ward it looped heal and swoop. A gargoyle runs its own retreat only below
  half life now.
- **P2: a Counselor in a Grim Ward** takes an ordinary step back instead of an endless fade-in. Its own retreat fades out
  first, so it is its own only while hidden; a visible one went straight to a fade-in, cast nothing, and was unhittable for
  the ward's whole length.
- **P2: a charge no longer pushes a walking target,** whose walk then ended relative to the pushed tile - into a wall -
  and left an invisible blocking tile behind.

## Fixed: sound

- **P2: a held refusal speaks once.** Seven refusals (the Paladin's cast skills short of mana or missiles, the shield rule,
  a failed Leap, the price checks) left the hold running, and the line replayed for as long as the button was down.
- **P2: a rift's Butcher** greets once; his arrival line was cut off and restarted, as Na-Krul's was until round 23.
- **The level's music returns after a movie** (the Lazarus cutscene); music_stop forgot the track (also in vanilla).
- **The HUD menu's Game Menu** stops a quest text's narration with its text.

## Fixed: bounds (carried from round 31)

- **The sprite-mix disk cache is checked for structure** - every list, sprite offset and sprite header inside the buffer -
  and a damaged file is recomputed rather than read past its end.
- The hero file's level clamp stays inside the visited array, and the dead game-save path writes hotkeys wider than a byte.

## Fixed: lighting and the mini-map

- **P2: mini-map markers sit on their tiles.** Waypoints, Town Portals, the Rift Monument, Search's diamonds and the hero's
  square were placed with a per-tile pitch that floors differently from the tile grid's: at the default scale they sat
  about four tiles short, and at the lowest zoom every marker sat on the hero.
- **A burning flame-trap line gets its lights back on a revisit.** It kept a stale light id and resized, then freed,
  whatever light held that slot - a champion's glow pulsed with the fire. A trap's freed light is forgotten too.
- **A glowing monster killed mid-step** keeps its light on its corpse, not a tile away.
- The light-radius assert catches the one-past-the-end radius.

## Fixed: Hellfire

- **P2: a Guardian Rift's Na-Krul resists fire, lightning and magic instead of being immune.** On floor 24 the books weaken
  him; a rift has none, so half of all Guardian Rifts fielded a boss a fire, lightning or magic caster could not touch
  inside a fifteen-minute clock.
- **Hork Spawn** no longer fills rift rosters: a summon that drops nothing, it filled the kill bar for no loot.
- **Unstable Anomaly** spares a petrified monster, as every other knockback does; it broke Stone Curse early.

## Not changed (open)

- **Traps** hit by the raw floor number, not the area ladder: the Crypt's traps hit harder than Hell's, and every rift's
  like Caves 9-10 at any tier (vanilla behaviour; the items moved to the ladder).
- **Hive and Crypt floors** are numbered three ways (waypoint list, automap, a bare stair tooltip in the Nest).
- **A waypoint warp** reveals the up-stairs area on the automap before the hero is moved to the sigil. **Corpse lights**
  are not restored on a revisit. The mini-map shows a waypoint marker before the sigil is found (maybe intended).

- **At 960x720 the two points frames** (level-up and unspent skill points) cover part of a panel cell each: the stash's
  last cell of its bottom row and the backpack's bottom-left cell. The frames are drawn over the panels and take those
  clicks. Hiding them when a window is open would go against the user's 2026-08-28 note ("dont hide ... when windows are
  open"); moving them is a layout call. The user's call.
- **Hover tooltips** of a docked page can show under the HUD where the click goes to the HUD. The orbs' boxes are larger
  than their drawn shapes in two cases. The plateless row's box is taller than its cells. The chat box's click band is the
  whole 640x128 strip.
- **Attract** does not work on ranged monsters (their shots pass through the lure). Conversion and Confuse ending re-aim
  no one. Stone Curse can still be broken by the gargoyle lift and the leader paths (vanilla). Frenzied snakes skip
  their attack sound.
- **Two sounds** on readying a skill over a lit aura (the aura's stop cue and the select sound).
- **Escape** spends a press on a transient centre message before any window. The event log now closes with the game menu
  and death every time.
