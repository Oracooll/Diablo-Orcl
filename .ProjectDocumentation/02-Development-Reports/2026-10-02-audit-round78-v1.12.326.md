# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 78 (v1.12.326)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit b88e1708.

Three read-only auditors ran. The regression review of v1.12.325 found nothing. The other two covered the Sorcerer's cold and arcane skills (1st pass) and the hero sheet, stats and XP (1st pass).

## Sorcerer, cold and arcane
- **Chilling Armor:** it no longer fires a free Ice Bolt on every tick of a monster's Inferno or Lightning. Those strike on each tick they overlap the hero, up to twenty bolts a second. The acid puddle was already excluded.
- **Blizzard:** it refuses, unpaid, a cast onto or past a wall, and its shards need sight of the centre. It is a vanilla missile, so the RfA-12 field refusals never covered it. It rained on closed rooms and through thin walls.
- **Glacial Spike's shatter:** it spares companions, minions and converted monsters, as Freezing Arrow does.
- **Teleport's text:** it now says it goes through walls, which is what the engine's Teleport does. The behaviour is kept and the text corrected.

## Hero numbers
These now recalculate with `CalcPlrInvKeepingLife`, so a lost life bonus at low life no longer kills the hero:
- **Passive slots:** changing a passive slot, for example unslotting Finery when its Strength kept +life armour usable.
- **Heavenly Strength:** its shield going to the pack.
- **Adria's respec:** in town.
