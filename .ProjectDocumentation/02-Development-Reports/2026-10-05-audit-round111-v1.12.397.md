# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 111 (v1.12.396-397)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 950 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks:**

## Fixed: regressions in v1.12.395
1. **The reward keystone through the Cube grid or the workshop bench (P2).** Round 109 dropped the window check, saying the cursor keeps the reward. Round 110 stopped the cursor counting outside a level change, so a reward clicked into the grid was never settled, and the refund minted a second one. The grid, the bench and the craft grid are now searched for the reward's seed (`LevskiGridHoldsKeystoneSeed`, `WorkshopHoldsKeystoneSeed`).
2. **"Fastest wins" missed Swift Harvesting and slowed arrows.** Swift Harvesting added its fast-attack flag after the clear. On a bow the speed flags add arrow speed, and Fastest plus Fast was faster there. The clear now runs after Swift Harvesting and after the weapon graphic is set, and never for a bow.
3. **Cast weapon blows counted once per target.** One Thousand Reeds on three foes filled a Mythic Rhythm charge. They now strike through `SideBlow`: the first landed blow counts, and the rest are side blows.

## Fixed: Rogue and Necromancer
4. **Reaping Point (P2)** struck the monster beyond a wall or a closed door, and swung with no target. It now needs a target and a clear line (`LineStepBlocked`, Holy Lance's rule).
5. **Teeth and Bone Splinters (P2)** were paid when they reached only magic-immune monsters, as most undead are. A tooth on a magic-immune monster no longer counts, and Bone Splinters passes them over (Phantom Volley's rule).
6. **The curses' "uniques do not"** now use `SparedAsUnique` (`FightsAsUnique` or a lesser affix). *Correction (round 112):* this is defensive only. Every lesser unique already counts as unique (`PrepareUniqueMonst` sets `uniqueType`), so the Frailty, Terror and Confuse cases described here could not happen.
7. **Poison Javelin** passes over acid-immune monsters, whose pool and price came for nothing.
8. **Turning Pike** is paid only for a landed blow or a pivot made.
9. **The javelin pool and plague cloud pulses** strike as bursts, as Shock Arrow's arc does since round 92. Each pulse on each monster had rolled and spent Sharpshooter's crit and Dead Ground's bonus.
10. **Text:**
   - Shadow Step steps to the cursor (no enemy needed).
   - Hunter's Mark is for "ranged hits", as its fact line says.

## For the user

## Reported, not coded (design calls)
- The javelin and spear throws work with a bow and roll its damage. Diablo II needs a javelin.
- Poison Explosion's cloud is visual only; the poison is applied once.
- Eternal Torment curses end on leaving the level and coming back.
- Area casts are paid on an empty area for some skills (Rain of Arrows, Bone Spear, Novas), while Phantom Volley and Blinding Flash refuse.
- Vault's hover does not show its three charges and 15 s cooldown.
- Strafe under Hunter's Claim aims at monsters its arrows then pass.
- Combination Strike and Mythic Rhythm credit the armed swing skill, not a cast weapon blow.
