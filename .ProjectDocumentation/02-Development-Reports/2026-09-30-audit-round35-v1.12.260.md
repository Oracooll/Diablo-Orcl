# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 35 (v1.12.260)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 35 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.259;
2. item tooltips (fifth pass);
3. performance hazards (second pass);
4. the class trees, investment and refunds (third pass);
5. status effects across all sources (fourth pass);
6. town and vendor dialog flow (third pass).

## Fixed: regressions of round 34

- **P1: a trap's missile still struck the hero's army.** Round 34's exception was one side of an "or", so a trap that met
  a skeleton or a companion fell through to the monster-faction test, which a trap passes against the army. It also
  read `Monsters[-1]` for a berserked golem slot. The trap case is now exclusive.
- **The cast's sight gate** no longer reaches Arc's and Chi Wave's hops, or the bursts a death sets off (Ashen Brand,
  Exploding Palm). A hop sees from the monster it struck, and a burst sees from the body.
- **Glass Cannon with a bow:** the sheet and hero-select add the Strength part before the percent, as the arrow does.

## Fixed: status effects

- **P2: a poison, burn, bleed or elegy renewed faster than once a second never struck.** The pulse came when the time
  left reached a whole second, and every refresh pushed it back up. Each effect now keeps its own second. The
  Necromancer's Poison Dagger swings, Cinder Touch spam, repeated Barbed Shaft or Rend bleeds and Deep Wounds on
  consecutive swings all deal their damage now.
- **Cinder Touch and Deep Wounds** take the longer and stronger of the old and new effect, as the poison always did.
  Deep Wounds cut a longer Rend bleed short.
- **Unstable Anomaly's throw** spares a monster that cannot be hit (a charge in flight, a quest speaker mid-talk), as
  every other area knockback does.

## Fixed: stores

- **The mouse's back button** closes the Refresh Until prompt first, as Escape does. It left the prompt on screen over no
  shop, and Enter there reopened Griswold anywhere.
- **The shop grid keeps its cursor** after a purchase, a No Room or a No Money, as its comment promised. It jumped back
  to the first item.
- **Adria's Supplies tab** is always rebuilt. Coming from an empty Sold tab it skipped the book levels and the usability
  tint.
- **A store is closed on a level change** with the other town windows (hardening; reachable only through the debug
  console).
- **The Adria respec** schedules the save, as the Abilities window's refunds do, and puts the aura's loop out through
  its tracker.

## Fixed: tooltips

- **P2: the Repair, Recharge and Identify tabs** no longer compare a worn piece with itself. The tab lists copies, so
  the "is this the worn item" test never matched.
- **P2: Cain's result and the buy confirmation** wrap a long affix list over up to four rows, where a five- or six-affix
  item ran over the requirements line. A vanilla unique says "Unique Item" there again.
- **The card's subtitle** is only ever an equipment type line: a potion's item level and an oil's first description
  line were taken for one.
- **A charm in the stash or the Cube** says it is inactive there.
- **The keystone tier** reads as the rift reads it (tier 1 at least).
- **A Zod-socketed item** says Indestructible once.
- **The repair hammer over a damaged ethereal** says no smith repairs it, not "Needs no repair".
- **Stash items** show the hammer's or Adria's fee, as backpack items do.

## Fixed: performance

- **Telemetry** writes its rows once a second, on a death and on exit, not by opening and closing the file per kill: an
  area skill killing thirty monsters opened the file thirty times in one tick. A crash loses a second of rows at most.
- **The runeword book** caches each entry's height. It formatted every word's lines every frame to count them.

## Not changed (open)

- **Gamepad:** in a shop grid the pad moves the inventory cursor, and A buys the highlighted shop item with no
  confirmation. The gamepad pass is parked for the user.
- **Performance:**
  - Companions load their sprite sheets on the cast: Call of the Ancients has a 20-80 ms hitch.
  - `PrewarmPlayerLook` copies whole sheets to discard them.
  - The sprite-mix memory cache never evicts.
  - Tooltip comparison cards are rebuilt every frame.
  - Recipe lists wrap every frame.
  - Sell All is O(n^2).
  - Monster scaling runs on first draw.
  - The rift way-home search can run hundreds of pathfinds.
  - Luminous monsters hold their light for the level (by design).
- **Status effects:**
  - A skill stun counts down only on the ticks a chilled monster acts, and pauses while it is frozen.
  - Stone Curse pauses chill and freeze.
  - Champions can be frozen but not stunned (FightsAsUnique against ShrugsOff).
  - Decrepify's slow ends after 55 minutes under Eternal Torment.
  - A curse's release clears chill and debuffs from other sources.
- **Class trees:**
  - The over-the-cap load migration knows only the global cap.
  - Effects cast at rank N outlive a refund (D2-like).
  - Five dead investment functions remain.
- **Tooltips:**
  - `PrintItemDur` omits the ethereal, broken and socket lines on unidentified items.
  - The bow speed note follows the wielded weapon.
  - Workshop hovers show only the name.
  - The loose stone level line ignores Ease.
- **Stores:** Escape on Wirt's tabs closes the store, while other vendors step back. The respec has no Yes/No step.
