## 1. The gold hint card on the skill menus and the HUD

The vendors' card (`oracool::ShowPanelStringsAsHintCard`, cursor_tooltip.h) is called after the panel strings are built in:
- **`oracool/skill_picker.cpp`:** the LMB/RMB skill menus;
- **`panels/spell_list.cpp`:** the vanilla spell list;
- **`control.cpp` `CheckPanelInfo`:** all six HUD hovers (the menu icons, Menu Bar, Town Portal, Run toggle, LMB well, RMB well).

The text is unchanged; only the frame is the card.

## 2. Selecting a burning aura keeps it lit

`ToggleClassAura` is now `SelectClassAura` (class_tree.cpp/.h, skill_picker.cpp, spell_book.cpp). Selecting the aura that is already burning returns true and changes nothing. An aura goes out when the right button is given another skill (`ClearClassAuraForRightButton`) or another aura is lit.

## 3. The Transmute button's card

In `oracool/levski_roar.cpp`, the Transmute button (the Cube's painted diamond and the Transmute plate) builds a card titled "Transmute":
- **Ready** (the picked recipe, or `FirstReadyLevskiRecipeFor` on the grid): "Makes: <name>" in gold.
- **Picked, not ready:** the recipe in red and its inputs.
- **Nothing:** how to begin.

## 4. Votive Strike's impact

In `oracool/rfa12_actives.cpp`, `VotiveBurst` puts vanilla's Holy Bolt explosion where the swing lands. It is:
- scaled to 50% (`ScaleClxList`, cached);
- lifted by half the lost height, so it stays centred;
- tinted infrared (`Tint::Hue`, `Rgb(255, 56, 32)`).

This follows the chat follow-up "scale down the holy bolt explosion to 50% size and tint infrared". ChatGPT's votive_strike sheet is no longer drawn.
