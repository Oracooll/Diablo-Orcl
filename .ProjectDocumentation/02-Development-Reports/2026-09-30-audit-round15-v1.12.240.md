# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 15 (v1.12.240)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 15 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.239;
2. monster AI, one AI type at a time;
3. the Sorcerer's and the Rogue's active skills (third pass);
4. the Monk's and the Necromancer's active skills (third pass);
5. every quest end to end in single player;
6. the save round-trip of everything rounds 2-14 touched.

Every finding was verified against the code first. The regression review found no P1 or P2 problems in v1.12.239.

## Fixed: monster AI

- **P1: a stun that landed mid-step left phantom tiles in `dMonster`.** The phantom blocked the hero. Sideways, it left a
  body that missiles passed through, beside a phantom that caught blows for it. A stun now undoes the walk first, as a
  flinch does. It also skips stone and a charge in flight.
- **Howl, Grim Ward, Sanctuary and Blinding Flash set a retreat most AIs never read.** Only the Fallen, bats,
  gargoyles, the Sneak and the Counselors ever acted on it. Other monsters froze at some ranges or walked the wrong way,
  never cleared the goal, and lost their pathfinding for good. One shared step now handles it for those AIs, and clears
  the goal when the steps run out.
- **A snake's pattern index** is guarded. A Howl's step count read past its array.
- **A monster's blow on a minion or companion** must be within reach. A regrouped companion took blows from across the
  map.
- **The FireMan AI slot** had a null function, which the debug spawn command could call.

## Fixed: the classes' skills

- **The six RfA-12 cold skills get Cold Mastery**: its share, and the resistance it pierces.
- **The RfA-12 skills get the RfA-12 damage-dealt half** (Hunter's Mark, Judgment, Dead Ground, Deadeye). Round 13
  had brought over only the passives' half.
- **An RfA-12 cast now picks only a target the hero can see.** A shot or a curse clicked beside a wall had struck the
  monster behind it.
- **A standing Command the Dead** holds, instead of following the hero's next blow on anything.
- **Soul Harvest, Death Mark, Bane and Army of the Dead** get the damage-dealt passives. Iron Maiden returns the
  monster's own blow, so it does not.
- **Leaping Crane's landing and Exploding Palm's burst** are melee weapon blows again.

## Fixed: gaps from the regression review

- **Placed shrines in a rift** use the rift's floor, as the goat shrine and the cauldron already did.
- **The affix floor cap of 25** now applies to jewellery only. On other gear it had let level-25 rows onto ilvl-90
  weapons.
- **Life reaching zero through a recalculation** (gear taken off at low life) now gets the cheat-death saves and Final
  Service before it kills. It now also takes the army.

## Fixed: saves

- **A stash file the game could not read stayed usable in memory.** Gold and items went into a stash that is never
  written back, and were lost at exit. That stash is now locked: gold goes to the backpack, and the chest stays shut
  with a message.
- **`LoadMonster` resets `spawnSerial`**, so its comment is true.

## Fixed: quests

- **P1: the Black Mushroom's Brain could be lost for good.** It dropped in a rift or a Sealed Map arena, both of which
  are thrown away, and the quest state had already moved on, so no second Brain ever came. It now drops only on a
  floor that is kept. On a full floor it makes room instead of never existing.
- **Guaranteed quest drops at the 127-item cap** make room first. This covers Theodore, the Cathedral Map, the quest
  uniques and Lachdanan's Steel Veil. Before, the quest advanced while the item vanished.
- **Celia** appears after any Nest floor, not only floor 17. A waypoint straight to 18-20 had left her out of town.
- **The controller's trigger search** ignores set levels, as the mouse does.

## Not changed (open)

- **The RfA-12 bow skills are not "arrows"** to the Rogue's on-hit passives (Night Stalker, Archery, Leech). A design
  call.
- **Damage-over-time ticks** follow three different rules for passives, flinch and procs. A design call.
- **The readied slot shows "-"** for every RfA-12 cast of four classes. It needs a damage-range query, as the Paladin
  has.
- **Wheel of Heaven and Sweeping Reed** strike with the bare weapon dice.
- **Storm Crucible and Ride the Lightning** strike along an 8-direction line.
- **Firestorm** launches from the hero.
- **Frozen Sentinel** fires into walls.
- **Minor Necromancer items:**
  - Bone Prison holds 4.25 s instead of 3.
  - Teeth's last tooth never lands.
  - Serration counts the hero's distance, not the missile's flight.
  - Decompose is paid for on an immune monster.
  - Bone Storm ends on the stairs.
  - Unholy Offering heals from a minion's maximum life.
- **Tragedy's share** skips the on-hit procs along with the damage passives.
- **Companions** survive the hero's death, unlike the army. **The user's call.**
- **Reforge** rolls a fresh base tier, by design.
- **Decay weapons saved before v1.12.239** show their first roll in the tooltip.
- **A converted monster** targets the hero's golem.
- **A waypoint sigil** can hide an item lying under it.
- **Quest special treasure** misses the unique +3.
