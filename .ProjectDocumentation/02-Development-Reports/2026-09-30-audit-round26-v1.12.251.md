# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 26 (v1.12.251)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 26 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.250;
2. the Sorcerer, Rogue, Monk and Necromancer against everything changed in rounds 18-25;
3. rifts (fourth pass);
4. crafting, the Cube and the workshop (fourth pass);
5. towners and quests (second pass);
6. shrines and dungeon objects (second pass).

Every finding was verified against the code first.

## Fixed: regressions of round 25

- **Dim Vision** also spares a monster mid-fade: a Counselor cursed during its fade-out went hidden with no way back.
- **Closing the window** (the X, Alt+F4, a bare quit) and leaving through the front end save the options: hotkey-changed
  settings (game speed, zoom, gamma, item labels) are written only when a menu closes since v1.12.250. The window close
  goes through `diablo_quit`, which saves them once the ini has been loaded.
- **The quest log** takes its click under quest text again, as vanilla: a click on another quest starts its text.
- **A refused hand-picked salvage** says when the stones are the reason.

## Fixed: the four classes

- **P2: the spin and sweep blows take the damage passives, Leech and Life Tap.** Fend, Wheel of Heaven and Sweeping
  Reed struck at full force since round 20 but skipped Relentless Assault, Ambush, Leech and the rest, which the front
  swing and the RfA-12 sweeps take. Shares of the front blow still take them once.
- **A Monk's staff cleave** no longer hits the side tiles a second time under Sweeping Reed or Wheel of Heaven.
- **Life Tap** heals from Army of the Dead, Soul Harvest and Death Mark too.
- **Ice Arrow** does not freeze a monster it missed in front of a wall.
- **Glass Cannon's hover** shows its +15% damage again. It moved off the sheet in round 20.

## Fixed: rifts

- **P2: the Guardian clock starts at the first step inside,** not when the keystone turns. Fifteen minutes spent
  shopping first had spent the keystone on a rift already out of time.
- **The arrival exit** keeps loot off itself and its neighbours, as the way home does.

## Fixed: crafting

- **P2: the Cube's auto-pick never chooses Reforge on a tie.** A unique beside one stack of Unique Encrustments ran
  Reforge unasked. Reforge rerolls at drop odds, often into a white base. Chosen from the book, it still runs.
- **P2: Recast stays within the item's class.** An imbued armour piece could become the set's ring and lose every shard
  and its punched sockets.
- **Consecrate** matches the item's class as well as its slot: a rare shield had become a set mace.
- **Items on Ogden's and Gillian's benches** show usable or red against the hero, as the Cube's do.

## Fixed: shrines

- **P2: the Weird Shrine** no longer wraps a 255 max-damage weapon to 0, and the Gloomy Shrine no longer wraps 0 back to
  255.
- **The Hidden Shrine** no longer revives a broken ethereal item, and it recalculates the hero at once.
- **Murphy's Shrine** no longer halves 1 durability to an unbroken 0.
- **The Mysterious Shrine and the Fountain of Tears** never kill: their -1 stat can switch off +life gear or take
  Vitality's life. They keep the hero at 1 life, as a stat refund does.

## Fixed: quests

- **Pepin** no longer asks for the Brain again after taking it (an unreachable branch, as upstream).
- **The Spectral and Golden Elixirs** make room on a full floor. The quest had moved on and the elixir silently did not
  appear.
- **Ogden** speaks of Leoric when the hero reached level 3 by waypoint.
- **Adria's quest mark** goes out once she has said her line about the Brain.

## Not changed (open)

- **Oil effects are lost on every rebuild** (Gillian's paid rework, every Cube rebuild). Keeping them needs either a
  scratch-rebuild diff or recorded oil deltas (an item-format bump).
- **Quest items carry across games** through the backpack and the shared stash, so a quest can be skipped. The
  behaviour is vanilla; refusing them in the stash is the user's call.
- **A towner's unique reward** can vanish when the floor is full of non-junk items. The Rock, the Anvil or the Sign is
  taken first.
- **Spell-level shrines** (Enchanted, Fascinating) ignore the book rank rule. The behaviour is vanilla.
- **The way home's fallback tile and the guardian's spawn spot** have no reachability check.
- **Gillian's Cleanse** costs gold on one tab and nothing on the Craft tab. Recast is not depth-gated. The Mystic's
  affix lock outlives a Cube reroll.
- **Gold can be put in the craft grids.** Ogden's step can spend its inputs when the output has nowhere to go (full pack,
  stash and floor).
