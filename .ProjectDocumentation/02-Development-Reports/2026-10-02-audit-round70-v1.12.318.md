# 2026-10-02 - Audit round 70 (v1.12.318)

**Date:** 2026-10-02. Debug only. 904 tests pass; the Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest. Commit 95bef5d6.

Three read-only auditors ran: a regression review of v1.12.317, items and crafting, and the Necromancer (12th pass).

## Regression review of v1.12.317
- **Weapon cleave side blows:** they ran the whole of `OnPassiveHit` after v1.12.317. That meant every side blow also moved Cadence's count, reset Counterstroke, ticked the combo and Mythic counters and ran the shared on-hit rules. A side blow is now a burst (`burst = true`): the per-blow passives pay, and the swing's own counters wait for the front blow.
- **Gillian's reroll:** the lock and the doubled price were paid on the bench item only. Taking one of the offers, which were built before the reroll, undid both. They are now copied into every offer. The wheel's dedupe also starts at slot 0, so the affix as it stands is never offered as new.
- **The crafting book's pressed button** is file-local (`static`).

## Items and crafting
- **Reforge** keeps the base tier, as Retier does (`PinnedBaseTier`). The fresh roll drew a new one, so a Normal base could come back Cruel for free.
- **A reworked staff** keeps its spell's share of the value. `GetItemAttrs` put the bare base value back, and the staff was priced as a plain stick.
- **Smart Loot** never trades a shield for a weapon. Both are `ILOC_ONEHAND`, so the aim could turn a shield drop into a sword.
- **Unbind the Level** takes the first wearable that still asks a level. Before, an already-unbound ring placed ahead of the helm made the recipe refuse.

## Necromancer
- **Golems:** they clamp to the last open tile toward a cursor past a wall, like the other summons.
- **Bodies:** a body never spawns behind a wall from where it was called (`LineClearMissile` in `SpawnBody`).
- **Minion armour:** it counts against a monster's blow, with a hit chance of at least 5%. Records set `armorClass` and nothing read it.
- **Final Service:** it needs a living minion. A record whose body was down was enough before.
- **Expired records:** an expired, bodiless record renumbers its ring, so the formation closes up.
- **Unholy Offering:** it redraws the life orb at once.
- **A dead caster's bolt:** it no longer reads the berserk flag of whatever monster now holds the caster's slot (`liveSourceMonster`).

## Test
- **`MercyHealsOnceBelowThirtyPercentAndThenWaits`:** it failed under one shuffle seed (2768). `ClearRfa12State` deliberately keeps Mercy's cooldown across the stairs, and an earlier test had armed it. The test now starts from a new game (`ForgetRfa12Clocks`).
