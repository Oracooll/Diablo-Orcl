## Fixed
1. **Quick-cast bow latch outlived its shot (P2).** A bow skill quick-cast from a key during a held plain attack repeated, and charged, on every held shot.
   - The latch a key arms is now one-shot: `MarkBowLatchOneShot` in QuickCast, `DropOneShotBowLatch` after the release frame.
   - Only a latch that key armed is marked; the held button's own is untouched.
2. **A stagger turned a held bow skill into plain arrows (P2).** It continued as plain, unpaid arrows. `SwingLatches` now stashes and restores the bow latches with the melee ones.
3. **Strafe from a key with another button held (P3).** It said "I can't do that". The spin is now taken only when Strafe is that button's skill (`StrafeReadiedOnHeldButton`); otherwise the key fires the volley.
4. **Elixirs didn't count toward item requirements (P2).** Their Strength/Magic/Dexterity counted for equipping (CanUseItem) but not for wearing (CalcSelfItems), so the item was switched off and drawn red. Both now see the elixirs. Test added: the 112-Strength axe test, with seven elixirs.
5. **Pepin's elixir stock (P3).** He offered a Barbarian the Elixir of Magic. The old base-cap gate on elixirs is gone, since they are timed and touch no base stat.
6. **Lightning mage's bolt flew sideways (P3, cosmetic).** The bone tooth now turns with the Charged Bolt each time it changes course. The user asked for the tooth on every mage, so it stays.
7. **Armour stands dropped a stray item (P2).** On Normal floor 6 (medium) and 10-16 (heavy), no armour base was that low after the loot-level halving, and the draw read a stale slot (a potion or a ring). `RndTypeItemsUpTo` now falls back to the type's lowest base; a draw that finds one is unchanged, seed for seed.
8. **Charge measured from two different tiles (P3).** The straight line came from the tile being left and the path from the one being reached. Both now use the path's start.
9. **Jab was free (P2).** Joining the swing chains in v1.12.375 skipped the only code that set `struck`. A chain's first landed thrust now pays.
10. **Shadow Step stopped 2 tiles out (P2).** The old "within 2 of the cursor" reach capped the step at 2 tiles. It now goes up to 6 tiles toward the cursor (+1 per 4 ranks, max 10), and the tooltip says so.
11. **Staff look after a gear change mid-swing (P3).** It could put an old worn sheet back; `ResetPlayerGFX` now drops it.
12. **Vault and a queued throw (P3).** Vault now clears the throw latch, so a throw queued from the old spot doesn't go from the landing.
13. **Barrage's later arrows (P3).** They could strike a new monster in the dead target's slot; the field now forgets the slot when it frees.
14. **Rift guardians healed on their own (P2).** Grimwing's gargoyle AI turned to stone and healed to full when the hero was far, and the scavenger Maw ate corpses back to full. A rift guardian takes neither heal now; Grimwing keeps his row's two short rests.
15. **Rift boss damage capped at 255 (P2).** On Hell a boss's damage passed the 255 cap, so every Guardian boss hit a flat 255. The spread is now kept scaled in the bytes, and the excess is laid on as a percent at every blow, melee and missile (`RiftBossDamagePercent`).
16. **Split copies could lose their tracking (P3).** A dungeon monster dying in the same slot number struck the copy off its list; `ForgetRiftConjured` now acts only inside the rift.
17. **`riftboss` left state behind when it failed (P3, debug).** A failed open leaves no forced boss; a failed entry leaves no full bar.
