# 1.8.30 - 960 pixels wide

> "i am running the game at 960x720"

That closed it in one line, after four wrong theories.

## The bug

`CheckCursMove` - the per-frame hover router - opens with:

```cpp
if (mainPanel.contains(MousePosition)) { CheckPanelInfo(); return; }
```

`GetMainPanel()` is the **vanilla 640x128 rect**, centred at the bottom. On 2026-08-15 that rect was
retired as an input authority in `LeftMouseDown`, because the new HUD occupies only a small plate at
the centre-bottom and everything else in the old rect is empty screen. `oracool::IsPointOverHudChrome`
was written for exactly that, and the click router was switched to it.

**The hover router was never switched.** So clicks behaved correctly while hovers were still being
swallowed by a rectangle nothing is drawn in.

## Why it took a week

It is **width-dependent**, and invisible at the width I assumed:

| Screen width | Main panel x | Inventory x | Overlap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1280 | 320 - 960 | 960 - 1280 | none |
| **960** | **160 - 800** | **640 - 960** | **640 - 800** |

The main panel is 640 wide and centred; the inventory is 320 wide against the right edge. At 1280
they touch at a single edge. At 960 the panel reaches 160px into the inventory, over `y` 592-666 -
**the bottom rows of the backpack grid.**

Which is where gold accumulates. So the symptom presented as *"gold cannot be Ctrl+Clicked"* rather
than *"the bottom of the inventory is dead"*, and the user's later measurement - *"only a few px
somewhere around the top part of the grid boxes"* - was the last row being clipped by `y = 592`
partway through its 28px height.

The hover returned early, `pcursinvitem` stayed `-1`, and `TransferItemToStash(-1)` returns on its
first line. **The gold path was never wrong.** Neither was the transfer, the stash, the tooltip, or
the backpack geometry - all four were read, doubted, and in three cases tested clean.

## The fix

One line: `CheckCursMove` now asks `oracool::IsPointOverHudChrome(MousePosition)`, the same authority
the click router has used since August 15. The two routers agree again.

## The lesson, which is not "check the resolution"

Four theories died here: the transfer path, the tooltip assert, the backpack hit rects, the
720-tall constants. Every one was a plausible mechanism I could reach from the source alone, and
every one was wrong.

What none of them had was **the environment the bug lived in**. The single most informative fact in
the whole investigation was one the user typed in nine words, and I never asked for it. Twice I
asked for datapoints - about non-gold items, about the stash grid - and both were guesses at
*mechanism*, not at *setup*.

**When a bug will not reproduce, ask what the setup is before asking what the code does.** Resolution,
window mode, and settings are cheap to ask for and are the class of fact that source reading cannot
supply.

## What else this fixes, unreported

Any hover over the region where an open side panel overlaps the legacy main-panel rect at widths
below 1280 - the bottom of the inventory, and the bottom of the stash, character sheet, quest log
and waypoint list on the left. All were dead to hover; all now respond.
