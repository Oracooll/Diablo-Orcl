# 2026-10-06 - Audit round 124 (v1.12.413)

**Date:** 2026-10-06. Debug only. All 954 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks:**
- a regression review of v1.12.412 (no P1 or P2);
- the hero's action state machine: movement, swings, spells, got-hit, latches, cooldowns.

## Fixed (P2)
1. **Vault and Leap kept the old walk path.** A hero standing on a blocked path, its steps dropped one a tick, kept the rest through the teleport. She then walked it from the landing, often back toward what she had leapt from. `ProcessTeleport` now drops the path and the queued action whatever the mode. The vanilla Teleport already did, through `OnSpellTile`.

## Fixed (P3)
2. **The ranged attack's scaled damage** is computed once and given to both the missile and the rift boss's extra shots. Those had lost the percent since round 123, harmlessly today.
3. **A minion mage's Charged Bolt** takes Might as well as its overflow.
4. **A key's bow skill that could not be paid at the release** now gives the held button its own skill back (`DropOneShotBowLatch`). Before, it dropped every latch and the button shot plain arrows after.
5. **A throw clicked again before its release** no longer queues a second swing. The first swing's release consumed the latch, and the queued one went out as a plain melee swing in the bow's sheet.

## Open (P3, larger changes) and design calls
- **The hit frame is read from the live latch each tick.** Clicking a plain attack mid-Shield-Bash on an axe or staff Paladin with Heavenly Strength can land a second blow in the same swing; arming Bash mid-plain-swing can lose the blow. The fix is a hit frame snapshotted at `StartAttack`.
- **A melee skill from a quick-cast key** stays armed for the rest of a held plain attack, and each swing pays for it. QuickCast and the QuickSpell keys are both off by default. Round 95 made only the bow latch one-shot.
- **A block can interrupt a swing before its hit frame** without clearing the latches. A Charge's arriving blow blocked away keeps its spent mana.
- **A mixed sheet finished during a javelin swing** is not applied until the next reload (cosmetic).
- **Vault's cooldown can be avoided:** two vaults every three seconds never start the 15 s cooldown.
- **Charge mana** is not refunded when the target dies or the dash runs out (deliberate).
- **A Counselor's Charged Bolt** is a flat 15, as in vanilla.

## Checked and fine
- **Movement speed:** clamped.
- **Every swing path** disarms at the start, and got-hit clears and stashes.
- **The staff look** ends on every path.
- **Spells** re-check the price and the cooldown.
- **The hit-frame price latches** are released by a scope guard.
- **Teleport landings** are in sight and fit.
- **Whirlwind** stops on every gate.
- **Death, town and window gates** hold.
- **Queued monster targets** are dropped on death.
- **Command handlers** are bounds-checked.
- **The ranged scaling** is applied once (round 123).
