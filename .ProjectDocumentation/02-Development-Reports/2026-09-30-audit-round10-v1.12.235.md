# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 10 (v1.12.235)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 10 ran six read-only tracks:
1. hero creation and the class data tables;
2. runtime item bonus totals;
3. difficulty and scaling;
4. the stash and belt interface;
5. monster attacks on the hero;
6. the town layout (checked against the extracted town `.dun`, `.til` and `.sol` data).

Every finding was verified against the code first. The town track found the placements sound, with one exception.

## Fixed: item sets and bonuses

- **A set piece the hero cannot use still counted toward its set.** When a piece's requirements lapsed (for example,
  a Strength ring taken off), the piece turned red and lost its own stats. Its set's rungs, the completion stinger and
  the milestone stayed lit. `IsSetPieceWorn` now also asks `_iStatFlag`. `IsSetPieceHeld` still counts any worn
  piece, working or not, so the drop weighting does not hand out a second copy of a red piece.
  - On the set list, a worn red piece now reads yellow (owned) rather than green.
- **The "Wear a set bonus" milestone** was checked only at a level-up or an imbuement, so a hero at the level cap
  never earned it. `CalcPlrInv` now claims it whenever a set bonus is active. It waits until the character is settled
  in a running game, not while a hero loads, which kept the writehero golden unchanged.
- **Set rungs drew on the game's main random stream** at every stat recalculation. The rung loop now runs under a
  `MainSeedGuard`.
- **Dread's boss claim** now recalculates the hero's stats right away.

## Fixed: monster attacks on the hero

- **Elemental-only damage reductions shrank the whole blow.** Bosses, rift guardians and the Searing, Voltaic and
  Glacial variants split each melee blow into a physical part and an elemental part. The whole blow went through one
  call tagged with the element, so, for example, Sixth Sense with Vigilant cut each boss hit to about 55%. The call is now
  physical, and the elemental part is adjusted by only its element's extra reduction.
- **A charge that hit a minion or companion moved the charger's map entry, not the target's.** This vanilla bug is
  now reachable because of the fork's summons. It left `dMonster` out of sync: the decoy could not be clicked, and a
  phantom blocking tile stayed behind. The charge now moves the target and skips a target that died from it.
- **Knockback from a charge ignored Immovable and Heavy Foot.** Both knockback sites could also land the hero on
  their own companion's tile. Both sites now ask `PlayerIgnoresKnockback` and move the companion out of the way first.
- **Chilling Armor fired a free Ice Bolt for every acid puddle tick** that landed. Acid puddles no longer trigger it.
- **Arrow evasion was rolled before the to-hit roll.** Arrows that would have missed anyway still triggered
  Tactical Advantage's haste. The check now runs after the to-hit roll.

## Fixed: difficulty and scaling

- **Town quest rewards on Nightmare and above** rolled at the quest's floor number (for example, ilvl 10 on Torment).
  They now roll at the area level.
- **Gold piles counted the difficulty twice.** For example, Nightmare floor 1 rolled 165-495. Every difficulty now
  rolls `5 x ilvl + rnd(10 x ilvl)`, and Torment keeps its multiplier. This lowers gold on Nightmare and above.
- **A low Guardian Rift keystone used in a higher difficulty** paid that low tier's loot while the hero fought this
  difficulty's monsters. The rift's area level is now floored at the difficulty's first rung.
- **Pepin stopped stocking stat elixirs** at the class row's old vanilla cap, not at the fork's cap of 255.

## Fixed: the stash and the belt

- **A book read from the stash was used up and taught nothing** when it was above the hero's level or at the spell
  ceiling. The backpack's refusal is now a shared `RefuseUnreadableBook`, and the stash asks it first.
- **A stale stash hover index** could point past the list after a removal (two clicks in one tick, on the last unit
  of a stack). Use and transfer now bounds-check it, and `RemoveStashItem` forgets the hover.
- **A right-click used a stash item from the world.** A hover that survived onto a ground-item label, even after the
  stash closed, used the item. The right-click now acts only over the open stash.
- **The stash opened under a bench or the Cube that refused to close.** It now backs out the same way the workshop
  and the Cube do.
- **The stash's buttons answered while it was closed.** Pressing, then Esc, then releasing on the same spot opened the
  withdraw box with no stash behind it.
- **A belt refill** now recalculates the hero before counting scrolls.
- **The closed backpack could be clicked through the HUD plate.** Its bottom-left cell sits under the plate's
  top-right strip. With the backpack closed, only a real belt slot answers there now.
- **At 4:3 the orbs took clicks and hover from the bottom rows** of an open backpack, stash, Abilities window or left
  panel, where the orbs are drawn clipped. They now yield to the open panel.

## Fixed: town and hero creation

- **Leaving a Sealed Map arena** put the hero beside Farnham in the far south-east corner. It now returns to the
  middle of town, the new-game spawn.
- **The new-hero preview showed a weapon the hero would not have.** With Naked Heroes on (the default), the preview
  now shows the hero bare-handed.

## Not changed (open)

- **The Necromancer still starts with Firebolt and the Sorcerer's staff.** Teeth and the wand family both exist now.
  This is the user's call; it was asked earlier.
- **Active charms are chosen by backpack list order**, not by grid position, so which three are active can change
  when items move.
- **Chests and other non-monster drops** draw their base from twice the area level.
- **The Thunderous death ring** is fired as a trap missile, so it also hits monsters.
- **Vampiric and Devouring** heal from the blow before the hero's reductions.

## Tests

- The set tests now mark worn pieces usable, as `CalcPlrInv` would.
- `BonusesRequireTheirPiecesToBeWorn` checks that a red piece does not count toward the set.
- `SetPieceListReadsGreenWornYellowOwnedRedMissing` checks that a worn red piece reads yellow.
- `NamedSetDropsLeanTowardTheSetYouAreCollecting` checks that a worn red piece is still held.
