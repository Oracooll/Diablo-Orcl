# 2026-10-01 - Audit round 46 (v1.12.279)

**Date:** 2026-10-01. Debug only.

**Tracks:**
- regression review of v1.12.278;
- Paladin and Barbarian, bugs only (3rd pass);
- Necromancer (8th);
- item movement between containers (4th).

## Fixed

### Round 45 slips
- **Staff charges on a rework at Gillian:** they are untouched when the charge multiplier is unchanged, and rescaled multiply-first in 64 bits when it changes. Divide-then-multiply took 8/18 to 6/18 on every rework.
- **Area skills aimed at the foot of a wall** strike from the last open tile toward it, instead of being refused by the new wall-tile clause. This covers Rain of Arrows, Wave of Light, Seven-Sided Strike, Valkyrie's Spear, and now Army of the Dead.
- **Wall-blocked movers say "I can't do that":** Vault, Heaven's Descent, Leaping Crane and Shoulder Gate with no room to move. Leaping Crane and Shoulder Gate record where he landed.

### Movement and walls
- **`TeleportTo` chooses the landing first** (`SightedLandingNear`): the nearest tile within five a hero may stand on and can see. The engine's own search could pick the room behind a wall when the target was crowded.
- **Leap and Leap Attack:**
  - **Walls:** they leap over monsters but not through walls.
  - **Landing:** they land on a tile the hero can see.
- **Fist of the Heavens:** on a wall tile it comes down on the last open tile toward it. Its 36 bolts had fanned out of the wall into both rooms.
- **New exports:** `LastOpenTileToward` and `SightedLandingNear`, in `rfa12_actives.h`.

### Paladin and Barbarian
- **Earthshaker Cry** costs nothing when no one hears it, as every other cry.
- **Whirlwind's Rage drain** redraws the orb.
- **The holy auras** don't pulse in town (towner ids in `dMonster`).

### Items
- **Stolen-and-downgraded potions** go to the belt, then the pack, then the stash, then the floor; they were lost with no belt room.
- **The shift-click swap undo** falls back to the pack, then the cursor.
- **The equip fallback** returns once the item is on the cursor; the clear after it destroyed it.
- **Belt merges on walk-over pickup** take potions only.
- **Charms coming back from the Cube or the workshop** recalculate the hero at once.

### Necromancer
- **Command the Dead** refuses a target the army could never strike: a talker, a hidden or fading monster, one behind a wall.
- **Golem armour** is clamped at the body's byte, as its damage since round 44.

## Open
- **Corpses on a revisited floor:** the corpse table empties on every level load while `dCorpse` is saved, so visible bodies can't be raised or exploded. This needs a per-level table; scoped for the next round.
- **The army panel** shows the group cap, not the rank cap.
- **Unholy Offering:**
  - **Reach:** it takes the nearest minion anywhere on the level.
  - **Heal:** it heals from the minion's maximum life, not its current life.
- **Revive and Raise** can spend a corpse before knowing the body can stand.
- **Bone Storm** ends at a level change.
- **Life Tap:** some army damage doesn't feed it.
- **Smaller gaps:**
  - Rallying Cry heals 99% of its total;
  - Blessed Hammer hits through walls (D2's does too);
  - a pasted item can enter a page locked mid-view.
- **Still the user's:**

  - passives and +skills;
  - Barbarian mana potions;
  - Murphy's Shrine;
