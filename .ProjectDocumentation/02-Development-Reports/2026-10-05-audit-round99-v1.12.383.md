# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 99 (v1.12.383)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 946 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks (three):** a regression review of v1.12.382, the Barbarian's actives, and the Sorcerer's fire and cold skills.

## Fixed: sight
1. **A wall-corner pinch mid-line (P3).** `SightLineClear` refused a squeezed wall corner only on an adjacent step. It now checks every step of the line, middle and ends, and closed doors at the end in both directions. That also covers Leap's landings (`SightedLandingNear`).

## Fixed: Barbarian
2. **Whirlwind's strikes (P2).** `EnemiesBeside` hit through squeezed wall corners and doors. It now needs sight.
3. **Cleave and Sweep (P2).** The side tiles reached through wall corners, and each side blow earned Rage. Now they need sight.
4. **Backhand and Rearward Reach (P2).** The tile behind the hero is a tile-diagonal when facing a screen-cardinal direction, and they reached through corners. Now they need sight.
5. **Sweeping Reed and `GatherAround` (P3).** Fend, Wheel of Heaven and Radiant Palm reached through wall corners. Now they need sight.
6. **Leap Attack (P3).** A missed arrival swing left the leap's prepaid blow set, so later Leap Attacks were free until one landed. It is now settled landed or not.

## Fixed: Sorcerer fire and cold
7. **Absolute Zero froze rift bosses (P2).** It froze a rift roster boss solid, and every stagger and shove moved him, because `ShrugsOff` did not know him: no unique row, no affix. It now follows `FightsAsUnique`.
8. **Area skills reached through closed doors (P2).** Frost Nova, Blizzard (the cast and each shard), Meteor rocks, Hydra and Fire Wall placement all passed through. They now use `SightLineClear`.
9. **Frozen Orb (P2).** It drifted through closed doors. Its move now stops on `IsMissileBlockedByTile`.
10. **Whiteout (P2).** It rolled through closed doors. It now stops on `LineStepBlocked`.
11. **Funeral Spiral (P2).** Its fireballs crossed closed doors. Now they stop there.
12. **Fireball's splash (P2, vanilla too).** It reached through the closed door it burst on. Firestorm and the Hydra fire through the same path.
13. **Glacial Spike's shatter (P3).** It chilled through wall corners and doors, and hit monsters that cannot be hit. It now needs sight and `isPossibleToHit`.
14. **Chill Touch and Bone Teeth's arc (P3).** It reached through wall corners. It now stops on the step test.
15. **Brittle Ground's second tile (P3).** It could lie past a door or corner; it now falls back to the first tile.

## Fixed: polish
16. **Wrath of the Heavens.** The tooltip now says "up to 5 pillars within 6 s". The last due pillar is no longer dropped on the storm's final tick. The refusal now says "I can't do that".
17. **Iron Maiden.** The keeps-its-target path now notes the owner was struck, for the companions' focus.

## Reported, not coded (design calls)
- **Earthshaker Cry and War Cry:** they cost Rage when anything is in earshot, even if a lone magic-immune unique is untouched. Making them pay only for what they affect is the user's call. Howl, Taunt and Ground Stomp follow the "affected" rule.
- **Rearward Reach:** it is neither a Rage generator nor a spender, so it is a free, unlimited 100%+ weapon blow. Is that the intent?
