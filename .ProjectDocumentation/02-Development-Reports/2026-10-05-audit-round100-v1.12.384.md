# 2026-10-05 - Audit round 100 (v1.12.384)

**Date:** 2026-10-05. Debug only. All 947 tests pass. The Debug `diablo.ini` was unchanged by ctest (md5 b3392ce4).

**Tracks (two):** a regression review of v1.12.383, and a sweep of every remaining door-blind check in the player skills.

## Fixed: regressions in round 99's own fixes
1. **Sight across lava pools (P1).** Every off-axis line across a lava pool or chasm was refused, because the corner-squeeze test read `IsTileSolid`, and lava is solid but a missile flies over it. Hit were Blizzard, Hydra, Fire Wall, Meteor Shower, cast sight, Leap's landings, auras, cries and Conversion. It now reads `PosOkMissile`, the property the line itself is stopped by. Test added: `OracoolAudit100.SightCrossesLavaButNotASqueezedCorner`.
2. **Frozen Orb's stop (P3).** The round-99 fix stopped it at barrels and chests and let it cross lava. It now stops at walls and closed doors only.

## Fixed: rift bosses as uniques
3. **The other copies of `ShrugsOff` (P2).** Round 99 fixed one of five; four still let a rift roster boss be controlled:
   - **warcries.cpp:** Conversion turned him to the Paladin's side; Repel and Stagger moved him.
   - **melee_skills:** Stun and Breaking Current stunned him.
   - **paladin_melee:** Shield Bash stunned him.
   - **passives:** Paralysis stunned him.

   All now follow `FightsAsUnique`.

## Fixed: the door sweep

     - The corpse search behind Corpse/Poison Explosion, Raise and Revive.

   - **Companions:** placement and the summon anchor, guard/bait holds, targeting, Volley and Double Throw.
   - **Sorcerer:** Storm Crucible's pair, Ring of Fire, Hellfire's Apocalypse.

   - **Vanilla spells:** Golem placement, rune placement, the Guardian's aim.
5. **The other fixes:**
   - Bone Wall's arms use the step test and door sight.

   - The Elemental's splash stops at the door it burst on.
   - Unstable Anomaly needs sight.
   - The Ancients' spin, the Fire Golem's pulse and Zeal's next target no longer reach across squeezed corners.
