# 2026-09-30 - Whole-code audit, round 22 (v1.12.247)

**Date:** 2026-09-30. Debug only. The audit continues at the user's word.

Round 22 ran six read-only tracks:
1. a regression review of v1.12.246;
2. every way damage to the hero is reduced, and how the reductions stack;
3. state that outlives the game (second pass);
4. single-player against multiplayer gating, and the network loopback;
5. the fork's missiles and fields (third pass);
6. the Hellfire content (second pass).

Every finding was verified against the code first.

## Fixed: regressions of round 21

- **Rage is cleared on a respawn.** v1.12.246's death guard froze the pool on the corpse, so a respawn carried the
  whole of it into town.
- **Flambeau and Blitzen** take the missile kind from the bow but the damage from all fire sources again (a Ruby, a Flame
  shard, Enchant).
- **Oil of Permanence refused on an ethereal** says so in the hero's voice.

## Fixed: missiles

- **P1: Fist of the Heavens struck its centre 37 times.** The ring's 36 bolts start on the target's tile, and each one hit
  the monster there on its first tick. The centre took about 2,300% weapon damage where the text says 150%. The bolts
  now leave the centre alone.
- **The small lightning ring reaches the 4 tiles its aim marks.** Fist of the Heavens' ring and the Thunderous champion's
  death burst flew about 90 tiles, across whole rooms.
- **Exploding, Freezing and Immolation Arrow** land their burst on the near side of the wall that stopped them. They had
  reached the far side of a one-tile wall.
- **Strafe** does not aim at monsters no arrow can hit, such as talkers or a charging counselor.
- **Blessed Shield** does not bounce toward a converted ally.

## Fixed: defence, statics and Hellfire

- **Flat "-X damage taken"** (Iron Robe, items) takes whole points off fire walls, acid puddles and Inferno. It had
  taken 1/64 of a point, a slip inherited from vanilla.
- **Doppelganger clones in a rift** fight at the rift's scale. At base stats they died in a hit and filled the kill bar
  as full monsters.
- **Celia's and the Hork Demon's amulets** make room on a full floor. Theodore had been taken and the amulet silently
  not given.
- **The Defiler** enters the quest log on the first Hive floor visited, not only on floor 17.
- **A new game starts clean:**
  - the runeword book's filters;
  - the crafting book's tab;
  - the Cube's tab and pressed buttons;
  - Gillian's imbue list scroll.

## Fixed: the network loopback in single player

- **An item held when a pickup lands** goes whole into the backpack or at the hero's feet. It had been dropped through
  the network message, which rebuilds an item from its seed, so a socketed, imbued or reworked item landed plain.
- **A pickup request that goes unanswered** clears its flag. This covers a request refused as a repeat or one arriving
  mid level change. Left set, auto-pickup skipped the item for good and the flag went into the level save. No request
  flag survives a level load.

## Not changed (open)

- **Self-inflicted costs run through the full damage pipeline.** Rage's end cost, Sacrifice, and Blood Star and Bone
  Spirit's life costs are soaked by Bone Armor, Mana Shield and the damage reductions (free under Rathma's Shield). They
  also trip the "when struck" passives. Poison ticks spend Galvanizing Ward the same way. The fix is a self-damage path
  through ApplyPlrDamage.
- **Poison and life drain** read the blow at different points on melee and missiles; element variants convert a third of
  a melee blow but the whole of a missile. The Thunderous burst counts as a trap (half trap damage, "killed by a trap").
- **The holy auras, Sanctuary and Radiance** strike through walls, where Static Field and Thunder Storm do not. D2's auras
  ignore walls - the user's call.
- **Oil bonuses** (to-hit, damage, armour, requirements, durability) are cleared by any rework or reforge.
- **The Auric Amulet** does nothing in single player (gold capacity is already 100 million), though its tooltip says it
  doubles it.
- **Floors 19-20 and 23-24** get no champion packs or Dread bosses: no Hellfire monster there has a unique row. It may be
  part of "the Hive and the Crypt stay as they are".
- **ItemLimbo:** one global holds the item a drop is placing, and an auto-pickup respawn can overwrite it mid-drop. This
  is a vanilla race that the fork's 10-tile pickup radius makes likelier.
- **An Oil of Permanence** can be lost when a rework adds and then removes "of the ages": the oil is not recorded on the
  item.
- **The event log** reopens at the top after the chat log closes.
- **Telemetry's session id** is per process, so two heroes in one run share one session.
