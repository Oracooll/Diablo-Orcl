Windows x64 Release build of Diablo Orcl.

The first release in seventeen days: 290 commits and the whole 1.12 series past v1.11.078. The package is the usual shape: the binary, its libraries, two archives (`oracool.mpq` for this fork's own art and data, `devilutionx.mpq` for the engine's) and a README. You supply Blizzard's archives yourself, as the README explains.

## Classes and skills

- **Every class's skill tree is built.** The 162 new skills are in and working, alongside the originals: 434 rows across the classes, each growing with every rank and saying so in its hover (current level and next level, D2-style).
- **The Barbarian runs on Rage** instead of mana: generators build it, spenders pay for it, and it carries down the stairs.
  - Whirlwind is held on the right button: he spins toward the cursor in a magic cloud, with two axes and two swords circling him.
  - Double Swing and Frenzy swing twice per click.
  - Rend is a curse cast round him, and Earthquake a train of shockwaves.
  - Leap, Ground Stomp, Rend, Whirlwind, Earthquake and the war cries go on the right button only.
- **The Necromancer** is a playable class: minions, curses, bone and poison, an army of up to 32 bodies.
- **Companions:** the Valkyrie, the Spirit Guardian, the Decoy and the three Ancients.
  - Talic whirls, Madawc throws hammers and Korlic fights with his sword, each in his own colour.
- **The Paladin's skills** have reworked effects, and shield skills show red while no shield is held.
- **Battle Command** raises every skill a rank, and the badges show it.

## Sound and effects

- **Sound:** every skill sound is a vanilla Diablo sound now, chosen per skill from the review pages. Melee skills play their own swing and impact.
- **Effects:** skills wear vanilla effects, scaled and tinted: Holy Bolt bursts, Blood Stars, Fire Wall flames, war cry rings sized to each skill's reach.

## Town and systems

- **Rifts:** the Rift Monument in town opens rifts, generated levels with a kill bar and a guardian at the end.
- **The artisans:**
  - Griswold's Salvage page;
  - Gillian's Mystic Workshop (reroll, imbue, cleanse);
  - Ogden's jeweller's collection boards and recipes;
  - Wirt's cart with Shop and Gamble.
- **Imbuement Shards** replace the Mystic Orbs: 24 kinds, a ledger per item.
- **Loot:**
  - one affix list;
  - no affix above the item's level;
  - Smart Loot leaning toward your class;
  - SORT gives every quality its own stash page;
  - eleven more monster variants.
- **Waypoints** are split into three Acts.
- **Buttons:** every button presses and acts on release.

## Screens

- **Character sheet:** redesigned, with Now/Base stat buttons and hit odds. Resistance bars run in nine parts to the 90 cap, and resistance can go negative, D2-style.
- **Advanced Stats window** beside it.
- **Tooltips:** a gold card for item tooltips and hints, and ethereal items marked in purple.

## Fixes worth naming

- **Nightmare drops:** they were rejected as invalid packets and never saved.
- **Weapon sprites:** equipping a weapon without re-equipping it could leave the hero drawn with the old one's sprites.
- **Summons in town:** summoning in town crashed the game.
- **Rifts:** closing a rift could strand you.
