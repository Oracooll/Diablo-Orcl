1. ITEM-01 and SAVE-01: prevent irreversible loss of owned items. Make stash sorting transactional and resolve workshop custody before saving/resetting.
2. UI-01/UI-02 alongside workshop lifecycle: repair Escape and interface ownership without discarding staged contents on a refused close.
3. SKL-01 and ITEM-02: fix resource payment and numerical item corruption, retaining the supplied failing probes.
4. WORLD-01, then verify/fix WORLD-02: invalidate stale rift travel and scope guardian identity correctly.
5. SKL-02: clear slot-local cold state on deletion and initialization.
6. QA-01 throughout: put each regression into the repository's isolated production fixtures, then run the targeted session matrix below.

STASH-01 was explicitly rechecked as requested. The old behavior persists at this commit; an intervening fix/reintroduction was not established. Other old findings were not copied into this list simply because they existed previously.
