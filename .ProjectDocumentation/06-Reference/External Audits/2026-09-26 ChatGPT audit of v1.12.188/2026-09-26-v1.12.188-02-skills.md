# Audit 2: skill costs, effects and monster-state lifetime

Baseline: v1.12.188, commit `acc970335a9c0ecd5287f236fb601f2c8d7a8601`, 2026-09-26. Source root is recorded in the index. Evidence bundle: `2026-09-26-v1.12.188-evidence/`.

## SKL-01 [P2]: the live spell path bypasses Necromancer Essence payment

Confidence: **confirmed**. Evidence: code tracing plus two executed tests against the prebuilt production DLL. No combat playthrough is claimed.

Locations:

- `Source/spells.cpp:195`: `ConsumeSpell` calls `SettleSkill` only for `UsesRage`; other tree skills follow the mana branch.
- `Source/spells.cpp:267`: `CheckSpell` similarly calls `CanPaySkill` only for `UsesRage`.
- `Source/oracool/rage.cpp:141`: resource facade contains the Essence-aware eligibility/settlement logic.
- `Source/oracool/essence.cpp:51`: prices the two corpse explosions at 10, Revive at 35 and fourteen curses at 25 Essence.
- `Source/oracool/warcries.cpp:719`: RfA-12 cast dispatch; the successful cast returns to normal spell consumption.

### Trigger and consequence

Use one of those seventeen skills as a Necromancer with insufficient Essence but otherwise eligible to cast. The helper facade refuses payment, yet the production spell check approves. On successful consumption, the production path leaves Essence unchanged. These skills can therefore bypass their declared Essence economy. This is distinct from the deliberate rule that Barbarians have no mana.

The executed gating probe set Essence to zero, supplied mana, and iterated every `SpellID` for which `EssenceCost` is positive. All seventeen returned `Success` from `CheckSpell(..., Skill, true)` while `CanPaySkill` rejected them. The settlement probe started with 100 Essence and called `ConsumeSpell` with the executed skill selected: every priced skill retained 100 Essence instead of paying 10, 25 or 35. These are resource-path probes, not assertions that every target-dependent spell can produce an effect without a valid target.

### Suggested fix

Route resource eligibility and successful settlement through the shared facade for Essence-priced skills as well as Rage users. Preserve the existing mana contracts for other classes and settle once, only when a cast commits; do not add a second payment inside each curse or summon implementation.

### Regression that fails today

Retained probes `EssencePricedSkillsAreGatedByEssenceInProductionCheck` and `EssencePricedSkillsSettleEssenceInProductionConsume`. Extend into a valid-target cast fixture for all seventeen skills, testing zero, cost−1, exact cost and surplus Essence, failure/cancel, and repeated casts. Assert failed casts spend nothing and successful casts spend exactly once.

## SKL-02 [P2]: dead monster slots retain chill and freeze

Confidence: **confirmed**. Evidence: traced deletion/reinitialization and an executed production deletion probe.

Locations:

- `Source/oracool/chill.cpp:18`: `ChillTicks` and `FreezeTicks` live outside `Monster`, indexed by slot.
- `Source/monster.cpp:910`: deletion clears warcry, RfA-12, minion and curse state, but not cold state.
- `Source/monster.cpp:149`: slot initialization likewise clears warcry/RfA-12 but not chill/freeze.
- `Source/oracool/rfa12_effects.cpp:567` and `Source/oracool/rfa12_actives.cpp:3159`: the indirect RfA-12 cleanup only clears its own marks.
- `Source/monster.cpp:5096`: dying/petrified monsters do not run the cold timer tick.
- `Source/oracool/chill.cpp:93`: only the whole-level reset clears both arrays.

### Trigger and consequence

Kill a monster while it still has cold timers, then reuse that monster slot before leaving the level—for example through dynamic spawning. The old timers survive deletion and initialization. The new occupant can be slowed/frozen without being hit. Since death skips cold aging, a quick death does not consume the remaining duration. Curse cleanup can clear chill for Decrepify specifically; it is not a general chill/freeze reset and does not repair this path.

The probe applied 100 chill ticks and 50 freeze ticks to a real `Monsters[]` slot, marked it invalid, and called `DeleteMonsterList`. The active count decreased as expected, but `IsMonsterChilled` and `IsMonsterFrozen` both remained true. The test verifies deletion; reuse propagation follows from the separately inspected initialization path.

### Suggested fix and failing regression

Provide per-monster cleanup that clears **both** arrays, call it on deletion and initialization, and retain the full-level reset. `DeletedMonsterSlotClearsColdState` currently fails both assertions. Extend it to allocate a different monster into the same slot and verify normal first-tick behavior, including freeze without chill and curse-generated chill.

## Coverage and limits

Reviewed class-tree/rank bounds, widened spell identifiers, resource dispatch, curse/ground-effect state, summon ownership and the selected melee/projectile call sites. Read the skill-rule tests and their explicit inert-row exemptions. The existing all-class/all-rank rule sweep passed; it checks growth and displayed facts, not every live hit, animation or cast transaction. No new high-ID rank truncation was established.

The 32 inert rows, always-hit spells and mana-free Barbarian design are excluded. There was no exhaustive live combat matrix for six classes, every rank, every equipment combination and every monster resistance. Such coverage remains necessary after the integration regressions above are added.
