The two skill wells

The LMB and RMB wells each show what their button will do. Their opening between the bezels is 46 x 46 pixels - the hard boundary every icon and background is scaled into, so nothing spills onto the frame.

Standing interface rules

  • Every window is keyboard-reachable and never needs more than two clicks to act.
  • OK and Cancel sit in the same two zones in every dialog.
  • A window never lets a click through to the world beneath it.
  • Content that overflows scrolls; it is never clipped silently.
  • An open window hides the HUD behind it rather than overlapping it.
  • Every window the player can browse carries a small red X in its own top-right corner, and clicking it closes that window. Escape closes it too.

The Abilities window

Four sheets: Spells (the book list, grouped by level band and sorted alphabetically inside each) and three class-tree pages. Both use the same 56 x 56 icon cell with the spend controls on the icon: green plus bottom-right, red minus bottom-left, the invested level between them.
