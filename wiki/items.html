Base items

Every row of AllItemsList: the base objects the game builds an item from. Quality level (qlvl) is the item level a drop needs before this base can appear at all.

Reading the table

  • qlvl is the authored value from iMinMLvl. Banded is what the game actually compares against - the authored ladder regrouped onto the 1-48 scale, topping out at Hell/Hell, so a depth opens a shelf of bases rather than one.
  • Opens at names the earliest area whose item level reaches the banded qlvl.
  • Drop of NEVER means the base exists but is never randomly generated: quest items and class starting gear. The set, gem, rune and charm families are REGULAR - they are kept out of the shared pool by the drop hook, not by this column.
  • Damage, armour, durability and requirements are the Normal-tier numbers. Multiply by the tier scale for the other three.
  • A few families marked REGULAR are still kept out of the shared drop pool and reach you through their own hooks instead: the item sets, the gems, runes, jewels, Mystic Orbs, charms, signets and encounter maps. The walk decides the multiplayer wire format rather than the single-player save - loading a hero overwrites the replay - so growing it is a change to what the pack expects, not to what your character rebuilds.

Every item, at every tier

The table above lists the Normal-tier numbers, because that is how the game authors them. Here is what each weapon and piece of armour actually becomes at each of the four base tiers, with the game's own rounding applied rather than a multiplier for you to do in your head.

Only gear whose numbers a tier changes is listed - weapons and armour. Potions, quest items and the belt slot carry no tier at all. Rings and amulets do take one, but they have no damage, armour or durability to scale, so the only figure that moves is their value. A tier opens at an item level of 1, 13, 25 and 37 respectively.
