Unique items

Named items with fixed powers. Every one of them is a specific object rather than a roll - finding it twice gives you the same item both times.

How a unique drops

  • The unique roll happens before any Oracool quality roll, so a unique is never displaced by a rare or a primal.
  • Its minimum level below is a character-level requirement carried by the item itself, separate from the item level of the drop that produced it.
  • Items from a unique monster get a 15% chance at the unique table - the reason champions and bosses are worth seeking out.
  • A unique can be forged at any base tier, so the same named item is stronger when it turns up deeper.

All uniques

Some uniques carry a curse alongside their gift - a lower light radius, worse armour, a stat penalty. That is the vanilla design and it is preserved: the strongest items ask for something back.
