Classes

Six heroes. They differ in starting stats, in how far each stat can be raised, and in how much life and mana a level or a point buys - and each has its own four-page skill tree.

Reading the curve. A Barbarian gains 2 life per level and 2 per point of Vitality; a Sorcerer gains 1 life per level and 1 per point, but 2 mana per level and 2 per point of Magic. Over 99 levels those small differences are most of what separates the classes.

What every class shares

  • Naked start. No gear, no potions, no gold, both buttons on the bare fist.
  • One skill point per level, spent on the class tree. Spells are not deepened with points - they take their level from books and item bonuses only.
  • No vanilla class skill. Item Repair, Trap Disarm and the rest are retired; the tree is the skill system.
  • Every spell is learnable by any class that meets the level band and the Magic requirement - the tree is what makes a class distinct, not the spell list.
