Controls

Mouse rules, the reserved F-keys, and what can be remapped.

Mouse

ActionDoes
Left click, worldMove, pick up, open, talk - and attack or cast whatever is on the left button. Interaction always wins over the skill.
Right click, worldCasts or swings whatever is on the right button.
Shift + clickForces the cast at the cursor instead of stepping toward a target.
Left click, a skill in the Abilities windowSpends a point in it.
Right click, a skill in the Abilities windowRefunds a rank, one at a time, free.
Click a skill well on the HUDOpens the picker for that button - this is where a skill is readied.
Either click, an auraLights the aura, which is the right button's setting.
Shift + click the RMB wellClears the readied spell outright.
Each sheet keeps its own click rules deliberately - auras behave differently from actives, and unifying them would make one of the two wrong.

Reserved keys

F1 to F12 are intercepted before the keymapper, which is what makes them unremappable: no INI row can double-book them, and nothing else fires on either edge of the press.

KeyDoes
F1 - F8Ability hotkeys. The bare key uses the right button's binding; Shift + the key uses the left button's.
F9 / F10Game speed down / up. Range 20-60, step 2.
F11Open or close the event log.
F12Screenshot. Auto-repeat is dropped, so holding it takes one.

VPrints the Oracool version to the message line
EnterChat box (three rows, 66 characters); the event log steps aside and returns on Escape. In Debug builds this is also the debug console
EscapeCloses the topmost thing, then the game menu
SpaceMaster closer - shuts every open window at once, and is reserved so no binding can take it
1 - 4Belt slots
XDebug builds only - toggles the debug overlay
Every letter above is a default, not a reservation - only F1-F12 and Space are intercepted. The keymapper does not reject duplicates: bind two actions to one key and the later registration silently wins, with no warning at load. The letters currently spoken for are A B C D F G I L P Q R S T V W X Z.

Remapping

Everything not on the reserved list is remappable from the Keymapping screen, which still lists Help and the quick-spell actions - both default-unbound - for anyone who wants them on other keys. Screenshot is listed too, but it is bound by default, to Print Screen.
