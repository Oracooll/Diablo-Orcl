Every development report in the project vault, newest first. Each one records what changed, why, and what to look at in game.

The phases

Development runs to a plan of numbered phases. What each one added, and where the wiki covers it.

PhaseClosed atWhat it was
Phase 0 - foundations1.6.30Named RNG streams, a bonus-provider stat sheet, the chunked hero-file tail, a zone registry, a sprite scaler, dev tooling, and the balance telemetry CSV. Invisible in play; everything after it stands on these.
Phase 1 - the item endgame1.7.7Sockets, gems, runes, runewords and charms, ethereal drops, Magic and Gold Find, gambling at Wirt, and the crafting window.
Phase 2 - the character1.7.11+The respec at Adria, the run toggle, and the aura gameplay pass.
The 1.8 linecurrentThe loot mechanics rework listed below.
The hero file's chunked tail is the last planned save-format growth: new state appends a chunk rather than moving anything. That is why characters break less often now than they did through the 1.5 and 1.6 lines.
