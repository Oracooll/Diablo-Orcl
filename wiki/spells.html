Every entry in SpellData, with the character-level band that gates learning it and the item level its book needs before it can drop.

The six bands

A spell cannot be learned below its band, and its book cannot be read below it either. Book availability follows from the same band, so a book you cannot read is a book that should not have dropped.

The Rule of Rangs

Rank 1 of a spell wants its band's character level; every rank after that wants one more. A band-1 spell reaches rank 10 at character level 10, and its last rank, 30, at level 30. Ranks cap at 30 - the same ceiling every skill and aura has.

Mana

Mana is the cost at spell level 1. Each level past the first subtracts Adj from it, down to a floor of Min - so a spell's cost falls as it deepens but never reaches zero. Healing and Heal Other add twice the character level to the base cost before that reduction.

The Mana column is not what every class pays. A class discount is applied before the Min floor: the Rogue, Monk and Bard pay a quarter less, and the Sorcerer pays half. Firebolt also halves its own Adj, so it cheapens more slowly than the table suggests.
