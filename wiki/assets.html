Art assets

Every PNG that ships in Packaging/resources/assets, measured on disk. These are the fork's own sprites - the vanilla art lives inside the MPQ archives in CEL/CLX form and is not listed here.

How art gets into the game

KindFormatPipeline
HUD, panels, icon stripsPNG, 32-bit Loaded directly and quantized onto the shared palette at runtime (oracool/hud_art.cpp)
Item iconsCEL Cut from a composite sheet by tools/ItemIconCel.cs, packed into the MPQ
Player spritesCLX Imported from PNG frames by the sprite importer
Objects (chests, monuments)CEL Cut by tools/ReliquaryCel.cs and friends
Palette rule. Anything quantized at runtime must land on palette indices 128-255. The lower half changes per dungeon level, so a sprite using it would shift colour when you take the stairs.

Icon strip conventions

  • A strip is one row of square cells; the cell size is the strip's height, so a recut at a new size needs no code change.
  • Index 0 is the transparent key.
  • Class-tree strips are cut at 56px; the LMB/RMB wells scale them into their 46x46 net opening.

The sprites

Every shipped PNG, drawn at its own size on a dark ground. Icon strips are one row of square cells, so a strip 448 pixels wide and 56 tall is eight 56px icons.
