Areas and levels

Twenty-four floors on sixteen rungs, four difficulties, one continuous ladder from 1 to 64. The area level is the number that decides what a place can drop and how good the rolls may be.

The ladder

area level = rung + 16 x difficulty

Six areas of four floors each, but only sixteen rungs: the Nest is as deep as the Caves and the Crypt is as deep as Hell. They are alternative routes at the same depth rather than a deeper descent, so they share those rungs instead of extending the ladder.

Hell/Hell is the threshold. Area level 48 - the bottom of Hell on Hell difficulty, and of the Crypt beside it - is the rung by which everything in the game is droppable: the last base item, the deepest spell book and Zod, the final rune. Torment exists for better rolls and deeper tiers, not for new things.

What the area level drives

  • Monster loot level - the area level, +3 for a unique, +2 for a champion.
  • Chests, floor spawns and quest rewards - the area level directly.
  • Gold piles - the amount scales with it.
  • Vendor stock - separately: the vendor's own level, lifted by the difficulty block.

Two things it fixed

The old floor number ignored difficulty entirely, so a chest in Torment held the same grade of loot as a chest in Normal. And the Nest and Crypt, being floors 17-24, used to rank as the deepest places in the game - which they were never designed to be. Hellfire treats them as a parallel path rather than a deeper one, and their monsters are pitched that way. They now sit on the Caves' and Hell's rungs, which is what the ladder always meant them to be worth.

Quest lairs

The Skeleton King's lair, the Bone Chamber, the Poisoned Water Supply and the Vile Betrayer's chamber take the level of the quest that owns them, run through the same ladder - so a lair is as deep as the floor it hangs off, on every difficulty. A named-encounter arena carries its own floor instead. Only a genuinely unknown set-level falls back to floor 1, the least rewarding answer on the ladder.

Town

Town has an area level like anywhere else, but nothing drops there, so the automap does not print one. Shops use their own level, described in Base tiers.
